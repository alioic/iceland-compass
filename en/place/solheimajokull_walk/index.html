Sólheimajökull glacier edge

An easy walk to the snout of Sólheimajökull and the lagoon in front of it.

A level path leads from the car park along the glacial lagoon to the snout of Sólheimajökull, an outlet of Mýrdalsjökull with ash-streaked blue ice and deep crevasses. The walk itself is short and easy, suitable for the whole family. Walking onto the glacier itself requires a guide and crampons.
