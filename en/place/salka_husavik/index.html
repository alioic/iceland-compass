Veitingahúsið Salka

A family-run restaurant in one of the oldest buildings in Húsavík.

Salka is located in the center of Húsavík in one of the town's oldest buildings. The menu ranges from fresh seafood and Icelandic lamb to pizzas, steaks and lighter fare, cooked from scratch.

About

Salka is a family-run restaurant in central Húsavík, set in one of the town's oldest houses. The menu spans fresh seafood and Icelandic lamb through to pizza, steaks and lighter dishes, all cooked from scratch. A varied offering in a historic setting makes it an easy choice for the whole family.

Good to know

Located in central Húsavík, within walking distance of the harbour and the Whale Museum. The broad menu suits a group with differing tastes. Popular in summer, so it is worth considering a booking at busy times.
