Kristjánsbakarí

A long-established Akureyri bakery with freshly baked bread and pastries.

Kristjánsbakarí is a well-known bakery in North Iceland recognized for its freshly baked bread, kleinur (twisted doughnuts), cinnamon rolls and strong coffee. It makes an ideal stop for breakfast or an afternoon treat.

About

Kristjánsbakarí is a long-established bakery in Akureyri, known for freshly baked bread, kleinur, cinnamon buns and strong coffee. A perfect stop for breakfast or an afternoon pick-me-up. A classic Icelandic bakery where you can grab fresh pastries to go or sit down with a cup of coffee while exploring the town.

Good to know

Located in Akureyri and well suited to a quick, budget-friendly bite on the road. It is worth stopping by early in the day when the fresh selection is at its best. A handy place to pick up bread and pastries as provisions for the day.
