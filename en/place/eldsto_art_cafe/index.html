Eldstó Art Café

A café and ceramics gallery where food is served on handmade pottery.

Eldstó Art Café is a family-run café and restaurant in Hvolsvöllur that also houses a ceramics gallery. It serves Icelandic dishes such as lamb and fish stew, presented in pottery made on the premises.

About

Eldstó Art Café is a family-run café and restaurant in Hvolsvöllur that also houses a ceramics gallery. What sets it apart is that dishes are served in handmade pottery crafted on site. The menu features Icelandic dishes such as meat soup and fish. It is a pleasant blend of dining and craft.

Good to know

Eldstó Art Café is in Hvolsvöllur in South Iceland, a convenient stop on the way east toward the waterfalls and black-sand beaches. The gallery is part of the visit and the pottery is worth a look. It works well for both a coffee stop and a full meal, and prices are moderate.
