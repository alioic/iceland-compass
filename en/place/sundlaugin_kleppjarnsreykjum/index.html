Sundlaugin Kleppjárnsreykjum

Little country pool in the greenhouse village of Kleppjárnsreykir.

Summer-season outdoor pool in the geothermal village of Kleppjárnsreykir in Borgarfjörður, with a hot tub and children's pool. A pleasant stop on the way through Reykholtsdalur.
