Kolugljúfur

A dramatic gorge with a series of waterfalls, named after the troll-woman Kola.

Kolugljúfur in Víðidalur is a narrow, deep gorge where the river Víðidalsá cascades down in a series of waterfalls. According to legend, the troll-woman Kola lived in the gorge. A bridge and viewing platforms make it easily accessible.

Kolugljúfur – A Dramatic Canyon and Waterfall Series in Víðidalur

Kolugljúfur is a narrow, deep canyon in Víðidalur valley in Northwest Iceland, where the river Víðidalsá plunges down in a majestic series of waterfalls known as Kolufossar. The canyon takes its name from the troll-woman Kola, who according to folklore lived there and caught salmon from the river. This blend of powerful nature and old legend makes Kolugljúfur a unique and memorable destination. Despite lying close to the ring road, the site remains relatively little visited, giving guests the chance to enjoy the waterfalls and canyon in peace and quiet.

Things to See and Do at Kolugljúfur

Kolufossar are the main draw, where the river tumbles in several steps into the canyon to form a powerful and photogenic whole. A bridge over the river and viewing platforms make it easy and safe to admire the falls from different angles. The canyon itself is spectacular, with vertical rock walls and a deep gorge. The site is ideal for photography and short scenic walks. Visitors can also recall the legend of the troll-woman Kola, which adds a delightful dimension to the visit and ties the landscape to Iceland's old storytelling tradition.

Where Is Kolugljúfur and How to Get There

Kolugljúfur lies in Víðidalur valley in Northwest Iceland, just off the ring road. From Route 1 you turn onto the Víðidalur road and follow signs to the car park by the canyon, from where it is a short walk to the bridge and viewing platforms. The route is easy and makes an excellent short stop on a journey between Reykjavík and North Iceland. The nearest town with accommodation and services is Hvammstangi. Thanks to its proximity to the ring road, Kolugljúfur is perfect for travellers who want to experience dramatic scenery without a long detour.

Best Time to Visit Kolugljúfur and Practical Tips

Kolugljúfur is accessible year-round, but summer from May to September is the most comfortable, when roads are clear and trails are dry. In summer the river Víðidalsá often carries a strong flow, making the waterfalls even more impressive. In winter the area can be icy and snow-covered, so take care near the canyon edges. Always stay safe by the clifftops, as they are unfenced and the rock can be loose. Bring good shoes and warm clothing, and allow yourself time to enjoy both the waterfalls and the tranquillity of Víðidalur.
