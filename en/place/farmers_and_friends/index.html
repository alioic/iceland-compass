Farmers & Friends (Farmers Market)

Icelandic design brand with a flagship store in Grandi.

Farmers Market is an Icelandic design company founded in 2005 that blends modern design with Icelandic heritage. Its Farmers & Friends flagship store in Grandi sells clothing in natural fabrics, wool sweaters, music, books and household items.

Icelandic design with roots

Farmers Market was founded in 2005 by designer Bergthóra Gudnadóttir and musician Jóel Pálsson and has since carved out a place in Icelandic design. The brand blends modern design with classic Icelandic heritage, emphasising natural fabrics, careful craftsmanship and respect for the environment. For travellers who want to buy Icelandic design straight from the maker, the flagship store in Grandi is an ideal place to find quality garments with a strong character and a distinctly Nordic sensibility.

Grandi flagship store

Farmers Market's headquarters at Hólmaslóð 2 house the design studio, warehouse and the Farmers & Friends flagship store in the lively Grandi district. The store sells the brand's full collection alongside Icelandic music, books, bags, boots and household items. The neighbourhood itself is rapidly developing with cafés, breweries and design shops, so a visit pairs well with other stops. It is a place for anyone who wants to shop local goods designed and made with sustainability in mind.

Wool and natural fabrics

Farmers Market focuses on natural fabrics and Icelandic wool in clothing for both women and men, including classic lopapeysa sweaters in modern cuts. The design combines the warmth and utility of Icelandic wool with clean lines and understated colours. For travellers who want something beautiful and long-lasting to take home, it is a good way to support an Icelandic design company and own a garment that endures and carries a story of its origin.
