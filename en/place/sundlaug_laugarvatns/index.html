Laugarvatn Swimming Pool

Laugarvatn's town pool, separate from the Fontana baths.

The Laugarvatn sports-centre pool is a 25-metre pool with three hot tubs, a cold pool and a sauna. It is the village's municipal pool, not to be confused with the lakeside Fontana spa.
