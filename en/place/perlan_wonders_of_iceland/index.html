Perlan – Wonders of Iceland

Interactive nature exhibition, a man-made ice cave and an observation deck on Öskjuhlíð hill.

Perlan on Öskjuhlíð hill houses the Wonders of Iceland exhibition, where interactive displays explore glaciers, volcanoes, caves and seabird cliffs. Highlights include a man-made ice cave, a volcano show and a planetarium with a northern lights film. The rooftop observation deck offers 360-degree views over Reykjavík.

The wonders of Icelandic nature under one roof

Perlan on Öskjuhlíð hill is one of Reykjavík's most popular attractions and ideal for families who want to explore Icelandic nature in an interactive way. The Wonders of Iceland exhibition covers glaciers, volcanoes, caves and birdlife using multimedia and real samples. The highlight is a man-made ice cave built from genuine snow and ice, where visitors walk into a cold tunnel and experience the world of glaciers without travelling far outside the city.

Volcanoes, northern lights and views

Perlan also features a volcano show that conveys the power of the country's eruptions, and a planetarium where the northern lights dance above guests in comfortable seats. At the top of the building an observation deck offers 360-degree views over Reykjavík, the mountains and the sea. This variety lets you combine learning, spectacle and sightseeing in one place, whatever the weather outside.

Good to know before you visit

Allow at least three hours to move through all of Perlan's exhibits at a relaxed pace. The building stands on Öskjuhlíð hill close to the city centre, with parking and easy access. On site there is also a restaurant and a café with a view. Perlan is indoors, making it a dependable choice on rainy days and in winter, and a reliable option for families exploring the capital region.
