Ljómalind Local Market

A Borgarnes local market with food and crafts straight from locals.

Ljómalind in Borgarnes is a local market selling food straight from the farm and crafts by locals. Around seventy producers offer their goods: skyr, eggs, dried fish, smoked salmon, jams, chocolate, ice cream, knitwear and wool goods, all from West Iceland.

Farm-direct in Borgarnes

Ljómalind is a lively local market in Borgarnes that emphasises food straight from the farm and crafts made by locals. Around seventy producers sell their goods at the market, from skyr made at a local creamery and fresh farm eggs to dried fish, grass-fed beef and smoked salmon. The market was founded by a group of women in 2013 and is an ideal place for travellers in West Iceland to buy genuine local goods straight from the people who make them, in the heart of Borgarnes.

A unique selection

At Ljómalind you find sugar-free chocolate, skyr confections, jams, ice cream and all sorts of crafts and wool goods, including knitwear from locals. The products are seasonal and few of each, so the selection changes from week to week and many producers sell only at Ljómalind. That makes many of the items unique to West Iceland and impossible to find elsewhere. For travellers who want to support small producers and find a special gift or picnic, it is a perfect stop on the way west.

Open all year

Ljómalind is located beside the tourist information office at Brúartorg 4 in Borgarnes and is open every day year-round, from 11 to 18 in summer and 13 to 18 in winter. The market is a cooperative where a group of women volunteer and produce goods, while other locals sell food or crafts on commission. This makes Ljómalind a warm and personal place where travellers meet the real food culture and craftsmanship of West Iceland straight from the source.
