Sauðárkróksbakarí

A long-established bakery on the main street of Sauðárkrókur's old town.

Sauðárkróksbakarí is among the oldest bakeries in Iceland, sitting on Aðalgata in the old part of Sauðárkrókur. It serves a wide selection of breads and traditional pastries alongside hot and cold sandwiches and soup, with seating indoors and out.

About

Sauðárkróksbakarí is one of Iceland's oldest bakeries, standing on Aðalgata in the old town of Sauðárkrókur. It offers a varied selection of breads and pastries along with hot and cold sandwiches and soup. It's a long-established, cosy place with seating indoors and out, ideal for morning coffee or a light lunch.

Good to know

The bakery is central on the main street in the old town of Sauðárkrókur, in Skagafjörður. It's a handy stop for travellers in Northwest Iceland after fresh bread and pastries. It's a pleasant place to sit down with a cup of coffee.
