Icelandic Lava Show

A live show where real molten lava is poured in front of the audience in Vík.

The Icelandic Lava Show in Vík is a unique experience where rock is melted to over a thousand degrees and glowing lava is poured in front of the audience. Guests feel the heat, see the colours and hear the sounds of flowing lava in a safe, indoor setting. Knowledgeable hosts explain Iceland's volcanism, making the show both thrilling and educational for the whole family.

Living lava right in front of you

The Icelandic Lava Show in Vík offers an experience found nowhere else, pouring real molten lava in front of the audience. Guests feel the heat, see the colours and hear the sounds of glowing lava in a safe indoor setting. This makes Iceland's volcanism tangible in a way that photos and text cannot capture. The show suits both children and adults and is a great addition to a south-coast trip, where volcanoes and lava are never far away.

Education and excitement combined

What makes the lava show special is its blend of excitement and learning. Hosts explain how lava forms, how it behaves and how eruptions have shaped Icelandic society. As the glowing lava flows, guests sense the forces of nature safely up close. This is especially powerful for children, who connect the learning to a real experience. The show is short but memorable and gives a deeper understanding of Iceland, a true land of fire.

Visiting Vík

The Icelandic Lava Show is located in Vík í Mýrdal on the south coast and is open year-round. Being indoors, it works well regardless of the weather and complements sightseeing in the area. Seating is limited, so booking ahead is recommended, especially during peak season. A visit fits neatly into a south-coast journey alongside the black sand beaches, waterfalls and the volcanic scenery of the Katla region.
