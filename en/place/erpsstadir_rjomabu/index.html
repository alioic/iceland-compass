Erpsstaðir Creamery

A family-run dairy farm in the Dalir region with homemade ice cream, skyr and a petting zoo.

Erpsstaðir Creamery is a family-run dairy farm in the Dalir region of West Iceland, known for its homemade ice cream, skyr and cheeses. Visitors can taste and buy dairy products straight from the farm, meet the animals in a small petting zoo and let children enjoy the play area. It sits on Route 60 near Búðardalur and is a popular stop.

Homemade ice cream straight from the farm

Erpsstaðir Creamery is a family-run dairy farm in the Dalir region of West Iceland, best known for its homemade ice cream. Alongside the ice cream, the farm produces Icelandic skyr, cheeses and various other dairy products made on site, which visitors can taste and buy directly from the producer. This gives guests a unique chance to learn about Icelandic dairy production and enjoy fresh farm products in a cosy rural setting on the way through West Iceland.

Farm animals and a play area for children

Erpsstaðir is not only for food lovers, as the farm also has a small petting zoo where children can get close to farm animals, along with a play area and outdoor space to run around. This makes the creamery an enjoyable stop for families who want to break up their journey, let the children move about and enjoy homemade treats at the same time. The visit combines learning, animals and tasty products in one place.

Opening hours and location

Erpsstaðir Creamery is on Route 60 near Búðardalur, on the road between the Ring Road and the Westfjords. The farm shop is generally open daily in summer, while outside the peak season it is best to get in touch before visiting, as this is a working farm. It is worth checking the farm's website for the latest opening hours. Erpsstaðir is an ideal rest stop for those travelling through the Dalir region who want to taste Icelandic farm food.
