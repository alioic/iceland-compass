Tærgesen

A restaurant and guesthouse in a historic wooden house by the harbour in Reyðarfjörður.

Tærgesen is a restaurant and guesthouse in an old wooden building in the heart of Reyðarfjörður, near the harbour. The menu features traditional Icelandic dishes, grill specialities, hamburgers and pizzas.

About

Tærgesen is a restaurant and guesthouse in an old, historic timber house in the heart of Reyðarfjörður. The menu features traditional Icelandic dishes, grills, burgers and pizzas. It's a relaxed, varied spot that suits both families and travellers, with the character of the historic building at its core.

Good to know

The place is a short distance from the harbour in Reyðarfjörður, one of the East Fjords. With a guesthouse on site, it's easy to combine a meal and a night's stay. It makes a convenient stop while touring the fjords.
