Jaðarsbakkalaug

Akranes town pool right by Langisandur beach, with hot tubs and a slide.

Outdoor pool at Jaðarsbakkar next to Langisandur beach, with a 25 m pool, hot tubs, steam bath, water slide and children's pool. Pairs perfectly with a sea dip and the Guðlaug baths.
