Heimabakarí

A family-run bakery and café a short walk from the Húsavík harbour.

Heimabakarí is a beloved bakery and café in Húsavík located close to the harbour. It is known for traditional Icelandic pastries and its cinnamon rolls, and takes pride in using locally sourced ingredients.

About

Heimabakarí is a beloved family-run bakery and café in Húsavík, a short distance from the harbour. It is known for traditional Icelandic pastries and its cinnamon buns, with an emphasis on local ingredients. A cosy spot to grab something freshly baked to go or to sit down with a coffee before heading out whale watching or exploring the town.

Good to know

Located a short distance from Húsavík's harbour, within walking distance of the whale-watching operators and the Whale Museum. A perfect stop for breakfast or a quick bite. It is worth stopping by early in the day when the fresh selection is at its best.
