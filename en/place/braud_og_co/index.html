Brauð & Co

Popular artisan bakery known for sourdough bread and cinnamon rolls.

Brauð & Co is one of the best-known bakeries in the capital area, with several branches. It is celebrated for sourdough loaves, cinnamon rolls and other freshly baked treats made in full view of customers.

About

Brauð & Co is one of the best-known bakeries in the capital area, celebrated for its sourdough bread and cinnamon buns. The baking happens in full view, so you can watch the craft as it comes together. The cinnamon buns are famous and often sell out, and everything is freshly baked. Its colourful building on Frakkastígur has become a local landmark.

Good to know

The bakery has several branches around the capital area, including the eye-catching colourful house on Frakkastígur downtown. It is mainly a grab-and-go bakery with limited seating. Arrive early, as the most popular buns sell out quickly on busy days.
