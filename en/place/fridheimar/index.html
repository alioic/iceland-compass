Friðheimar

Dining inside a tomato greenhouse on the Golden Circle.

Friðheimar is a horticultural farm where guests dine among tomato plants in a lit greenhouse. Everything revolves around the tomato — endless tomato soup with home-baked bread, tomato bloody marys and even tomato ice cream. There are also horse shows.

About

Friðheimar is a working greenhouse farm on the Golden Circle where guests dine among the tomato plants in a bright, glowing greenhouse. Everything revolves around the tomato: bottomless tomato soup with fresh-baked bread, tomato bloody marys and even tomato ice cream. It is a genuinely unique, family-friendly experience, and the farm also puts on popular Icelandic horse shows.

Good to know

Friðheimar sits in Reykholt in Bláskógabyggð, conveniently placed along the Golden Circle near Geysir. It is extremely popular, so table reservations are all but essential, particularly around lunchtime. Prices are on the higher side, but the greenhouse setting is very much part of what you are paying for.
