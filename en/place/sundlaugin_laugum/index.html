Laugar Swimming Pool

A historic school pool with hot tubs in Reykjadalur valley, between Goðafoss and Mývatn.

A 25-metre pool with two spacious hot tubs and a children's pool at the Laugar boarding-school campus in Reykjadalur. One of Iceland's oldest covered pools and a handy stop between Goðafoss and Lake Mývatn.
