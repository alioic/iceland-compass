Brennisteinsalda

The most colourful mountain at Landmannalaugar, laced with steaming sulphur vents.

Brennisteinsalda, the “Sulphur Wave” (855 m), takes its name from the yellow sulphur that streaks its slopes alongside red, green and black mineral layers. The hike follows the first stretch of the Laugavegur trail from Landmannalaugar up to the summit, where steam rises from the ground. It lies in an F-road highland area passable only during the summer months.
