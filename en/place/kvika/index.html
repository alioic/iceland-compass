Kvika Foot Bath

A tiny geothermal foot bath on the shore by Grótta.

Kvika is a small natural foot bath, an artwork by Ólöf Nordal, set on the shoreline on the way out to Grótta on Seltjarnarnes. Passers-by can walk up, sit down and warm their feet in geothermal water while looking out over the sea and mountains.
