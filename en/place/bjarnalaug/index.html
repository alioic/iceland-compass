Bjarnalaug

A historic indoor pool in Akranes from 1944, named after Bjarni Ólafsson.

Bjarnalaug is a 12.5-metre indoor pool in Akranes, designed by Guðjón Samúelsson and opened in 1944. It is mainly used for lessons and infant swimming and is open to the public on winter weekends.

A historic pool from 1944

Bjarnalaug in Akranes opened in 1944 and is among the oldest indoor pools in Iceland. It was designed by the state architect Guðjón Samúelsson and named after the sea captain Bjarni Ólafsson. The pool is 12.5 metres long and the water is warm, around 33-34 degrees, which suits lessons and infant swimming well. Bjarnalaug is mainly used by schools and courses but opens to the public on winter weekends. It is a cosy and historically significant part of Akranes swimming culture.

An indoor pool for cooler days

Bjarnalaug is especially welcome when the weather is chilly, being a warm indoor pool. It sits on Laugabraut in the heart of Akranes, within walking distance of the town's services. While the larger outdoor pool of Akranes, Jaðarsbakkalaug, is open year-round, Bjarnalaug is a good choice for families with young children and anyone who enjoys a calm, warm indoor pool. Note that opening hours are limited to the winter months, so check the schedule before you set out.
