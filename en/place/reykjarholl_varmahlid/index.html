Reykjarhóll

An easy walk up the wooded hill of Reykjarhóll with wide views over Skagafjörður.

Reykjarhóll rises above the village of Varmahlíð, cloaked in woodland with clearly marked trails. A short, rewarding walk leads to the top of the hill, where on a clear day the view opens across most of the Skagafjörður region. It is an ideal outing for families and a shorter leg-stretch.
