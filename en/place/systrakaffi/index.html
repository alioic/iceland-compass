Systrakaffi

A cozy café and restaurant in the heart of Kirkjubæjarklaustur.

Systrakaffi is a family-run spot in Kirkjubæjarklaustur offering a varied menu of pizzas, lamb dishes, soups and homemade cakes. It is a popular stop for travellers along the south coast.

About

Systrakaffi is a cosy, family-run café and restaurant in the heart of Kirkjubæjarklaustur. The menu is varied, with pizzas, lamb, soups and home-baked cakes, so most people find something to their taste. The atmosphere is friendly and relaxed, and it is a popular stopping point for travellers making their way through the southeast.

Good to know

Systrakaffi is centrally located in Kirkjubæjarklaustur, a handy waypoint between Vík and Skaftafell on the south route. It is one of the few restaurants in the area, so it can get lively at peak times. The menu suits families well and prices are moderate.
