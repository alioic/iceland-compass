Humarhöfnin

Langoustine dining in Iceland's langoustine capital at the foot of Vatnajökull.

Humarhöfnin in Höfn specialises in langoustine caught in the bay just offshore. In a cosy timber house the langoustine is served in countless ways — in soup, on pizza and grilled — with views over the glacier tongues of Vatnajökull.

About

Humarhöfnin in Höfn specialises in langoustine (Icelandic "humar") caught in the bay just offshore. Höfn is often called Iceland's langoustine capital, and here the catch appears in every form — in soup, grilled, or on a pizza. Set in a warm timber house, it suits both a celebratory dinner and a relaxed evening meal by the harbour town.

Good to know

The restaurant sits in Höfn at the foot of Vatnajökull, and many tables look out over the glacier's tongues. Höfn is on the southeast corner along the Ring Road, a natural stop near Jökulsárlón glacier lagoon. Booking ahead is wise in the busy summer season.
