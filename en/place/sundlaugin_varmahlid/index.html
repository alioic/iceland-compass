Varmahlíð Swimming Pool

A popular family pool with two water slides in Skagafjörður.

An outdoor pool in Varmahlíð with two water slides and hot tubs, just off the Ring Road in Skagafjörður. A favourite stop for families and for rafters coming off the glacial rivers.
