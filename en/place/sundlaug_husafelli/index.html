Sundlaugin Húsafelli

Geothermal pool in the Húsafell woodland, open year round.

Outdoor pool dating from 1965 in the lovely Húsafell forest setting, with two pools, two hot tubs, a sauna and a water slide. Popular with travellers and summer-house guests.
