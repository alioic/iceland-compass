Skool Beans

A specialty coffee café inside an old yellow school bus in the heart of Vík.

Skool Beans is a small café set inside a converted American school bus where the beans are roasted on site. It serves specialty coffee, tea and hot chocolate in a warm, homey atmosphere.

About

Skool Beans is a small, distinctive specialty coffee shop fitted out inside an old yellow American school bus in the heart of Vík. The beans are roasted on site and the menu covers specialty coffee, tea and hot chocolate. The atmosphere is warm, homely and personal, and it has become a favourite for many travelling along the south coast.

Good to know

Skool Beans is in the village of Vík í Mýrdal, a convenient stop as you head east along the south coast. The space is small, so a queue can form at busy times. Prices are moderate. Ideal for grabbing a proper coffee and warming up before heading on to explore the black-sand beaches.
