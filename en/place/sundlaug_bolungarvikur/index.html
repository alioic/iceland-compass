Sundlaug Bolungarvíkur

A well-equipped pool in the coastal village of Bolungarvík.

Sundlaug Bolungarvíkur is a welcoming, well-appointed pool in the northernmost settlement of the Westfjords. It offers an indoor pool, two hot tubs, a waterslide and a children's pool, plus a sauna, cold tub and relaxation area. A great stop for families exploring the northern Westfjords.
