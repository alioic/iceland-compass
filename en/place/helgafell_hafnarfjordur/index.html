Helgafell (Hafnarfjörður)

A popular tuya above Hafnarfjörður with a short but steep climb to the summit.

Helgafell is a 338 m tuya just above Hafnarfjörður and one of the most popular mountain hikes in the capital region. The trail crosses lava and gravel to the foot of the mountain, then climbs a steep and sometimes loose final stretch to the summit. From the top there are wide views over the capital area, the Reykjanes Peninsula and the surrounding mountains.
