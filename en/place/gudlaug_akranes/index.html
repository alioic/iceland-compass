Guðlaug

A three-tier bathing pool built into the sea wall at Langisandur beach in Akranes.

Opened in 2018 and designed by Basalt Architects, Guðlaug is set into the breakwater above Langisandur beach. Its lowest tier is a shallow wading pool at beach level, the middle a warm soaking pool overlooking the shore, and the top a viewing deck.
