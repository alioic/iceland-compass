Hand-knitted woollens from a small workshop in Ísafjörður.

Hand-knitting in Ísafjörður

Ívaf is a small knitting workshop in Ísafjörður, the largest town in the Westfjords, run by Sigga Sifá since 2016. It makes hand-crafted woollens such as sweaters, hats and scarves, inspired by Icelandic knitting tradition but with a modern twist. The focus is on Icelandic wool, sustainability and local production rather than mass manufacture. For travellers keen to buy local produce and genuine Icelandic craft straight from the maker, Ívaf is an ideal stop in the Westfjords.

The wool and the craft

Ívaf's products are made from Icelandic wool, known for its warmth and durability and well suited to the cool Westfjords climate. The designs draw on traditional patterns while remaining contemporary, so each garment is unique. As this is handwork, production is small in scale and every piece bears the mark of its maker. This makes sweaters and accessories from Ívaf a quality gift and a memorable piece of local produce from the fjord.

Finding the products

Ívaf is one of many creative workshops enlivening the cultural life of Ísafjörður. The products are available straight from the workshop and also in selected shops, including in Ísafjörður and Reykjavík. For those travelling through the Westfjords who want to support a small craft business, it is an ideal way to take a piece of the region home. It is best to follow Ívaf on social media and its website for the latest products and where they are sold.
