Sjávarborg

A restaurant by the harbour in Hvammstangi, above the Seal Center.

Sjávarborg is located by the harbour in Hvammstangi, above the Icelandic Seal Center, and is open all year round. It offers a varied menu of fresh seafood, and guests can sometimes watch whales swim in the sea while they dine.

About

Sjávarborg is a restaurant by the harbour in Hvammstangi, located above the Icelandic Seal Centre. It offers a varied menu of fresh seafood, and you may even spot whales swimming offshore while you dine. It's a pleasant place with a good view over the sea and a warm atmosphere.

Good to know

The restaurant is by the harbour in Hvammstangi on the Vatnsnes peninsula, in Northwest Iceland, and is generally open year-round. Vatnsnes is known for seals and the Hvítserkur sea stack. It pairs well with a visit to the Seal Centre.
