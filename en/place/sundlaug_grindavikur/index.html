Sundlaug Grindavíkur

Grindavík's town pool on Austurvegur, open again after the seismic events.

Outdoor pool at the Grindavík sports centre with a 25 m pool, hot tubs and a water slide. Opening hours can change with conditions in town, so check ahead before visiting.
