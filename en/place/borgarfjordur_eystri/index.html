A remote fjord with a puffin colony and some of the best hiking trails in the country.

Borgarfjörður eystri – puffin colony and hiking paradise in East Iceland

Borgarfjörður eystri is a remote, peaceful fjord in East Iceland centred on the village of Bakkagerði, famous for one of the country's most accessible puffin colonies at Hafnarhólmi. Colourful rhyolite mountains, the jagged Dyrfjöll peaks and the vast Víknaslóðir trail network make this a true nature and hiking paradise. The rocky knoll of Álfaborg and deep-rooted folklore about hidden people lend the area a mystical atmosphere. For travellers seeking untouched scenery, birdwatching and quiet away from the crowds, Borgarfjörður eystri is one of the hidden gems of the East Fjords.

Things to see and do in Borgarfjörður eystri

Hafnarhólmi is the highlight of any visit, where viewing platforms let you get remarkably close to puffins and other seabirds through the summer. The Víknaslóðir trails offer some of Iceland's finest multi-day hikes through deserted bays and colourful mountains, while shorter day walks suit everyone. The rocky outcrop of Álfaborg is tied to legends of elves, and the dramatic Dyrfjöll peaks tower over the area. In Bakkagerði you can visit a small church with an altarpiece by the painter Jóhannes Kjarval and enjoy the calm life of an isolated East Fjord village.

How to get to Borgarfjörður eystri

Borgarfjörður eystri lies at the northern end of the East Fjords, about an hour's drive northeast of Egilsstaðir over a mountain pass with spectacular views. The road has been improved in recent years but can still be demanding in winter. Egilsstaðir, connected to the Ring Road and served by an airport, is the natural starting point. The journey itself is part of the experience, with views over Héraðsflói bay and the surrounding mountains. Many travellers combine the visit with a wider East Iceland tour or a hike to nearby Stórurð beneath the Dyrfjöll.

Best time to visit Borgarfjörður eystri and practical tips

The best time to visit Borgarfjörður eystri is from May to August, when puffins are nesting at Hafnarhólmi and the Víknaslóðir trails are open. Early summer sees Hafnarhólmi teeming with birdlife, though the puffins head out to sea toward the end of August. In winter the mountain road can close in snow, so keep a close watch on conditions. Bring sturdy hiking boots, windproof layers and binoculars for birdwatching. Book accommodation in advance, as services in the village are limited, and allow time for both the birds and the trails.
