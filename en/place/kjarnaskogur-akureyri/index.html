Kjarnaskogur Forest

Iceland's most-visited forest, with playgrounds and trails by Akureyri.

Kjarnaskogur lies just south of Akureyri and is one of Iceland's most popular outdoor recreation areas. The forest has children's playgrounds, walking and cycling trails, barbecue areas and varied facilities for families. It is sheltered and well suited to walks, play and time outdoors in beautiful surroundings.

Iceland's most-visited forest

Kjarnaskogur, just south of Akureyri, is one of Iceland's most visited outdoor areas and a true family paradise. It has many trees, sheltered spots and varied trails suited to both walking and cycling. For children there are three playgrounds along with other activities to keep them busy. It is an ideal place to spend part of a day in fresh air amid beautiful surroundings.

Outdoor time for the whole family

Kjarnaskogur has an illuminated walking trail, barbecue areas, tables and benches that make the area accessible and comfortable for families. You can bring a picnic, grill and let the children run and play in a safe environment. The forest is sheltered, making it a good choice even when the wind blows. It is a free and enjoyable activity near Akureyri.

Trails and playgrounds

Kjarnaskogur offers an extensive network of walking trails suited to all ages, from short easy routes to longer walks. The three playgrounds, including a water play area, make the forest especially exciting for younger visitors. The area is also popular for cycling and outdoor games in summer. Kjarnaskogur is a perfect stop to enjoy nature right beside the town.
