Reykjanes Pool in Ísafjarðardjúp

One of Iceland's longest pools, fed by free-flowing geothermal water.

The 50-metre outdoor pool at Reykjanes in Ísafjarðardjúp was built in 1934 and is heated by naturally flowing geothermal water. It sits beside Hotel Reykjanes and is open to visitors for a small fee.
