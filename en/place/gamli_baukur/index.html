Gamli Baukur

A timber house by the harbour in Húsavík, perfect after whale watching.

Gamli Baukur stands by the harbour in Húsavík, built from driftwood and old timber. The place is known for its fresh fish and fish soup and is ideal for sitting down after whale watching, with views over the boats and Skjálfandi bay.

About

Gamli Baukur stands right on the Húsavík harbour, built from driftwood and old timber. Known for fresh fish and fish soup, it is a natural place to sit down after a whale-watching trip. A warm harbour-side atmosphere with views over the boats and Skjálfandi bay makes it a favourite stop for locals and visitors alike.

Good to know

Set directly on Húsavík's harbour, within walking distance of the whale-watching operators and the Whale Museum. Ideal for a meal after a boat trip out on Skjálfandi. It is popular in summer when the town is busiest with visitors, so arriving early can help.
