SEA LIFE Trust Beluga Whale Sanctuary

A visitor centre in the Westman Islands with an aquarium, rescued puffins and beluga whales in Klettsvík bay.

The SEA LIFE Trust Beluga Whale Sanctuary in the Westman Islands is home to two beluga whales that travelled a long way to a natural bay by the island. The harbour-side visitor centre features an aquarium of local marine life and a puffin rescue, including the well-known Tóti. The exhibition teaches visitors about the whales, the sea life and the puffin colony, and is fun and educational for children.

Puffins and marine life in the islands

The SEA LIFE Trust visitor centre in the Westman Islands is a fun destination for families wanting to discover the islands' sea and bird life. The aquarium shows species that live in the surrounding waters, and the rescue houses puffins that cannot survive in the wild, including the well-known Tóti. This gives children a rare chance to see puffins up close. The centre combines learning and experience and is a great addition to a visit to the puffin colonies and nature of the Westman Islands.

The belugas in Klettsvík

The SEA LIFE Trust sanctuary is known for two beluga whales brought a long way to a natural bay by Heimaey. In the visitor centre, guests learn about the whales' journey, their care and the conservation work behind it. The exhibition explores how animals in human care can gain a life closer to their natural home. For children it is an instructive story about caring for animals and the ocean. The sanctuary is unique worldwide and puts the Westman Islands on the conservation map.

Visiting the centre

The SEA LIFE Trust visitor centre sits by the harbour in the Westman Islands, easy to find on arrival by ferry. The facility is indoors and works well regardless of the windy island weather. Opening hours are seasonal, often from spring into autumn, so it is worth checking before you go. The centre fits well with other island attractions and is especially popular with families wanting to see puffins and marine life up close in a safe setting.
