About

Bryggjukaffi is a small, friendly café in Flateyri, open during the summer months. The menu features soups, freshly baked bagels, coffee and cakes. The atmosphere is cosy and personal, and the café is a welcome addition to the little village for travellers passing through the northern Westfjords.

Good to know

Flateyri is a small village by Önundarfjörður with an interesting history and a beautiful setting between mountains and sea. The café is mainly open during the summer season. It is a good stop for anyone wanting to experience quiet village life and enjoy a light refreshment while touring the Westfjords.
