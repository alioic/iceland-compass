Ölvisholt Brewery

A countryside brewery in a former dairy barn where you can taste the beer straight from the brewers.

Founded in 2007 by two neighbouring farmers, Ölvisholt Brewery sits in an old dairy farm just outside Selfoss. Its taproom opens to visitors on selected days for tastings of craft beers such as the smoky Lava stout and lighter lagers. It is one of Iceland's oldest independent breweries and its beers are stocked across the country.

Craft beer in the countryside

Ölvisholt Brewery occupies a former dairy in Flóahreppur, a short drive from Selfoss. Neighbouring farmers have brewed craft beer here since 2007, making it one of Iceland's oldest independent breweries. On open days you can settle into the taproom, chat with the brewers and taste the range straight from the tanks. It is an ideal stop for travellers who want to buy genuinely local South Iceland produce and support small makers.

Lava and more

Ölvisholt's best-known beer is the smoky Lava stout, dark and full-bodied, but the range also spans light lagers and seasonal experiments. The beers are sold in state liquor stores and restaurants around the country, yet tasting them on site is the real treat. Travelling through South Iceland, you can take a short detour, buy a case to bring home and hear the story behind each brew.

Visiting the brewery

Ölvisholt's taproom opens to guests on selected days, so it is worth checking current hours on the brewery's website before setting out. Its rural setting makes the visit a relaxed pause between Selfoss and the wider South Iceland lowlands. Here you directly support a small family business, learn about Iceland's brewing tradition and take home a product that is truly homegrown in the south.
