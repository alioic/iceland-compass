Vífilsfell

The highest mountain near Reykjavík, with a steep, rocky final scramble to the 655 m summit.

Vífilsfell is a 655 m hyaloclastite ridge just east of Reykjavík, near Sandskeið off the South Iceland road. The route begins with gentle slopes but turns steep and rocky higher up, where the last stretch requires scrambling, in places with fixed ropes. The summit offers one of the finest panoramas in the capital region over mountains, glaciers and the city.
