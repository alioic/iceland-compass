Café Sumarlína

A cozy café and restaurant in an old wooden house in Fáskrúðsfjörður.

Café Sumarlína is a family-run spot in an old wooden building in Fáskrúðsfjörður. The menu includes pizzas, seafood, lamb, fish soup and homemade cakes.

About

Café Sumarlína is a family-run spot in an old timber house in Fáskrúðsfjörður. The menu mixes pizzas, seafood, lamb and fish soup, along with home-baked cakes. It's a relaxed, friendly café and restaurant that suits all ages and makes a popular stop on a journey through the East Fjords.

Good to know

The place is in Fáskrúðsfjörður, a small East Fjords fishing village with a French heritage dating from the era of French fishermen. The village lies just off the Ring Road. It's an easy choice for lunch or dinner while touring the fjords.
