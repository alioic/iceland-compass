Ölverk Pizza & Brewery

A family-run Hveragerði brewery using geothermal energy to brew beer and fire its pizzas.

Ölverk opened in 2017 in Hveragerði and is run by a young local couple. It brews craft beer using geothermal energy from the Hengill area and serves wood-fired pizzas and its own hot sauces. The brewery offers tours explaining Iceland's beer history and the local use of geothermal power.

Geothermally brewed beer

Ölverk in Hveragerði is among the few breweries that use geothermal energy to brew their beer. The town's abundant geothermal heat plays a direct role, and the brewers happily explain it on tours. The range changes regularly, so there is always a reason to drop in and taste something new. For travellers on the Golden Circle it is a fun piece of local produce from the heart of Hveragerði.

Pizzas and sauces

Beyond its beer, Ölverk is known for wood-fired pizzas and its own hot sauces. The place is family and locally run with a relaxed atmosphere, whether guests arrive at lunch or in the evening. Here you can combine a good meal with craft beer in one spot while supporting a small South Iceland business that emphasises local production and sustainable energy.

A visit to Hveragerði

Hveragerði sits on the Golden Circle, just half an hour from Reykjavík, making Ölverk an easy stop along the way. Tours cover Iceland's beer history and the town's use of geothermal power. Check the opening hours on the brewery's website and enjoy buying beer made with energy drawn straight from the ground beneath your feet.
