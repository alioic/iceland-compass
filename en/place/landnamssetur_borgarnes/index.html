The Settlement Center

Exhibitions on the settlement of Iceland and Egil's Saga in the heart of Borgarnes.

The Settlement Center in Borgarnes houses two audio-guided exhibitions, one on the settlement of Iceland and the other on Egil's Saga and the fate of Egill Skallagrímsson. Set in old houses by the sea, it also has a restaurant. The exhibitions tell the stories in a vivid and visual way for all ages.

Two stories in the heart of Borgarnes

The Settlement Center in Borgarnes offers two carefully crafted audio-guided exhibitions. The first covers the settlement of Iceland, telling how Norse settlers claimed the land and built a society in a new place. The second is dedicated to Egil's Saga and the fate of Egill Skallagrímsson, one of the best-known figures of the Icelandic sagas. Set in historic houses by the sea, the centre brings ancient stories vividly to life for visitors of all ages.

Vivid storytelling for families

The Settlement Center's exhibitions are presented in a visual and accessible way, with an audio guide that leads visitors through the story step by step. This makes the complex narratives of the Icelandic sagas easy to follow and engaging for children and adults alike. The centre is an ideal stop for families travelling through West Iceland who want to encounter the country's history in a living way, and the on-site restaurant makes it easy to pause for a meal.

Location and opening hours

The Settlement Center is in Borgarnes, about an hour's drive from Reykjavík and well placed by the main road on a journey through West Iceland. It is open every day and also has a popular restaurant offering, among other things, a buffet. Allow time to go through both exhibitions with the audio guide and to enjoy the historic surroundings by the harbour in the heart of the town.
