Ægir Brugghús

Reykjavík craft brewery with taprooms downtown and in Grandi.

Ægir Brugghús opened in the Grandi district in 2017 and brews craft beer using Icelandic ingredients. The beer is served at the Ægir 101 taproom on Laugavegur and Ægir 220 in Hafnarfjörður, where guests can taste the range straight from the brewer.

Craft beer in Reykjavík

Ægir Brugghús is a Reykjavík craft brewery that opened in the Grandi district in 2017 and has since built a solid reputation for a varied range of beers. The brewery focuses on using Icelandic ingredients where it can and makes everything from light lagers to dark, full-flavoured styles. For travellers who want to taste beer brewed on the spot, Ægir is a good representative of Iceland's craft brewing culture and an easy stop within the capital.

Downtown taprooms

Ægir runs the Ægir 101 taproom at Laugavegur 2, at the end of the rainbow street, offering a wide selection of beer all brewed at their Grandi brewery. Here guests can sit down and taste many styles in one place, straight from the maker. The spot is a convenient addition to a stroll through the city centre and a good way to get to know Icelandic craft beer without straying far from the main tourist routes downtown.

Ægir 220 in Hafnarfjörður

In the old harbour of Hafnarfjörður, Ægir runs the Ægir 220 bar and function room, housed in a beautifully restored stockfish building. The atmosphere is historic and cosy, and the beer is the same as brewed in Reykjavík. For visitors touring the capital region, this is an enjoyable stop that combines the town's history with Icelandic beer culture. Hafnarfjörður is a short drive from Reykjavík and easy to reach for an afternoon out.
