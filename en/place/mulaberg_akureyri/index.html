Múlaberg Bistro & Bar

A bistro and bar at Hotel Kea in the heart of Akureyri.

Múlaberg Bistro & Bar is located on Hafnarstræti in connection with Hotel Kea and has long been known for quality food and professional service. It offers a bistro menu in a relaxed setting.

About

Múlaberg Bistro & Bar is on Hafnarstræti in connection with Hótel Kea in the heart of Akureyri, long known for quality food and good service. It offers a bistro menu in a comfortable setting. A relaxed spot for a good meal or a drink at the bar, whether at lunch or in the evening.

Good to know

Located on Hafnarstræti in central Akureyri, tied to Hótel Kea and so handy for hotel guests and others exploring the town centre. Within walking distance of the pedestrian street and the main landmarks. Booking a table is wise at busy times.
