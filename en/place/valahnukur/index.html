Valahnúkur

A short but steep climb to a panoramic summit in the heart of Þórsmörk.

Valahnúkur (465 m) rises above the huts in Langidalur, and its summit opens up sweeping views of Eyjafjallajökull, Mýrdalsjökull, the Tindfjöll peaks and the whole Þórsmörk valley. The trail is marked and colour-coded on each side, ideal for hikers wanting a big reward in little time. Access is summer-only and requires driving a mountain road with unbridged river crossings.
