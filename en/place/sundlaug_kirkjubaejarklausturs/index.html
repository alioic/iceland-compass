Sundlaug Kirkjubæjarklausturs

A cosy village pool with a view of Systrafoss waterfall from the hot tub.

The Kirkjubæjarklaustur pool sits in the middle of the village as part of the sports centre, with two hot tubs, a slide and a children's pool. A special draw is that bathers can watch the Systrafoss waterfall from the hot tub, making it a fine stop along the southeast route.
