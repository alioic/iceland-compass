Kaffi Kú

A restaurant above a cowshed with views over the cows and Eyjafjörður.

Kaffi Kú is a country restaurant in Eyjafjarðarsveit, set on the upper floor above a modern cowshed where guests can watch the cows and the milking robots. The menu features homemade country food and baked goods - a popular stop just a short distance from Akureyri.

About

Kaffi Kú is a country restaurant in Eyjafjarðarsveit, set on the upper floor above a modern cowshed where guests can watch the cows and milking robots at work. The menu features homemade farm cooking and baked goods. A fun and unexpected stop where rural life and food come together, popular with families and curious travellers alike.

Good to know

Located in Eyjafjarðarsveet valley a short drive from Akureyri, it makes an easy half-day trip out of town. Family-friendly, with the view over the cowshed part of the experience. It is worth checking opening hours before visiting, especially outside the peak season.
