Vík Swimming Pool

The sports-centre pool in Vík, a welcome stop on the south coast.

Vík's pool on Mánabraut is part of the village sports centre, with a roughly 17-metre pool, a hot tub, a sauna and a gym. A cosy stop after exploring the black-sand beaches.
