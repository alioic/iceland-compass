Ósvör Maritime Museum

A reconstructed fishing station from the age of rowing boats.

Ósvör near Bolungarvík is a reconstructed fishing station showing how fishing was carried out in the days of rowing boats. Here you can see the salt house, drying shed, fishermen's hut and a traditional six-oared boat, often with a guide dressed in the skin clothing of bygone fishermen. It offers a vivid insight into the hard lives of Westfjords fishermen.

Ósvör near Bolungarvík

Ósvör is a reconstructed fishing station just outside Bolungarvík that offers a rare insight into the fishing life of the Westfjords in the age of rowing boats. Here stand the salt house, drying shed and fishermen's hut as they were in centuries past, together with a six-oared boat that was operated from the site. A guide often welcomes visitors dressed in the skin clothing of the fishermen and tells of the hard struggle for survival at sea. The site is largely outdoors, and the setting beneath steep mountains by the sea is dramatic. Ósvör is an interesting and educational stop for the whole family.

Opening hours and visiting

Ósvör is open daily through the summer and at other times by arrangement. The fishing station is a very short drive from Bolungarvík and close to Ísafjörður, making it a convenient stop while exploring the area. A visit usually takes about half an hour and suits both children and adults. As the site is largely outdoors, it is wise to dress for the weather. The guide in traditional skin clothing brings the visit to life and makes the history tangible. Ósvör is a great place to understand what life was like at the edge of the sea in earlier times.

Living maritime history

Ósvör is more than an ordinary museum; it is a living reconstruction where visitors step into the world of the old fishermen. Boats, fishing gear and the hut are presented in a hands-on way that delights children and informs adults. The setting, at the foot of Mount Bolafjall and facing the open sea, underlines just how hard the struggle for survival was in these outposts. For families, the visit is an enjoyable way to connect the history, nature and culture of the Westfjords. Ósvör reminds us of the foundation that fishing laid for settlement and life in the Westfjords for centuries.
