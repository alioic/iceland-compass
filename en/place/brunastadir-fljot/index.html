Brúnastaðir Farm

A family farm in Fljót with a farm shop, goat cheese and a petting zoo.

At Brúnastaðir in Fljót the family runs a small farm shop selling goat cheese made on the farm alongside other local produce. There is a petting zoo with Icelandic farm animals and light refreshments such as ice cream, coffee and home baking. The farm sits by Lake Miklavatn in the north of Skagafjörður.

Farm shop at Brúnastaðir

Brúnastaðir in Fljót is a family-run farm in the northern part of Skagafjörður with a focus on open agriculture and local produce. The farm shop sells goat cheese made on site along with other products from the district. The farm stands by Lake Miklavatn and suits travellers on the Tröllaskagi route between Skagafjörður and Siglufjörður. It is a pleasant stop for families and anyone interested in Icelandic farming.

Goat cheese and local produce

At Brúnastaðir, goat cheese is one of the main products and is sold straight from the farmers in the small shop. Alongside the cheese you can often find home baking, ice cream and other refreshments on site. Buying directly from the farm supports local people and lets travellers taste products that rarely reach supermarkets. The visit also offers a glimpse of daily life on an Icelandic farm.

Petting zoo and family stop

Brúnastaðir has a petting zoo with Icelandic farm animals, including goats, pigs, rabbits, hens, lambs and horses, which delights younger guests. Play equipment and open surroundings make it ideal for families travelling through Northwest Iceland. Alongside the zoo, visitors can buy local produce and enjoy refreshments. The location in Fljót is scenic and off the well-worn tourist track.
