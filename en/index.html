    About

    This site is a guide to Iceland — made to help you plan a trip anywhere in the country. Every place is linked to nearby accommodation and activities so you can put together your own journey.

    Iceland's nature is powerful and the weather changes fast. Always check safetravel.is and vedur.is before you set off, especially in the highlands and near volcanic areas.
