KHB Brugghús

Brewery and distillery in one of Borgarfjörður eystri's oldest houses.

KHB Brugghús is a craft brewery and distillery in an old freezing plant in Borgarfjörður eystri. It produces beer, small-batch gin and landi (Icelandic moonshine), with the beers named after the area's elf and ghost stories. Visitors can drop into the taproom, taste the products and book a tour.

Brewing and distilling in Bakkagerði

KHB Brugghús occupies one of the oldest houses in Borgarfjörður eystri, a village of barely ninety people, and combines a brewery and a distillery under one roof. It produces craft beer, small-batch gin and traditional landi, and the products have won international recognition. The beers are brewed in the Czech tradition and named after local elf and ghost stories. For travellers keen to buy local produce, it is a unique place where history, folklore and craft come together.

Products and awards

KHB makes beer, gin and landi, with an oak-aged landi and further products added over time. KHB Gin is blended with hand-picked botanicals, including home-grown lemongrass, and has won medals at international competitions. The web shop and taproom let you buy the products directly from the maker. The emphasis is on small batches and hands-on production, making each variety a genuine piece of local produce from a remote fjord in East Iceland.

Visiting Borgarfjörður eystri

Borgarfjörður eystri is famed for dramatic landscapes, a puffin colony and hiking trails, and KHB Brugghús adds a cultural dimension to a visit. Guests can drop into the taproom, taste the beer, gin and landi, and book a tour to hear the story of the house. As opening hours can vary, it is best to make contact in advance. The brewery is a welcome addition for anyone collecting local produce and keen to support small rural production.
