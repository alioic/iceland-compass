Sundlaug Vestmannaeyja

A lively family pool with waterslides on Heimaey island.

The Westman Islands pool first opened in 1976, with a renewed outdoor area added in 2010 that is considered among the best in Iceland. It has a 25-metre indoor pool, hot tubs and several water slides, including Eldfell, Stórhöfði and Dufþekja, plus a large children's pool with a climbing wall.
