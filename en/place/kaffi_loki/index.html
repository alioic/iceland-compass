Kaffi Loki

Café opposite Hallgrímskirkja focused on Icelandic food.

Kaffi Loki sits across from Hallgrímskirkja and focuses on traditional Icelandic fare. The menu features rye bread, plokkfiskur (fish stew), dried fish and rye-bread ice cream.

About

Kaffi Loki stands opposite Hallgrímskirkja and focuses on traditional Icelandic food. The menu features rye bread, plokkfiskur (fish stew), harðfiskur (dried fish) and the unusual rye-bread ice cream. It is a great place to try authentic Icelandic dishes in a warm setting, with a view of one of the city's main landmarks.

Good to know

It sits at the top of the Skólavörðuholt hill, directly across from Hallgrímskirkja, making it ideal for lunch or coffee after visiting the church. A good choice for anyone wanting to sample Icelandic food in an approachable way. The view of the church is a welcome bonus with your meal.
