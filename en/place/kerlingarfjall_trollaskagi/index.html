Kerlingarfjall (Kerling)

A long, demanding climb to the highest peak of the Tröllaskagi peninsula at 1,538 metres.

Kerling, often called Kerlingarfjall, is the highest peak of the Tröllaskagi peninsula at 1,538 metres, straddling the boundary between Skagafjörður and Eyjafjörður. It is a long, strenuous mountain hike for experienced walkers, often with snow and steep sections near the top, but the summit rewards you with sweeping views over the fjords and the sea. Choose settled weather and carry the right gear.
