Kaffi Duus

Well-established restaurant by the small-boat harbour in Keflavík.

Kaffi Duus is a well-known restaurant by the marina in Keflavík with views over the harbour. It focuses on fish dishes and also houses a café and bar.

About

Kaffi Duus is a long-established, well-known restaurant by the small-boat harbour in Keflavík, with lovely views over the water. The focus is on fish dishes made with fresh seafood, though it also houses a café and bar. The dining room is spacious and the mood relaxed. A popular destination for both locals and travellers on Reykjanes.

Good to know

Kaffi Duus is by the small-boat harbour in Keflavík on Reykjanes, close to Keflavík Airport and so a handy choice for a first or last meal of an Iceland trip. The dining room is roomy and suits groups. Prices are moderate. The harbour view is part of the experience.
