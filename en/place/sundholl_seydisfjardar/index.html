Seyðisfjörður Swimming Hall

A historic 1948 indoor pool in the arts village of Seyðisfjörður.

Seyðisfjörður's swimming hall opened in 1948 and offers a 12.5-metre indoor pool with an outdoor hot tub, cold tub and sauna. A cosy bathing spot in one of the East Fjords' prettiest towns.
