The Motorcycle Museum of Iceland

A museum devoted to the history of motorcycles in Iceland, at Krokeyri in Akureyri.

The Motorcycle Museum of Iceland holds a varied collection of motorcycles spanning their history in the country. Visitors can view old and new bikes alongside objects and photographs that illustrate Icelandic motorcycle culture. The museum is interesting for the whole family, especially those fascinated by vehicles.

For vehicle enthusiasts

The Motorcycle Museum of Iceland in Akureyri is a unique museum tracing the history of motorcycles in the country from the beginning. It displays a varied selection of bikes, from old classics to newer models, along with objects and photographs. The museum strongly appeals to those who love vehicles but is also fun for the whole family. It is an ideal indoor activity when the weather does not favour being outside.

The story of Icelandic motorcycle culture

At the Motorcycle Museum you can learn how motorcycles have accompanied Icelanders over the years and shaped a distinct culture. The exhibition is well arranged and gives good insight into the development of the bikes and the stories of their owners. The staff often share knowledge and create an engaging atmosphere. It is an enjoyable and educational stop in the capital of the north.

A convenient stop in Akureyri

The Motorcycle Museum is located at Krokeyri in Akureyri and makes a convenient stop for travellers in town. The museum is open daily in summer and at weekends in winter, while groups can book visits outside the usual hours. The setting is pleasant and the visit suits all ages. It pairs well with the other museums and activities in Akureyri.
