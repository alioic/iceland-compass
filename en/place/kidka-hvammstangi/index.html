KIDKA Wool Factory

One of Iceland's largest wool factories, with a factory shop in the heart of Hvammstangi.

KIDKA has knitted garments from Icelandic wool for decades and runs a factory shop in the village of Hvammstangi. You can buy lopapeysur, hats, mittens and blankets straight from the maker, all designed and finished on site. The shop sits just off the Ring Road.

Wool factory in Hvammstangi

KIDKA is one of Iceland's largest wool factories and has worked with Icelandic wool for more than forty years. The factory stands in the small village of Hvammstangi in Northwest Iceland, roughly halfway between Reykjavik and Akureyri. In the factory shop you can see how lopapeysur, blankets and other knitwear are made and buy them directly from the producer. It is an ideal stop for travellers seeking genuine Icelandic woollens.

Lopapeysur straight from the maker

The KIDKA shop stocks lopapeysur, hats, mittens, socks and blankets that are designed and finished on site from pure Icelandic wool. Buying directly from the factory often means better value and a wide selection that reflects local craftsmanship. The woollens are warm, durable and well suited to Iceland's changeable weather. For anyone wanting a souvenir with a real connection to the region, a visit to KIDKA is a rewarding choice.

Craft and wool in Northwest Iceland

Húnaþing vestra has a long tradition of wool processing, and KIDKA is one of its leading representatives today. The factory is an important employer in the district and keeps alive the skill of turning Icelandic wool into beautiful garments. Travellers on the Ring Road can easily stop, view the production and support local people at the same time. Hvammstangi also offers the Icelandic Seal Centre and other worthwhile stops nearby.
