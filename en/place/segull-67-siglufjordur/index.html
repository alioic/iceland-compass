Craft brewery in Siglufjörður

Segull 67 is a small family-run brewery in the herring town of Siglufjörður on the Tröllaskagi peninsula. Housed in a renovated herring factory, it blends a maritime atmosphere with a cosy taproom. There you can try the core beer range along with seasonal brews and watch the brewing through a glass wall. The name refers to the compass needle and the family's lucky number, 67, tied to an old truck and boat of their forebears.

Taproom and brewing

In the Segull 67 taproom, guests can sit down, taste the beer and view the brewing equipment through a glass wall. The brewery offers a varied selection, including a pale lager, amber lager and IPA, plus special Christmas, Easter and summer beers. The place is open most days of the week and makes a good stop on a tour of Northeast Iceland. A visit supports a small, independent business in beautiful surroundings.

Distillery and Icelandic ingredients

Upstairs at Segull 67 is the Íshaf 67 distillery, producing spirits such as gin and aquavit from Icelandic ingredients and pure water. The focus is on herbs and materials from the surrounding area, giving the drinks a distinctive character. Together the brewery and distillery form a complete experience under one roof. Siglufjörður itself is a historic herring town with the Herring Era Museum and a scenic harbour that make the visit even more memorable.
