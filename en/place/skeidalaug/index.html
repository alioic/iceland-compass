A renovated country pool on Skeið

Skeiðalaug in Brautarholt opened in 1975 and underwent a thorough renovation in 2023-2024. Today it has a 16.68-metre pool along with two large hot tubs, one with massage jets, an indoor cold tub and an infrared sauna. The pool sits by Route 30 in Skeiða- og Gnúpverjahreppur and makes a handy stop when touring the highland farming districts of Árnessýsla, for instance on the way to Gullfoss and Geysir. The upgrades have turned Skeiðalaug into a modern, comfortable pool for locals and travellers alike.

A swim on the South Iceland farm roads

For anyone travelling the Golden Circle, Skeiðalaug is a welcome place to swim away from the crowds. It is open every day and offers new hot tubs, a sauna and a cold tub that make a visit relaxing after a day trip. Its location on Route 30 makes it a convenient stop between Selfoss and Flúðir. Skeiðalaug is a fine example of a well-executed renovation of a historic country pool and is ideal for families and travellers who want to experience genuine Icelandic swimming culture.
