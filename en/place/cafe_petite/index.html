About

Café Petite is a cosy, welcoming café in Keflavík offering hot drinks, desserts and light bites. The atmosphere is calm and hospitable, perfect for settling in with a coffee and something sweet. It is a small, personal spot in the heart of town that works well for a break from the day's travels.

Good to know

Café Petite is in Keflavík on the Reykjanes peninsula, not far from Keflavík Airport. That makes it a handy stop for anyone arriving or departing the country, or wanting a quiet coffee break on Reykjanes. Prices are moderate. An ideal spot for hot drinks and desserts in a relaxed setting.
