Hellulaug

An unspoiled natural pool by the sea in Vatnsfjörður, near Flókalundur.

Hellulaug is a small natural pool just below Road 60 near Flókalundur, holding around 38°C. It sits at the water's edge with views over Breiðafjörður and is completely free. There are no facilities, so bring everything you need.
