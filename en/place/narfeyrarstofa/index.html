Narfeyrarstofa

Seafood in a beautifully restored timber house in Stykkishólmur.

Narfeyrarstofa occupies a historic timber house by the harbour in Stykkishólmur. The kitchen focuses on fresh fish and shellfish from Breiðafjörður — not least blue mussels and scallops — in a warm, old-fashioned setting across two floors.

About

Narfeyrarstofa is a seafood restaurant set in a beautifully restored, historic timber house by the harbour in Stykkishólmur. The kitchen builds on fresh fish and shellfish from Breiðafjörður, especially blue mussels and scallops. Spread over two warm, old-fashioned floors, it is known for carefully prepared dishes in a calm, welcoming setting, making it a strong choice for dinner on the Snæfellsnes peninsula.

Good to know

The restaurant sits in the heart of Stykkishólmur, just steps from the old harbour, and many tables look out over it. It pairs well with a stroll through the old town or a boat trip among the islands of Breiðafjörður. On busy summer evenings it is wise to book a table ahead.
