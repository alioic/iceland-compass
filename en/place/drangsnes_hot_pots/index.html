Drangsnes Hot Pots

Three seaside hot tubs right in the middle of the village of Drangsnes.

The Drangsnes hot pots are three tubs, each a different temperature, set on the shoreline right beside the road. They appeared after hot water was struck during drilling in 1997. Changing rooms sit across the street, access is free, and the view stretches across Steingrímsfjörður toward Grímsey island.
