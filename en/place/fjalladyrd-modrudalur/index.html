Fjalladýrð, Möðrudalur

Iceland's highest farm, with a country shop, handcraft and a café.

Möðrudalur is the highest inhabited farm in Iceland, where the family runs the Fjalladýrð travel service. The shop sells Icelandic woollens and handcraft made by locals along with essentials for travellers. At the Fjallakaffi café you will find homemade treats such as kleinur, ástarpungar and meat soup.

Iceland's highest inhabited farm

Möðrudalur is the highest inhabited farm in Iceland, at about 469 metres above sea level, on the highland edge of Northeast Iceland. There the family runs the Fjalladýrð travel service, combining accommodation, dining and a shop. The place is a historic stop on the route between Mývatn and Egilsstaðir and holds a special place in travellers' hearts. The surroundings are dramatic and give the visit a unique feel.

Handcraft and woollens

The shop at Möðrudalur sells Icelandic woollens and handcraft made by locals, along with general essentials for travellers. This lets guests buy souvenirs with a genuine connection to the area and support a small rural service on the highland edge. The shop is part of a complete experience where farming, history and hospitality come together. For many, a stop at Möðrudalur is a fixture on the northeast route.

Fjallakaffi and homemade treats

The Fjallakaffi café offers homemade treats such as kleinur, ástarpungar and the celebrated Icelandic meat soup. The menu emphasises local ingredients, including trout from highland lakes and lamb straight from the farm. In the calm and distinctive setting of Möðrudalur it is pleasant to pause, have a bite and recharge. The place suits both those stopping briefly and those staying overnight.
