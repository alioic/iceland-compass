Reyðarfjörður Swimming Pool

Fjarðabyggð's town pool in Reyðarfjörður with good family facilities.

Reyðarfjörður's pool is part of the town sports centre, with an outdoor pool, hot tubs and facilities for children. A pleasant stop in one of Fjarðabyggð's fjords.
