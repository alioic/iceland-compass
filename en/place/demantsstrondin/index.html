Diamond Beach

Black-sand beach where ice chunks wash ashore and glisten like diamonds.

Diamond Beach, known in Icelandic as Fellsfjara on Breiðamerkursandur, is a jet-black sand beach just across Route 1 from Jökulsárlón. Chunks of ice drift out of the lagoon into the sea and wash back onto the shore, where they sparkle like scattered diamonds on the black sand. It is one of Iceland's most famous photography spots, especially in the low golden light of sunrise and sunset. Diamond Beach Iceland (Fellsfjara): ice that glistens like diamonds on black sand

Diamond Beach Iceland, known in Icelandic as Fellsfjara on Breiðamerkursandur, is a jet-black sand beach just across Route 1 from Jökulsárlón glacier lagoon. Icebergs that calve from Breiðamerkurjökull drift through the lagoon and out to sea, and the waves wash the ice chunks back onto the shore. There they rest, clear and blue, glistening like scattered diamonds on the black sand, which gave the beach its famous name. It is one of South Iceland's most beautiful and most photographed spots, where the contrast of black sand, white ice and ocean creates an unforgettable scene year-round.

Photography and the best time to visit Diamond Beach

Diamond Beach is a photographer's dream. Clear ice chunks on jet-black sand create striking contrasts that shine best in low, soft light. The finest light usually comes at sunrise and sunset, and in winter the blue hour and even the northern lights can add to the magic. The amount and position of the ice changes constantly with the tides, wind and season, so every visit is unique. Bring sturdy clothing against wind and spray, and allow plenty of time to walk the shore and find the most beautiful pieces of ice along the water's edge.

Safety at Diamond Beach: beware of sneaker waves

Although Diamond Beach looks peaceful, it can be dangerous. Sneaker waves can surge suddenly and wash far up the sand without warning, and the ocean is freezing cold with strong currents. Always keep a safe distance from the sea, never turn your back on the waves, and never climb onto ice near the water, as it can roll or be swept out. Keep a close eye on children. With proper caution it is perfectly safe to enjoy this remarkable place, but respect the power of the ocean and stay higher up on the dry sand beyond the reach of the waves.

How to get to Diamond Beach

Diamond Beach lies in the southeast of South Iceland, on the Ring Road (Route 1) directly opposite Jökulsárlón, between Skaftafell and the town of Höfn í Hornafirði. From Reykjavík it is around a five-hour drive east along the South Coast. A car park sits on the south side of the bridge over the river Jökulsá, just steps from the beach, with only a short walk down onto the black sand. Most visitors combine Diamond Beach and Jökulsárlón in a single stop, as the two sit side by side, and many organised day tours from Reykjavík visit both.
