Kaffi Kú

A café in the loft above a cowshed selling farm-direct beef.

Kaffi Kú sits at Garður farm in Eyjafjarðarsveit, about 10 km south of Akureyri, in the loft above a robotic dairy barn. Guests can watch the cows being milked while enjoying waffles and cakes with cream from the farm. On site you can also buy beef straight from the farm, fresh or frozen.

A café above a cowshed in Eyjafjörður

Kaffi Kú is a distinctive café at Garður farm in Eyjafjarðarsveit, only about 10 kilometres south of Akureyri. The café is in the loft above a robotic dairy barn, and guests can watch the cows through a glass wall while enjoying refreshments. The place opened in 2011 and is run by locals. It is a cosy and unusual experience for the whole family in Northeast Iceland.

Refreshments and cream from the farm

At Kaffi Kú, waffles, cakes and hot chocolate take centre stage, served with cream straight from the farm. The atmosphere is homely and the view over the barn gives the visit a special feel. Guests are also welcome to enter the cowshed, pet the calves and watch the milking robot at work. The blend of refreshments and farming makes the place memorable.

Farm-direct beef

Besides the café, the family at Garður sells beef straight from the farm, including mince, burgers, tenderloin and more, fresh or frozen. This lets guests take home produce directly from the producer and support local people. Kaffi Kú is a short distance from Akureyri and so an easy detour for those staying in town. The place combines refreshments, experience and local produce in one spot.
