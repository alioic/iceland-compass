LAVA Centre

An interactive exhibition about Iceland's volcanoes, earthquakes and geology in the heart of Hvolsvöllur.

LAVA Centre is a high-tech, interactive exhibition in Hvolsvöllur that explores volcanic activity, earthquakes and the making of the Icelandic landscape. Visitors move through halls filled with animations, soundscapes and models that bring magma, volcanic zones and tectonic plates to life. Suitable for all ages, it offers great context before heading out into South Iceland's volcanic scenery.

Iceland's volcanism in one place

LAVA Centre in Hvolsvöllur is one of the country's finest volcano exhibitions and a great fit for families touring South Iceland. It uses animations, sound and interactive models to explain how magma, volcanic zones and plate boundaries shape Iceland. Children and adults experience the forces of nature safely indoors and gain a clear understanding of the volcanism that defines the region. Open year-round, it is ideal for learning before heading to nearby volcanic systems and lava fields.

An interactive experience for all ages

What sets LAVA Centre apart is how vivid the exhibition feels. Rather than reading long panels, visitors walk through halls where the ground seems to tremble, magma flows and volcanoes come to life. This makes complex geology accessible to children and curious adults alike. The exhibition highlights famous Icelandic eruptions and how they have shaped both landscape and society. For families it is a fun and educational way to grasp this land of fire.

Visiting Hvolsvöllur

LAVA Centre is centrally located in Hvolsvöllur along Route 1, making it easy to visit while travelling the south coast. The exhibition is open daily throughout the year, a reliable stop regardless of weather or season. There is also a restaurant and shop on site. A visit works well as the start of a journey through the volcanic area between Hekla, Eyjafjallajökull and Katla. It is worth allowing plenty of time to enjoy every hall fully.
