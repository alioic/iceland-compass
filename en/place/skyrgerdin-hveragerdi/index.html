Skyrgerðin (The Old Skyr Factory)

The old skyr factory in Hveragerði, where skyr is still made on site the traditional way.

Skyrgerðin occupies a 1930 building in Hveragerði that once housed a skyr factory. Skyr is still made on site the traditional way and served with light dishes, cakes and coffee. The house has long been a gathering place for locals and travellers in the heart of town.

Skyr made on site

Skyrgerðin in Hveragerði keeps an old tradition alive by making skyr on the premises the way it was done long ago. The building was raised in 1930 as a skyr factory and has since been a heart of the town for both locals and travellers. Here you can taste fresh skyr at its best and get to know one of Iceland's oldest dairy traditions. It is genuine local produce in a beautiful historic setting.

Café and light dishes

Besides skyr, Skyrgerðin runs a café with light dishes, soups, sandwiches, cakes and sweets. The atmosphere is warm and the spot is well suited to a pause on a Golden Circle trip. Guests can enjoy a meal, buy products to take away and see how an old dairy tradition thrives in Hveragerði. For anyone interested in Icelandic food it is an essential stop.

History in every corner

The building that houses Skyrgerðin has a long history, from a skyr factory on the ground floor to a hotel and restaurant on the upper floors. Today the place unites that history and living food craft under one roof. Its location in central Hveragerði makes it a convenient stop between Reykjavík and South Iceland. Here you support local production and take home a taste of Icelandic tradition.
