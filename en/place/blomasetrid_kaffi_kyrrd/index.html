Blómasetrið - Kaffi Kyrrð

A calm, warm café in the old town of Borgarnes.

Blómasetrið - Kaffi Kyrrð is a café, gift shop and guesthouse on Skúlagata in the old town of Borgarnes, beside Englendingavík bay. It serves light meals, cakes and quality coffee in a relaxed atmosphere, including sandwiches, quiche and salads.

About

Blómasetrið - Kaffi Kyrrð is a calm, welcoming café in the old part of Borgarnes, combined with a gift shop and guesthouse. In a relaxed atmosphere it serves light meals, homemade cakes and good coffee, including sandwiches, savoury pies and salads. It is a cosy place to sit down and enjoy some quiet, just as the name Kyrrð suggests.

Good to know

The café is on Skúlagata in the old town, close to Englendingavík, and fits well with a leisurely walk through the oldest part of Borgarnes. The setting is homely and draws both locals and travellers. A good stop for anyone wanting coffee and a bite in quieter surroundings than by the main road.
