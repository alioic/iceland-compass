Smiðjan Brugghús

A brewpub in Vík serving its own craft beer alongside burgers and grilled dishes.

Smiðjan Brugghús is a small brewery and brewpub in the heart of Vík, founded by locals in 2018. It brews craft beer on site, from IPAs to stouts, and serves burgers, ribs and grilled fare. The spot is a popular gathering place for both residents and travellers seeking shelter from the south coast weather.

Beer brewed in Vík

Smiðjan Brugghús occupies an old industrial building in the middle of Vík, where all the beer is brewed on site. The range runs from hoppy IPAs to dark stouts and seasonal experiments, so there is always something new to taste. It is genuine local produce and an ideal place to settle in after a day among the black sands and Reynisfjara beach.

Food with the beer

Alongside its craft beer, Smiðjan serves burgers, ribs, chicken wings and other grilled dishes, with options for vegans too. The place is warm and lively and often becomes a meeting point for locals and travellers at once. For visitors who want to support a small South Iceland business, it is a natural stop in Vík, whether at lunch or in the evening.

A stop in Vík

Vík is one of the most popular destinations on the south coast, and Smiðjan Brugghús has become a fixture of any visit. Here you get beer found nowhere else, brewed by locals in the village itself. Check opening hours on the brewpub's website, as weather and season can affect them, and enjoy supporting hands-on local production while travelling through South Iceland.
