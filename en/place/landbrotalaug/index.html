Landbrotalaug

A tiny natural hot pot hidden in the lava on the Snæfellsnes peninsula.

Landbrotalaug is a small, rustic natural spring on the eastern edge of Snæfellsnes. The classic stone-lined pot fits only two or three people and holds a steady 38°C, with a larger pool alongside. There are no facilities, so it is a soak for those who want raw, authentic nature bathing.
