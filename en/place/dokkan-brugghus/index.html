Dokkan Brugghús

The Westfjords' first and only brewery, in the heart of Ísafjörður.

Dokkan Brugghús is a small family business founded in Ísafjörður in 2017, the first brewery in the Westfjords. The beer is brewed with spring water that rises in the remote Westfjords mountains. Everything is made on site at Sindragata, and the bar is open in the afternoons and evenings in summer.

The Westfjords' first brewery

Dokkan Brugghús was founded in Ísafjörður in 2017 and is the first and only brewery in the Westfjords. The family behind it focuses on craft beer made from pure spring water that rises in the region's mountains and filters through an ancient lava pile. The beer is brewed and sold on site at Sindragata in the heart of town. For travellers keen to buy local produce and taste beer from the region, Dokkan is a natural stop in the Westfjords.

The beer and the water

Much of Dokkan's character comes from the water used in the beer. The spring water rises in the remote Westfjords mountains and is purified as it seeps through a lava pile millions of years old, giving it a low mineral content. From this water the local team brews a range of craft beers. At the bar you can taste the beer straight from the maker in a relaxed setting. Hours are longer in summer, so it is worth checking before a visit.

Ísafjörður as a base

Ísafjörður is the largest town in the Westfjords and a good base for exploring the fjords, Hornstrandir and the surrounding area. Dokkan Brugghús adds to the town's culture and food scene and lets visitors taste local produce straight from the maker. The town has varied accommodation, shops and dining within walking distance. The brewery is a welcome addition for anyone collecting local produce and keen to support small production in the Westfjords.
