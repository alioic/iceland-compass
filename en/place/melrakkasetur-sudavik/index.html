The Arctic Fox Centre

A nature centre about the Icelandic arctic fox in Súðavík.

The Arctic Fox Centre in Súðavík is a nature centre dedicated to Iceland's only native land mammal, the arctic fox. The exhibition covers the animal's life, the history of fox hunting and ongoing research, and orphaned fox cubs are often cared for at the centre over the summer. The Arctic Fox Centre in Súðavík

The Arctic Fox Centre is a nature centre in Súðavík dedicated to the arctic fox, Iceland's only native land mammal. The exhibition explores the animal's biology, its adaptation to harsh weather and the long history of interaction between people and foxes. The centre also carries out research and sometimes cares for orphaned fox cubs that visitors can see during the summer. It is housed in Eyrardalur, the oldest building in Súðavík, which also has a welcoming café. A visit suits families and anyone interested in the nature and wildlife of the Westfjords.

Opening hours and access

The Arctic Fox Centre is open daily through the summer months and welcomes guests of all ages. Súðavík is a short drive from Ísafjörður, making the visit an easy addition to a trip around the northern Westfjords. The exhibition is indoors, so it is a good choice in any weather. There is a café serving hot drinks and waffles on site. Outside the peak season it is sensible to confirm opening in advance. The centre brings together education, history and wildlife in the heart of the old village.

A visit for families

The Arctic Fox Centre is a great destination for families, as the foxes captivate children and adults alike. When cubs are being cared for at the centre, visitors can watch them up close, which is a rare experience. The exhibition is accessible and informative without being complicated, and the café at Eyrardalur is a nice place to relax afterwards. Súðavík is a quiet village where it is pleasant to walk around and enjoy the view over Álftafjörður. It is a visit that combines nature, history and a warm atmosphere.
