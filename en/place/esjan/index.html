The city mountain — the most popular day hike among capital residents.

Esja – Reykjavik's Mountain and Most Popular Day Hike

Mount Esja is Reykjavik's own mountain, rising above the city across Kollafjordur bay and dominating the skyline. It is the most beloved day hike in the greater capital area and accessible to anyone wanting to climb higher amid beautiful surroundings. The trail, about 6.6 km round trip, takes most people 2–4 hours and is rated moderate. Esja is a symbol of Reykjavik's outdoor culture and the perfect blend of proximity to the city with the feel of a genuine mountain ascent.

What to See and Experience on Esja

Most hikers head up to the rock known as Steinn at around 600 metres, where many turn back and enjoy the view. More experienced walkers can scramble on up to Thverfellshorn, where a fixed chain helps on the last and steepest stretch. From the mountain, magnificent views open over Reykjavik, Faxafloi bay, Mosfellsbaer and the surrounding peaks. The route passes streams, moss and rocky bands, and every gain in altitude reveals a new perspective. Esja is ideal for exercise, photography and drinking in the wide-open landscape.

Getting to Esja – Location and Access

Esja lies north of Reykjavik, across Kollafjordur bay, and the trailhead by Esjustofa is only about a 20–25 minute drive from downtown. There is a large car park and Straeto buses serve the area, so the mountain is remarkably accessible without a car. Marked trails lead up the mountain from the car park. Because Esja is so close to the city, it is perfect for a spontaneous outing, but remember that although access is easy, this is a real mountain hike that requires preparation and good footwear.

Best Time to Hike Esja and Practical Tips

Esja is hiked all year, but summer and autumn offer the safest and most comfortable conditions. In winter the upper section can be icy and hazardous; crampons and experience are then essential, and it is often wise to turn back lower down. Dress in layers, bring wind- and waterproof clothing, water and snacks, and check the forecast before setting out. Good hiking footwear makes all the difference. Esja is also great preparation for bigger mountain hikes in Iceland.
