Efstidalur II

A family-run farm on the Golden Circle with an ice cream barn and restaurant.

Efstidalur II is a working dairy farm near Laugarvatn where guests enjoy homemade ice cream made from the farm's own milk while watching the cows through a glass wall. The restaurant focuses on produce from the farm, including beef, skyr and cheeses.

About

Efstidalur II is a working dairy farm and family-run country spot by Laugarvatn on the Golden Circle. Guests enjoy homemade ice cream made from the farm's own milk while watching the cows through a glass wall. The restaurant emphasises farm-sourced ingredients, including beef, skyr and cheese. It is a genuine countryside experience for the whole family.

Good to know

Efstidalur II sits by Laugarvatn and makes a handy stop on the Golden Circle, between Þingvellir and Geysir. The ice cream parlour is especially popular with children and anyone wanting something quick. You can either sit down in the restaurant or grab ice cream on the go. Prices are moderate.
