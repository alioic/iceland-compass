Naustahvilft

A steep climb to the troll seat known as The Pot, with sweeping views over Ísafjörður.

Naustahvilft is a bowl-shaped hanging valley high on Kirkjubólsfjall above Skutulsfjörður, nicknamed The Pot and tied to a folk tale of a resting troll woman. The path is short but very steep and loose, and the top rewards you with one of the finest panoramas over the town of Ísafjörður and its fjord.
