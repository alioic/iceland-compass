Sundlaugin í Vogum

A small, cosy pool at the edge of Vogar, only ten minutes from the airport.

The pool in Vogar is a 16-metre outdoor pool set in a sheltered spot at the entrance to town. It has a hot pot, a children's pool, a cold tub, a sauna and spacious changing rooms, ideal for those seeking a relaxed, low-key soak.
