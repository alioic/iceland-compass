Skógarböðin (Forest Lagoon)

Geothermal baths tucked into a birch forest above Eyjafjörður.

Forest Lagoon opened in 2022 in the birch woods on the slopes of Vaðlaheiði, just minutes from Akureyri. The water comes from a hot spring struck during construction of the Vaðlaheiði tunnel and flows untreated into two infinity pools. There is also a dry Finnish sauna, a cold pool and a swim-up bar.
