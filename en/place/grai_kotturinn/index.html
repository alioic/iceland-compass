About

Grái kötturinn ("the Grey Cat") is a small, cosy café in a basement on Hverfisgata. It is one of downtown's most popular breakfast and brunch spots, known for big, generous portions. Its pancakes and hearty breakfasts draw both regulars and travellers. A snug basement that is perfect for starting the day.

Good to know

The café is on Hverfisgata downtown and is small, so it fills up fast, especially at weekends. It pays to arrive early or be ready for a short wait. It is primarily a breakfast and lunch place rather than an evening spot.
