Móðir Jörð – Vallanes

Organic farm shop and café between Egilsstaðir and Hallormsstaður forest.

At the Vallanes farm by Lagarfljót, the family grows organic grain, herbs and vegetables and turns the harvest into specialty foods. The Aspen House holds a small farm shop and café selling wholegrain products, jams, chutneys and lacto-fermented vegetables. Open daily in summer.

Organic farming at Vallanes

Móðir Jörð at Vallanes is one of East Iceland's best-known organic farms. Barley is grown on fields beside Lagarfljót and milled into wholegrain products sold in the on-site farm shop. Visitors can buy seasonal vegetables, jams and fermented foods, or enjoy a vegetarian meal in the Aspen House café. The farm is an ideal stop for travellers who want to buy local produce directly from the maker and see organic cultivation in an Icelandic setting, just a short drive from Egilsstaðir.

What the shop sells

The Vallanes farm shop offers a range of specialty foods made from what is grown on the land. You will find barley flakes and wholegrain products, jams, chutneys and lacto-fermented vegetables in the New Nordic style. During summer, fresh vegetables are sold straight from the garden as the harvest allows. The shop and café are open daily in high season and on weekdays otherwise, so travellers crossing the Hérað district can easily stop to restock their supplies.

Location and visiting

Vallanes sits by Lagarfljót, roughly midway between Egilsstaðir and the forest at Hallormsstaður, making it a convenient stop on an East Iceland road trip. Accommodation in Egilsstaðir is close by, and there are many walking routes nearby. Móðir Jörð combines organic growing, food production and hospitality in one place, a fine example of local produce you can buy directly from the farmer. It is best to check current opening hours on the website before setting off.
