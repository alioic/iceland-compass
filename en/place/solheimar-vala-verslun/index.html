Vala Shop, Sólheimar

A shop in the Sólheimar eco-village selling crafts from its art workshops and organic produce.

The Vala shop sits in Sólheimar, Iceland's oldest eco-village, founded in 1930 in Grímsnes. It sells handicrafts from the community's art workshops, including ceramics, candles, artwork and weaving, alongside organic goods from its bakery and greenhouses. The shop shares a building with the art studio and the organic café Græna kannan.

Crafts from the art workshops

The Vala shop in Sólheimar sells handicrafts made in the community's art workshops, from ceramics and candles to artwork and weaving. Sólheimar is Iceland's oldest eco-village, home to a diverse group who work together on creative projects. Each product carries a story and directly supports the residents' work. For guests on the Golden Circle it is a distinctive piece of local produce with meaning.

Organic produce on site

Beyond crafts, Vala offers organic goods from Sólheimar's bakery and greenhouses, as the community is a pioneer of organic farming in Iceland. The organic café Græna kannan shares the same building, so you can sit down and enjoy a break. That way you can combine shopping, coffee and a walk through Sólheimar's lovely surroundings in a single visit.

Visiting Sólheimar

Sólheimar in Grímsnes is only about 80 km from Reykjavík and sits conveniently on the Golden Circle. The Vala shop is open daily and invites guests to support a creative, sustainable community through their purchases. Here you can wander among art workshops, greenhouses and woodland and buy products straight from the people who make them. It is local produce with a strong community focus.
