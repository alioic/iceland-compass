Guðrúnarlaug

A historic stone pool in Sælingsdalur named after Guðrún Ósvífursdóttir of the Laxdæla Saga.

Guðrúnarlaug is a small stone-built natural pool in the Sælingsdalur valley of the Dalir region, named after Guðrún Ósvífursdóttir, the heroine of the Laxdæla Saga. Mentioned in both the Laxdæla and Sturlunga sagas, it was rebuilt in 2009 after lying unused for over a century. The water sits around 37-40°C and it is open year-round.
