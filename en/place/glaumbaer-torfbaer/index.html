Glaumbaer Turf Farm Museum

A historic turf farm in Skagafjordur where old rural life comes alive.

Glaumbaer preserves one of Iceland's best-known turf farms, built from turf, stone and timber. Visitors walk through the interconnected rooms, where furnishings and everyday objects show how people lived in the Icelandic countryside in centuries past. The site also includes two old timber houses and a cosy cafe.

The turf farm at Glaumbaer

Glaumbaer in Skagafjordur is one of Iceland's best-known turf farms and offers a unique glimpse into rural life in past centuries. The rooms of the farmhouse are linked in a row, and visitors move between the sleeping quarters, kitchen and pantry as they once were. It is an informative and enjoyable visit for the whole family and a great way to connect children with Icelandic history. The museum is open daily through the summer.

A family day in Skagafjordur

The Glaumbaer museum is an ideal stop on a trip through Northwest Iceland and works well as a short break between natural attractions. In the old timber houses on site there is a pleasant cafe where you can enjoy refreshments at a relaxed pace. The setting is beautiful and peaceful, and children get the chance to imagine life inside a turf house. The visit combines learning, history and a calm moment on the road.

History and culture at Glaumbaer

At Glaumbaer you can learn about daily life on an Icelandic farm before modern conveniences arrived. Furnishings, tools and objects show how people worked and lived and how resourceful they had to be. The museum is part of the Skagafjordur Heritage Museum and carefully preserves the cultural heritage of the district. It is a worthwhile addition to any journey focused on history and family-friendly activities.
