Seljavallalaug

One of Iceland's oldest pools, hidden in a narrow valley under Eyjafjöll.

Seljavallalaug was built in 1923 to teach locals to swim and was the largest pool in Iceland until 1936. The 25-metre pool is set into the mountainside in a narrow valley below Eyjafjallajökull, fed by a hot spring seeping through the rock. It is a roughly 20-minute walk in and free to visit.
