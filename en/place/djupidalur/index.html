Djúpidalur

A country pool and hot soak at the Djúpidalur travel service in Reykhólasveit.

At Djúpidalur in Reykhólasveit in the southern Westfjords there is a swimming pool and hot tub at the travel service. The setting is peaceful and the pool is a popular stop when travelling the area.

A bathing spot in Reykhólasveit

Djúpidalur is a travel service in Reykhólasveit in the southern Westfjords where guests can enjoy a swimming pool and hot tub in beautiful, quiet surroundings. The place is off the beaten track but a welcome stop for anyone travelling through Reykhólasveit and the surrounding area. Accommodation is available along with a place to relax after hikes and outdoor activities nearby. Djúpidalur suits those who want to experience a calm country atmosphere and reach warm water far from the crowds while touring the Westfjords.

A quiet swim in the Westfjords

For travellers who want a swim without the crowds, Djúpidalur is a good option in the southern Westfjords. The pool and hot tub are part of the travel service, so it is best to contact them in advance about access and opening hours. The setting is peaceful and ideal for a rest while exploring Reykhólasveit, an area known for its distinctive landscape and rich birdlife. Djúpidalur is a little-known but pleasant addition to Westfjords bathing culture.
