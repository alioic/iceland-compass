Kaffi Lára

A cosy café and bar by the harbour in Seyðisfjörður.

Kaffi Lára – El Grillo Bar in Seyðisfjörður is known for its warm atmosphere, home-cooked food and a good selection of Icelandic beer, including El Grillo, named after a shipwreck in the fjord.

About

Kaffi Lára – El Grillo Bar by the harbour in Seyðisfjörður is known for its warm atmosphere, home-cooked food and a good selection of Icelandic beer. Among them is El Grillo, named after a British oil tanker that sank in the fjord during the Second World War. It's a cosy place that works as both a café and a bar.

Good to know

The spot is central by the Seyðisfjörður harbour, within walking distance of the rainbow street. Seyðisfjörður is in the East Fjords and is where the Norröna ferry arrives from Europe. It's a nice place to settle in after a stroll around town.
