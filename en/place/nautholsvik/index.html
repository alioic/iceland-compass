Nauthólsvík Geothermal Beach

A man-made warm beach where geothermal water is fed into a sheltered cove to heat the sea.

Opened in 2001 and known to locals as Ylströndin, Nauthólsvík is an engineered beach where hot geothermal water warms a small lagoon of seawater. Visitors soak in hot pots, use the steam bath, and take a bracing swim in the cooler open sea beyond.
