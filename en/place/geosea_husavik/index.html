GeoSea

Geothermal sea baths on the clifftop overlooking Skjálfandi bay.

GeoSea opened in 2018 on the Húsavíkurhöfði headland and is heated with geothermal seawater from boreholes at around 38–39°C. The baths sit on a cliff edge high above the sea with an uninterrupted view over Skjálfandaflói bay. Bathers can spot whales passing by and the northern lights in winter.
