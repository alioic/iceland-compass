Kópasker Swimming Pool

Small village pool in Kópasker on the Melrakkaslétta peninsula.

Kópasker Swimming Pool is a small outdoor pool with a hot tub in the village on the eastern shore of Öxarfjörður. Entry is by key card from morning until night, a welcome stop on a drive around remote Melrakkaslétta.
