About

Kattakaffihúsið is Reykjavík's first cat café, where cats looking for a home wander among the guests and tables. It serves coffee and light refreshments in a warm, relaxed setting. It is a special and fun experience, especially for cat lovers, as the company of furry friends comes with your cup of coffee.

Good to know

The café is in central Reykjavík. Because there are cats on site, friendly house rules apply to protect the animals, and booking a table ahead can be worthwhile at busy times. A perfect choice for a calm, cosy moment in feline company.
