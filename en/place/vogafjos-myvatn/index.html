Vogafjós

A family farm by Lake Mývatn with a cowshed, food and a small shop of local goods.

Vogafjós stands on the east shore of Lake Mývatn and is a family-run farm with accommodation, a restaurant in the cowshed and a small shop of local products. The kitchen focuses on homemade food, including smoked trout, mozzarella and salad cheese from their own milk, and rye bread baked underground. Guests can watch the cows being milked twice a day.

Cowshed and local produce by Mývatn

Vogafjós is a family-run farm on the east shore of Lake Mývatn that combines accommodation, dining and a small shop of local products. The restaurant is in the cowshed itself, and through large windows guests can watch the cows being milked twice a day. The kitchen emphasises homemade food from the district, including cheese and dairy from their own milk. The place is open year round and is a popular stop in the Mývatn area.

Cheese and local products

At Vogafjós, mozzarella and salad cheese are made from the milk of the farm's cows, while smoked trout and rye bread baked underground are among the signature dishes. A small shop on site offers local products such as flavoured salt and items from Icelandic craftspeople. This lets guests take home a taste of the Mývatn area. The focus on homemade produce gives the visit a genuine rural feel.

An experience in the Mývatn area

Mývatn is one of the most popular natural areas in Northeast Iceland, and Vogafjós is a convenient stop near many of the main highlights, such as Dimmuborgir and the nature baths. On the farm, guests can enter the cowshed, pet the calves and taste warm milk straight from the cows. The blend of farming, dining and accommodation makes Vogafjós a complete experience. It suits both those stopping briefly and those staying longer by the lake.
