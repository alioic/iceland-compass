Hoffell heitir pottar

Geothermal hot tubs with views of Hoffellsjökull glacier near Höfn.

The Hoffell hot tubs sit beneath the Arnarbæli cliff about 10 km north of Höfn, overlooking Hoffellsjökull, an outlet glacier of Vatnajökull. The tubs are man-made but filled with natural geothermal water, run by the family at Hoffell alongside their guesthouse.
