Bjargarsteinn Mathús

A popular Grundarfjörður restaurant built around Icelandic ingredients.

Bjargarsteinn Mathús in Grundarfjörður showcases Icelandic ingredients and cooking, where local produce meets culinary creativity. It is one of the most popular sit-down restaurants in the area, and reservations are recommended, especially on summer evenings.

About

Bjargarsteinn Mathús in Grundarfjörður focuses on Icelandic ingredients and cooking where local seafood meets creativity. It is one of the most popular dinner spots in the area, known for carefully crafted dishes and a warm atmosphere. The setting and the view over the fjord make it a fine choice for a memorable evening on the Snæfellsnes peninsula.

Good to know

The restaurant is in Grundarfjörður, close to the iconic Kirkjufell mountain, and many tables enjoy views over the fjord. Booking a table is recommended, especially on summer evenings when it is busiest. It pairs nicely with a photo stop at nearby Kirkjufell before or after dinner.
