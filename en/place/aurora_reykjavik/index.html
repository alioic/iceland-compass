Aurora Reykjavík

A northern lights centre in the Grandi district where you can learn about and experience the aurora all year round.

Aurora Reykjavík is an exhibition in the Grandi district dedicated to the northern lights. Visitors learn about the aurora through science, stories and photography, watch the lights on a large screen and try interactive stations and virtual reality. Open all year, it lets guests experience the northern lights regardless of the weather.

The northern lights all year round

Aurora Reykjavík in the Grandi district is an exhibition devoted to the northern lights, letting guests experience them indoors at any time of year. It explains the nature of the aurora through scientific descriptions, folklore and striking photography. On a large screen you can watch the northern lights dance over Icelandic landscapes, which is ideal for those visiting outside the best aurora season or when weather keeps the lights hidden from the sky.

An interactive experience for all ages

The exhibition features touchscreens and interactive stations where guests can learn how the aurora forms, along with a virtual reality experience that places you inside the dancing lights. This makes Aurora Reykjavík an entertaining and educational visit for families and curious travellers. Staff can also share tips on how best to photograph the northern lights, useful for anyone planning to seek them in the night sky later in their trip.

Location and visiting

Aurora Reykjavík sits in the Grandi district by the harbour, about a ten-minute walk from the city centre and reachable by car or bus. The exhibition is open all year, with hours varying by season. Around the centre are other museums, cafés and restaurants, so it is easy to combine the visit with other activities in this lively harbour area. It is an especially good choice on days when the weather does not allow aurora viewing outdoors.
