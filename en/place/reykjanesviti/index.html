Reykjanesviti and Valahnúkamöl

An easy coastal walk past Iceland's oldest lighthouse, the Gunnuhver hot spring and the rugged Valahnúkamöl cliffs.

On the southwestern tip of the Reykjanes Peninsula, an easy trail links Reykjanesviti, Iceland's oldest lighthouse, the Gunnuhver geothermal area and the boulder-strewn coast at Valahnúkamöl. The route is mostly flat and suits everyone, passing steam vents, surf-battered cliffs and seabird ledges. It is a dramatic spot where the open ocean meets raw volcanic land.
