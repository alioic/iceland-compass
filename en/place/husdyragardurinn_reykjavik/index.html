Reykjavík Family Park and Zoo

Farm animals, native Icelandic wildlife and rides in Laugardalur in the heart of Reykjavík.

The Family Park and Zoo in Laugardalur combines a petting zoo of Icelandic farm animals with a family park full of rides. Visitors meet cows, sheep, horses, goats, foxes, seals and fish, and learn about Icelandic nature in a hands-on way. It is open year round and is a favourite with families.

A petting zoo in the heart of Reykjavík

The Reykjavík Family Park and Zoo sits in Laugardalur valley, only minutes from the city centre. Here families can get close to Icelandic farm animals such as cows, sheep, horses and goats, and also see seals, foxes and fish from Iceland's nature. The park is open all year round and is a popular stop for families, both locals and visitors, who want to learn about the country's animal life in a calm and accessible setting.

Rides and fun for the whole family

Beyond the animals, the site includes a family park with rides, slides and play areas that delight the youngest guests. In summer the staffed rides run longer, while in winter their opening depends on weather and weekends. That makes the park an easy place to spend a few hours with children, whether the plan is to meet the animals, play on the rides or simply relax and enjoy a snack in the green surroundings of Laugardalur.

Practical information for your visit

The Family Park and Zoo is open every day, with longer hours in the summer months and shorter hours in winter. Allow at least two hours to walk the grounds, meet the animals and try the rides. Access is easy, with parking and public transport close by. Laugardalur swimming pool, the botanical garden and other attractions are within walking distance, so it is simple to combine the visit with other activities in the valley.
