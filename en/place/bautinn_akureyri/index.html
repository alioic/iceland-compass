Bautinn

A long-established restaurant in one of the oldest houses in Akureyri.

Bautinn sits right in the heart of Akureyri in one of the town's oldest and most beautiful houses and has long been a local favorite. It offers good-quality food at affordable prices in a lively, welcoming atmosphere.

About

Bautinn is a long-established restaurant in the heart of Akureyri, set in one of the town's oldest and prettiest houses, and long a favourite with locals. It serves good food at fair prices in a lively, welcoming atmosphere. A classic spot for traditional Icelandic fare that suits the whole family.

Good to know

Located in central Akureyri, within walking distance of the pedestrian street, shops and museums. Family-friendly and reasonably priced, making it a good choice for an everyday meal. It can get lively at busy times in summer.
