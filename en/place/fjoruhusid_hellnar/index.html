Fjöruhúsið

A family-run café perched on the sea cliffs at Hellnar.

Fjöruhúsið is a family-run café in Hellnar on the southern side of Snæfellsnes, set on rocky cliffs overlooking the Atlantic. It serves homemade dishes such as seafood soup, quiche, waffles and a range of cakes, and is open through the summer season.

About

Fjöruhúsið is a family-run café in Hellnar on the southern Snæfellsnes peninsula, perched on cliffs overlooking the Atlantic. It serves homemade dishes such as seafood soup, pie, waffles and a selection of cakes. Its seaside location and homely atmosphere make it one of the most popular café stops in the area during summer.

Good to know

The café sits along the coastal path between Hellnar and Arnarstapi, one of the loveliest shore walks on Snæfellsnes, and is an ideal stop before or after the hike. It is mainly open during the summer season. The views over the ocean and sea cliffs are part of the draw.
