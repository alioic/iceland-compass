Café Babalú

Warm, colourful café a short walk from Hallgrímskirkja.

Café Babalú is a small, homely café on Skólavörðustígur known for its quirky, eclectic interior. It is a favourite for coffee, hot chocolate and homemade cheesecake.

About

Café Babalú is a small, homely café on Skólavörðustígur, known for its quirky and playful interior. It is popular for coffee, hot chocolate and home-baked cheesecake. The atmosphere is warm and relaxed, with a colourful, cosy feel that makes the place memorable. A lovely spot to warm up on a cold day.

Good to know

The café is on Skólavörðustígur, just a short stroll from Hallgrímskirkja, making it an ideal coffee break after visiting the church. It is small and cosy but can get tight at busy times. Best for a relaxed pause over coffee and cake rather than a full meal.
