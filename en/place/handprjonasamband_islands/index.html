Handknitting Association of Iceland

Shop of the cooperative of Icelandic hand-knitters in central Reykjavík.

The Handknitting Association of Iceland, founded in 1977, is a cooperative of hundreds of Icelandic knitters. Its stores on Skólavörðustígur and Borgartún sell hand-knitted lopapeysa sweaters and wool goods made by members from Icelandic wool.

A knitters' cooperative

The Handknitting Association of Iceland was founded in 1977 and is owned and run by hundreds of Icelandic knitters, mostly women, from all over the country. Its Reykjavík stores sell above all goods that members have hand-knitted from Icelandic wool. Here you can buy a genuine lopapeysa straight from the person who knitted it, which makes each piece unique. For travellers who want to support local craft, this is one of the most reliable stops in the city centre.

A genuine lopapeysa

The association's main focus is on goods made from Icelandic wool, especially the thick traditional lopi. The store carries hand-knitted lopapeysa sweaters in many sizes and patterns, along with hats, mittens, scarves and more, plus some machine-made items using Icelandic wool. Because members knit at home, the selection is varied and always changing. It is an ideal place to buy durable local goods that carry a genuine story of Icelandic craftsmanship you can take home.

Central Reykjavík

The association's shop at Skólavörðustígur 19 sits in the heart of the city on one of Reykjavík's most popular shopping streets, leading up to Hallgrímskirkja church. A second store is on Borgartún. The location makes it easy for travellers to drop in, handle the garments, find the right size and buy direct. The staff know the products and the story behind them. It is a convenient and dependable way to bring home an Icelandic wool piece.
