Skaftfell Bistro

An artistic bistro and cultural centre in the heart of Seyðisfjörður.

Skaftfell is a visual art centre and bistro in one and the same building in Seyðisfjörður. A relaxed spot where artists and travellers meet over popular pizzas, soups and home baking — surrounded by contemporary art.

About

Skaftfell Bistro is an art centre and bistro under one roof in the heart of Seyðisfjörður. It is a relaxed spot where artists and travellers meet over popular handmade pizzas, soups and home baking, surrounded by contemporary art. The mood is creative and easygoing, and the place is one of the beating hearts of the town's cultural life.

Good to know

Skaftfell is centrally located in Seyðisfjörður, a short walk from the famous rainbow street and blue church. The town sits in the East Fjords, reached by a mountain road that can close in winter snow. It's worth browsing the art exhibitions while you're there.
