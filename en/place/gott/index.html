Gott

A popular family-run spot on Heimaey with fresh local ingredients.

Gott is a cosy, family-run restaurant on Heimaey that emphasises fresh, healthy local ingredients — fish straight off the boats, home baking and dishes to suit the whole family.

About

Gott is a cosy, family-run restaurant on Heimaey in the Vestmannaeyjar islands. The focus is on fresh, healthy, locally sourced ingredients, including fish straight off the boats and home-baked sides. The menu is varied and suits the whole family, and the atmosphere is relaxed and friendly. A good spot to refuel after exploring the islands.

Good to know

The restaurant is in Vestmannaeyjar, reached by ferry from Landeyjahöfn or by plane. Gott works well for families with children and for anyone after lighter, fresher fare. During the busy summer season it can be worth arriving early or booking a table, as the islands get lively.
