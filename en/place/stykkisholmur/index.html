Stykkishólmur

A beautiful harbour town on Snæfellsnes with old timber houses.

Stykkishólmur is a picturesque seaside town with well-preserved timber houses, a colourful harbour and views over Breiðafjörður with its countless islands. From here the ferry Baldur sails across to the Westfjords.

Stykkishólmur on Snæfellsnes – a picturesque harbour town on Breiðafjörður

Stykkishólmur is one of the most charming coastal towns in West Iceland, set at the northern edge of the Snæfellsnes peninsula on the island-studded bay of Breiðafjörður. The town is celebrated for its beautifully preserved old timber houses, its colourful working harbour and its relaxed yet welcoming atmosphere. As the largest settlement on Snæfellsnes, Stykkishólmur is an ideal base for exploring the peninsula. It also serves as a natural gateway between West Iceland and the Westfjords, thanks to the Baldur car ferry that crosses Breiðafjörður from the harbour.

Things to see and do in Stykkishólmur

In Stykkishólmur you can wander the old town with its historic timber houses and climb out to the island of Súgandisey, where a lighthouse crowns sweeping views over Breiðafjörður. A highlight for many visitors is a boat tour among the countless islands, skerries and rich birdlife of the bay. Culture lovers should not miss the Library of Water (Vatnasafnið), a unique art installation devoted to Icelandic water. Cafés, fresh seafood and a leisurely stroll along the harbour round out a visit to this photogenic Snæfellsnes town.

Getting to Stykkishólmur – location on Snæfellsnes

Stykkishólmur lies roughly two to two and a half hours' drive from Reykjavík. The route runs north through Borgarnes and then follows the Snæfellsnes road along the northern coast of the peninsula. The town is well signposted and accessible year-round. From Stykkishólmur, the Baldur car ferry sails across Breiðafjörður with a stop at the island of Flatey, a convenient way to link a Snæfellsnes trip with the Westfjords without driving all the way around the fjords. This makes the town a practical stop on a longer road trip through West Iceland.

Best time to visit Stykkishólmur and practical tips

Stykkishólmur welcomes visitors all year. Summer, from June to August, is the liveliest season, with the longest daylight, active boat tours and regular ferry crossings over Breiðafjörður. In autumn and winter the town is quieter and lovely under snow, with good chances of northern lights on clear nights. It is wise to book accommodation and boat tours ahead during the peak summer months. Bring warm, windproof clothing, as the sea air on Breiðafjörður can be cool, and allow time to enjoy a walk through the historic old town.
