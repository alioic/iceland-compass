Sægreifinn

The little seaside shack by the harbour, famous for its lobster soup.

Sægreifinn is an unpretentious seaside shack by Reykjavík's old harbour that became world-famous for its lobster soup. Guests sit on barrels and pick fresh fish and whale skewers from the ice to be grilled — a simple but authentic experience.

About

Sægreifinn ("the Sea Baron") is an unpretentious harbourside shack in Reykjavík that became world-famous for its lobster soup. Guests sit on barrels, pick fresh fish and skewers straight from the ice, and have them grilled on the spot. The mood is relaxed and genuine. The lobster soup is the classic order, and the grilled fish skewers are a firm favourite too.

Good to know

You will find it right on Reykjavík's old harbour, an easy walk from downtown and the Grandi area. It is small and popular, so expect a short wait at busy times. A great spot for a simple but memorable meal with a real harbour atmosphere, at lunch or early evening.
