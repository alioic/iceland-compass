Dill

Renowned fine-dining restaurant focused on New Nordic cuisine.

About

Dill is one of the most respected restaurants in the country, focusing on New Nordic cuisine made from Icelandic ingredients. It offers a multi-course tasting menu that changes with the seasons, often with wine pairing. This is a fine-dining experience where seasonal produce and careful craft take centre stage. An evening for those looking to treat themselves.

Good to know

Dill is in central Reykjavík and one of the most sought-after fine-dining spots in Iceland. Booking well in advance is essential, often weeks ahead. Expect a leisurely, multi-course evening rather than a quick meal, and note that a tasting menu is the main format.
