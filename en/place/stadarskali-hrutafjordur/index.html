Staðarskáli

A historic roadside stop and shop on the Ring Road in Hrútafjörður.

Staðarskáli in Hrútafjörður is one of Iceland's best-known roadside stops, roughly halfway between Reykjavik and Akureyri. It has a service centre, a shop, food and a tourist information point. The place has long been a traditional halt on the northern route.

A roadside stop in Hrútafjörður

Staðarskáli is one of the best-known stops on the Ring Road, located in Hrútafjörður roughly halfway between Reykjavik and Akureyri. For decades it has been a traditional halt where travellers stretch their legs, grab a bite and fill up on fuel. A modern, spacious service centre offers a shop, food and good facilities. For many Icelanders, Staðarskáli is an inseparable part of the northern journey.

Shop and services on the way

Staðarskáli has a shop with travel essentials, snacks, drinks and various goods that come in handy on the road. There is also food service, restrooms and charging stations for electric cars. A tourist information point provides guidance about the area and nearby destinations. Its location makes it an ideal place to rest and prepare for the next leg of the route.

Part of the northern route's story

Staðarskáli has a long history as a gathering place and rest stop in Hrútafjörður and is closely tied to travel culture in North Iceland. Although the building has been renewed, the place keeps its status as a symbolic midpoint between the capital and Akureyri. Here locals and travellers meet, and it is a good gateway to Húnaþing vestra. Few drive the northern route without stopping at Staðarskáli.
