Dynjandi

The thundering bridal veil of the Westfjords - a roughly 100 m tiered waterfall.

Dynjandi is the most majestic waterfall in the Westfjords, around 100 metres high and fanning out as it descends like a bridal veil. Below it are six smaller waterfalls, followed by a trail that runs alongside them. The falls are protected and one of the foremost symbols of the Westfjords.

Dynjandi – the majestic jewel of the Westfjords

Dynjandi, sometimes called Fjallfoss, is the most magnificent and best-known waterfall in the Westfjords and one of the great symbols of this remote region of Iceland. The waterfall drops around 100 metres and fans out as it falls, resembling a bridal veil, which gives it an exceptionally beautiful and photogenic form. Dynjandi tumbles down a mountainside into Arnarfjörður, one of the grandest fjords in the Westfjords. The area is protected, and the falls draw travellers who want to experience pristine nature and powerful cascades far from the crowds. Dynjandi is an essential stop on any Westfjords journey.

Things to see and do at Dynjandi

Below the main Dynjandi cascade lies a series of smaller waterfalls, six in all, with a footpath running alongside them up to the base of the main falls. The walk is short but steep and offers a unique chance to experience each waterfall in turn and feel the power and spray of the water. At the top, Fjallfoss appears in all its glory, with sweeping views over Arnarfjörður. Photographers are especially drawn to Dynjandi for its distinctive fan shape. A campsite and toilet facilities are located by the lower car park, making a visit comfortable and convenient.

Getting to Dynjandi – location on Arnarfjörður

Dynjandi lies at the head of Arnarfjörður in the southern Westfjords, along Route 60, which links the region's settlements. From Ísafjörður the drive takes about an hour and a quarter to an hour and a half, while from Reykjavík the journey spans several hours, often via the Dýrafjörður tunnel and mountain roads. A marked car park sits at the base of the falls, with the trail leading up from there. Its location makes Dynjandi an ideal stop on a loop through the Westfjords, whether driving between Ísafjörður, Þingeyri or Bíldudalur.

Best time to visit Dynjandi and practical tips

The best time to visit Dynjandi is from June to September, when the mountain roads of the Westfjords are passable and summer daylight is longest. During this period the water flow is often strong and the waterfall is at its most powerful. In winter the roads to the falls may be closed or difficult due to snow, so check road conditions and weather forecasts carefully. Wear sturdy walking shoes and waterproof clothing, as the path can be wet and the spray from the falls reaches visitors. Allow plenty of time to walk all the way up to Fjallfoss.
