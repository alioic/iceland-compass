Museum of Icelandic Sorcery & Witchcraft

An exhibition on sorcery and the witch-hunts of Strandir.

The Museum of Icelandic Sorcery & Witchcraft in Hólmavík explores Iceland's magical traditions, its runic staves and the witch persecutions of the 17th century. The exhibition paints a vivid and at times eerie picture of the superstition and sorcery of earlier centuries through objects, texts and reconstructions. There is also a restaurant on site focusing on local ingredients.

The Sorcery and Witchcraft Museum

The Museum of Icelandic Sorcery & Witchcraft in Hólmavík is one of the country's most unusual museums, exploring the magical culture of Icelanders in centuries past. It tells of runic staves, incantations and the notorious witch persecutions of the 17th century, when people were burned at the stake for sorcery. The exhibition blends scholarship, folklore and reconstructions to give a vivid and at times unsettling picture of the old worldview. The museum is indoors and suits any weather. There is also a restaurant on site focusing on local ingredients from Strandir.

Opening hours and visiting

The museum is open daily, with longer hours in summer and shorter hours in winter. Hólmavík is the largest settlement in Strandir and a good stopping point on a journey around the north-eastern Westfjords. A visit usually takes about an hour and appeals to both history enthusiasts and families. There is a restaurant on site where you can enjoy a meal after your visit. Note that the subject matter can be fairly dark, but it is presented in an informative and engaging way for a broad range of visitors.

Culture and history in Strandir

The Sorcery and Witchcraft Museum sheds light on a history that was long left unspoken: the persecution of alleged sorcerers in Iceland. The exhibition is part of a larger project on the history of the Strandir region and forms part of the area's cultural life. For travellers it is a welcome chance to learn about the folklore and superstition that shaped people's lives for centuries. Hólmavík also offers a pretty harbour, a swimming pool and good services. A visit to the museum is a memorable addition to a trip around Strandir.
