Grillmarkaðurinn

Award-winning downtown restaurant with a focus on grilled dishes.

About

Grillmarkaðurinn ("the Grill Market") is a well-known, award-winning restaurant in central Reykjavík that works closely with Icelandic farmers and producers. The menu is built around fish and meat, often straight off the grill, with a focus on fresh, quality Icelandic ingredients. The setting is refined and warm, ideal for a memorable dinner.

Good to know

The restaurant is centrally located in Reykjavík, within walking distance of the main downtown landmarks. It is a popular dinner spot, so booking a table ahead is wise, especially at weekends. A good fit for a foodie meal or a special occasion centred on grilled dishes.
