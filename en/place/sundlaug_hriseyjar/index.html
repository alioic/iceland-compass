Hrísey Swimming Pool

A little island pool with hot tub and cold plunge on Hrísey in Eyjafjörður.

A 12.5-metre outdoor pool with a hot tub, paddling pool and cold plunge on Hrísey, the pearl of Eyjafjörður. An unforgettable open-air swim after the short ferry ride to the island.
