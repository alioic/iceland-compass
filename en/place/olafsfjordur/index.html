Ólafsfjörður og Héðinsfjörður

A quiet fjord town on Tröllaskagi with the uninhabited Héðinsfjörður alongside.

Ólafsfjörður is part of Fjallabyggð, connected to Siglufjörður and Eyjafjörður by long tunnels. Between them lies Héðinsfjörður, uninhabited since the mid-20th century - a tranquil fjord with a lake, trails and untouched nature beneath the steep peaks of Tröllaskagi.

Ólafsfjörður and Héðinsfjörður — tranquillity on the Tröllaskagi peninsula

Ólafsfjörður is a quiet fjord town on the Tröllaskagi peninsula, part of the Fjallabyggð municipality and linked to Siglufjörður and Eyjafjörður by long road tunnels. The town sits beneath steep peaks at the mouth of its fjord and is defined by calm, a pretty harbour and its closeness to untouched nature. Right next door lies the uninhabited fjord of Héðinsfjörður, deserted since the mid-20th century — one of the hidden gems of North Iceland. Together these two fjords form an area that captivates travellers seeking solitude, mountains and genuine Icelandic scenery away from the busiest tourist routes.

Things to do in Ólafsfjörður and Héðinsfjörður

Héðinsfjörður is a paradise for hikers, with its still lake, marked trails and pristine nature beneath the peaks of Tröllaskagi. In Ólafsfjörður itself you can enjoy trout fishing, wander the town and experience the relaxed life of a northern fishing community. In winter Tröllaskagi is renowned for excellent snow, both at ski areas and for backcountry ski touring, and the tunnels make it easy to travel between the fjords. The Múlagöng and Héðinsfjörður tunnels are part of the experience in themselves. The area is ideal for combining outdoor activity, hiking and quiet in a dramatic mountain landscape.

Getting to Ólafsfjörður — the route across Tröllaskagi

Ólafsfjörður sits on the northern side of the Tröllaskagi peninsula and connects to the outside world through tunnels — the Múla tunnel to Dalvík and Eyjafjörður, and the Héðinsfjörður tunnels to Siglufjörður. From Akureyri the drive takes about an hour, and the route is part of the scenic coastal loop around Tröllaskagi that links Eyjafjörður with Skagafjörður. The tunnels keep the area accessible year-round, though it is wise to check road conditions in winter. The location makes Ólafsfjörður and Héðinsfjörður perfect stops on a circuit of Tröllaskagi, between Siglufjörður, Dalvík and Akureyri.

Best time to visit Ólafsfjörður and practical tips

Summer and early autumn are best for hiking in Héðinsfjörður and enjoying the calm of Ólafsfjörður, when roads are clear and the landscape is at its greenest. In winter Tröllaskagi draws skiers and ski tourers, but you must pay close attention to the weather, avalanche risk and tunnel conditions. Bring good gear for changeable mountain weather, as conditions can shift quickly. It is worth planning your trip with several stops around Tröllaskagi, and treating the fragile, little-touched nature of Héðinsfjörður with respect and care.
