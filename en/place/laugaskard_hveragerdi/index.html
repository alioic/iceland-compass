Sundlaugin í Laugaskarði

A historic 50-metre outdoor pool tucked in a sheltered gap in Hveragerði.

Laugaskarð pool opened in 1938 and was for a time the largest in Iceland after being extended to 50 metres in 1963. Set in a sheltered gap with mountain views and heated by geothermal steam, it also has a hot tub, a cold plunge and a natural steam bath.
