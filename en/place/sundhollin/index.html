Sundhöll Reykjavíkur

Reykjavík's oldest pool, a 1937 functionalist landmark in the heart of downtown.

Designed by celebrated architect Guðjón Samúelsson, Sundhöllin opened in 1937 as the city's first indoor pool. Its white, almost art-deco building frames the historic indoor bath, while a later extension added an outdoor pool and rooftop hot pots.
