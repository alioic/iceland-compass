Kaffihús Bakarans

A popular bakery and café in the heart of Ísafjörður.

Kaffihús Bakarans sits on Hafnarstræti in central Ísafjörður and is open throughout the week. It serves freshly baked bread and pastries alongside soups, sandwiches, crêpes and other light fare.

About

Kaffihús Bakarans is a popular bakery and café in the heart of Ísafjörður on Hafnarstræti. It offers freshly baked bread and pastries along with soups, sandwiches, pancakes and other light dishes. The place is homely and relaxed, drawing both locals and travellers looking for a good bite and good coffee in an unhurried setting.

Good to know

The bakery sits in a handy spot in central Ísafjörður and is open every day of the week, making it a convenient stop at any time. It is ideal for morning coffee, a lunch break or an afternoon snack. Its downtown location makes it easy to combine a visit with a stroll through town.
