Sólskinsbúðin Farmers Market

A farmers market in a Flúðir greenhouse selling vegetables, baked goods and produce straight from nearby farms.

Sólskinsbúðin is a farmers market inside a greenhouse in Flúðir, selling vegetables, home-baked bread, meat, fresh herbs, eggs and more. Everything is sourced on site or from nearby farms, and guests can see into the greenhouses where tomatoes and cucumbers are grown. Cultivation has taken place in Flúðir since 1944.

Farm-direct in a greenhouse

Sólskinsbúðin in Flúðir is a farmers market inside a greenhouse where guests can buy vegetables, baked goods, meat, eggs and herbs straight from the producers. The produce comes from the site or nearby farms, and visitors can watch tomatoes and cucumbers growing right beside the market. It is a living example of local produce with a short path from field to counter, only about an hour from Reykjavík.

Fresh harvest in Flúðir

Flúðir is known for greenhouse cultivation, and Sólskinsbúðin makes full use of this tradition, with growing on the site since 1944. The market offers a varied selection by season, and the shop often shares on social media what is available on any given day. For travellers on the Golden Circle it is an ideal place to buy fresh Icelandic produce straight from the farmers.

A Golden Circle stop

Sólskinsbúðin is within walking distance of central Flúðir and fits well into a Golden Circle and South Iceland trip. You can buy provisions for the day, fresh vegetables and home-baked bread, and coffee is available too. It is best to follow the shop's social media for opening hours and availability. The visit supports local cultivation and offers a genuine taste of South Iceland food production.
