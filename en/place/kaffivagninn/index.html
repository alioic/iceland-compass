Kaffivagninn

About

Kaffivagninn sits on Grandagarður by the west harbour and is said to be the oldest operating restaurant in the country. It serves Icelandic home cooking and the day's fish dishes with a view over the harbour. The place is full of history and unpretentious, where fishermen and locals have long eaten side by side with visitors.

Good to know

Kaffivagninn is in the Grandi area by the old west harbour, within walking distance of downtown. It is ideal for lunch or an evening meal with a harbour view and a focus on fish. At busy times it is worth booking a table for dinner, especially at weekends.
