Álafoss Wool Store

Historic wool store in Álafosskvos selling lopi yarn and sweaters.

Álafoss has worked with Icelandic wool since 1896, and its store in Álafosskvos in Mosfellsbær sells lopi yarn, wool, hand-knitted lopapeysa sweaters and wool goods. The little valley itself is a scenic heritage spot beside the old wool mill.

Wool since 1896

Álafoss is one of the oldest names in Icelandic wool, working with the fibre since 1896. The store sits in Álafosskvos in Mosfellsbær, just outside Reykjavík, beside the old wool mill by the river. Here travellers can buy genuine Icelandic lopi, yarn and sweaters right in the heartland of the trade. The spot combines shopping and history and is a popular stop for knitters and anyone interested in Icelandic craft. It is an easy detour from the capital.

Lopi, yarn and sweaters

The store carries a wide range of Icelandic lopi and yarn in every colour, along with hand-knitted lopapeysa sweaters and other wool goods. Those who want to knit their own sweater find materials and inspiration here, while finished garments made from Icelandic wool are also for sale. Álafoss is an ideal place to buy durable local goods, whether as a gift or for yourself. The shop is open on weekdays and welcomes visitors year-round.

Álafosskvos valley

Álafosskvos is a small, pretty valley in Mosfellsbær where the river drops over the waterfall the mill is named after. The area is historic and pleasant to wander, and the wool store is its heart. For travellers who want to get out of the city centre while staying in the capital region, this is an easy and rewarding visit. You can combine nature, history and local shopping in one calm spot just minutes from Reykjavík.
