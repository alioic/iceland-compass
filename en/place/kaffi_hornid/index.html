Kaffi Hornið

A well-established café and restaurant in a log building in Höfn.

Kaffi Hornið is a cozy log-cabin spot in Höfn focused on local ingredients and seafood. The langoustine soup is among its most popular dishes, alongside lamb and other Icelandic fare.

About

Kaffi Hornið is a long-established, cosy spot in a log house in Höfn, focused on local ingredients and seafood. The langoustine soup is among the most popular dishes, but you'll also find lamb and other traditional Icelandic fare. It's a favourite with both locals and travellers, with a relaxed atmosphere.

Good to know

The place is in central Höfn, within walking distance of the harbour. Höfn is on the southeast corner along the Ring Road and a natural stop near Vatnajökull. The log-house setting keeps it warm and welcoming year-round.
