Dalvík – A Peaceful Fishing Town on Eyjafjörður

Dalvík is a cosy, laid-back fishing town on the western shore of Eyjafjörður, framed by the high mountains of the Tröllaskagi peninsula. It is known for its warm atmosphere, excellent whale watching and great year-round outdoor recreation. In nearby Árskógssandur you'll find the unusual Beer Spa, where guests bathe in young beer, and the ferry departs from there to Hrísey, the pearl of Eyjafjörður. Dalvík is an ideal stop for travellers who want to experience relaxed Icelandic town life, maritime culture and the spectacular scenery of the fjord without the crowds found at larger destinations.

Things to See and Do in Dalvík

Whale watching from Dalvík is one of the town's main draws, as Eyjafjörður is rich in humpback whales, minke whales and other species. Sea angling is also popular, and you can often grill your catch afterwards. In Árskógssandur the Beer Spa offers a uniquely relaxing soak, and from there it is a short ferry ride to Hrísey. In summer the town bursts into life during the Great Fish Day, one of Iceland's largest town festivals, with a free seafood feast. Around Dalvík, numerous hiking trails and ski routes wind through the mountains of Tröllaskagi.

How to Get to Dalvík

Dalvík lies on the western side of the Tröllaskagi peninsula along Eyjafjörður, just a 40-minute drive north of Akureyri on a good road that follows the fjord. From Dalvík the route continues through the Ólafsfjörður and Héðinsfjörður tunnels to Ólafsfjörður and Siglufjörður, making the town a natural part of a Tröllaskagi loop. From Reykjavík the drive takes about five hours via the Ring Road and north. At Árskógssandur, just south of Dalvík, the ferry to Hrísey departs. The road is passable year-round, but in winter it is wise to check conditions with the Icelandic Road Administration.

Best Time to Visit Dalvík and Practical Tips

Summer, from June to August, is the best time to visit Dalvík, with long bright days, prime whale-watching conditions and a lively atmosphere, especially during the Great Fish Day festival in August. During this period it is wise to book accommodation and tours well in advance. In winter the area is popular with skiers on Tröllaskagi and offers good opportunities to see the northern lights. Bring warm, waterproof clothing for boat trips and hikes, and allow time to enjoy both the town and its surroundings, including the Beer Spa and the island of Hrísey.
