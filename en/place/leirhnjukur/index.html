Leirhnjúkur

A loop through fresh lava and steaming geothermal ground in the Krafla caldera.

Leirhnjúkur sits in the heart of the Krafla caldera and is one of Iceland's youngest lava fields, formed in the Krafla Fires of 1975-1984. A marked loop trail crosses ropey pahoehoe lava, passing bubbling mud pots and steaming fissures where geothermal heat escapes. The terrain is mostly flat but the surface is uneven, so sturdy shoes are essential and you must keep to the path.
