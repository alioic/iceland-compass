Flóra Culture House

A design and culture house in central Akureyri with goods from local makers.

Flóra is a concept store and culture house set in a green oasis in central Akureyri. It offers a varied selection from small producers, artists, designers and farmers, including woollens, honey, herbs, candles and art. The house is also a venue for events and workshops.

A design store in central Akureyri

Flóra is a concept store and culture house in one of Akureyri's oldest buildings, Sigurhæðir, set in a green oasis in the town centre. It offers a varied selection from small producers, artists, designers, farmers and other creative people. The store emphasises handmade items and products made under conditions friendly to people and nature. It is an ideal place to find distinctive souvenirs with a North Iceland connection.

Goods from local makers

At Flóra you can find woollens, honey, coffee, Icelandic herbs, candles, art and much more, all chosen with an eye to quality and sustainability. By shopping there, people directly support creative locals and small producers in North Iceland. The selection is ever-changing and reflects the seasons and the variety of craft in the region. The store is part of a lively cultural life in the house.

Culture and events

Besides the shop, Flóra is a venue for workshops, exhibitions and cultural events in the heart of Akureyri. The house hosts studios for artists and creative people and keeps the town's cultural life vibrant. Guests can thus both shop and enjoy culture in the same place. Its central location makes Flóra a convenient stop for anyone walking around Akureyri.
