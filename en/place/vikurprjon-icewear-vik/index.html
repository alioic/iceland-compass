Víkurprjón (Icewear)

One of Iceland's oldest wool works in Vík, with lopapeysa sweaters, wool goods and an on-site factory.

Víkurprjón has processed Icelandic wool in Vík since 1972 and is today part of Icewear. It offers lopapeysa sweaters, knitwear, outdoor clothing and souvenirs, and visitors can see production in the factory. The same building also houses a café and a small grocery store.

Icelandic wool in Vík

Víkurprjón is one of Iceland's oldest wool works and has processed Icelandic wool in Vík since 1972. The shop offers lopapeysa sweaters, knitwear and a wide range of outdoor clothing under the Icewear brand. What makes it special is that production happens on site, and visitors can watch the wool become finished garments. It is genuine local produce in the heart of the south coast.

Lopapeysa sweaters and yarn

The lopapeysa is one of Iceland's most recognisable symbols, and Víkurprjón offers a good selection, both finished garments and yarn to knit your own. You will also find hats, mittens, scarves and other woollens suited to Icelandic weather. For travellers who want a genuine Icelandic keepsake, it is an ideal stop where tradition and craft meet under one roof.

More than a shop

The Vík building holds not only the wool shop but also a café and a small grocery store, making it a convenient stop on the southern route. You can tour the factory and see the machines that work the wool. Here you can combine shopping, coffee and insight into Iceland's wool tradition in a single visit, while supporting production with deep roots in Vík.
