Icelandic Museum of Rock 'n' Roll

An interactive museum on the history of Icelandic popular music in Reykjanesbær.

The Icelandic Museum of Rock 'n' Roll in Reykjanesbær traces the story of Icelandic pop and rock music from the early twentieth century to the present day. The museum is interactive, with sound booths, instruments and multimedia where visitors can listen, play and learn. It lies close to Keflavík Airport and suits all ages.

The story of Icelandic music in Reykjanesbær

The Icelandic Museum of Rock 'n' Roll in Reykjanesbær is dedicated to the history of Icelandic pop and rock music, from the first decades of the twentieth century to the present. The museum traces the music's development over time and tells the stories of well-known musicians and bands that have shaped Iceland's musical heritage. It is an enjoyable and educational destination for music lovers of all ages, and its location near Keflavík Airport makes it an easy stop on a tour of the Reykjanes peninsula.

An interactive experience for the whole family

The Icelandic Museum of Rock 'n' Roll emphasises interactivity, letting visitors try instruments, sing in a sound booth and listen to Icelandic music through multimedia stations. This makes the visit lively and fun for children and adults alike, as guests can take an active part rather than simply looking. The museum is therefore ideal for families who want to learn about and enjoy Iceland's musical heritage at the same time.

Opening hours and location

The Icelandic Museum of Rock 'n' Roll is open every day from morning until evening, except on the major holidays of the year. The museum is in Reykjanesbær, only a few minutes' drive from Keflavík Airport, making it a convenient destination at the start or end of a trip to Iceland. Allow time to make full use of the interactive stations and enjoy rediscovering Icelandic music from different periods of its history.
