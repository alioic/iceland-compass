The Icelandic Seal Center

Interactive seal exhibition in the red harbour house at Hvammstangi.

The Icelandic Seal Center is a research and education facility dedicated to Iceland's harbour and grey seals. Exhibitions explain seal biology, distribution and the long relationship between seals and people since settlement. It makes a perfect first stop before heading out onto the Vatnsnes peninsula to watch seals in the wild.

A family visit to the Icelandic Seal Center

The Icelandic Seal Center in Hvammstangi is a great stop for families travelling through Northwest Iceland. Inside the red harbour house, children and adults alike get to know harbour and grey seals through a lively, interactive exhibition. You can learn about how seals live, where they gather, and how to watch them without disturbance. It works perfectly as a first stop before driving out onto the Vatnsnes peninsula, where seals often haul out on rocks along the shore.

Seal watching from Hvammstangi

Vatnsnes is one of Iceland's best seal-watching areas, and the Icelandic Seal Center offers helpful guidance on where and when you are most likely to see them. Staff point visitors toward spots such as Illugastadir and Osar and explain why the tides matter. This helps guests set realistic expectations and plan their day well. The exhibition itself suits all ages and is ideal for a short stop on the drive between Reykjavik and Akureyri.

What to expect at the Seal Center

The Icelandic Seal Center is an indoor exhibition, which is a real advantage in the changeable northern weather. Visitors can explore educational displays about seals as well as the birds and whales of the Vatnsnes peninsula at a relaxed pace. The center sits centrally in Hvammstangi with cafes and other services close by. It is a comfortable, informative addition to any family trip through the Hunathing vestra district.
