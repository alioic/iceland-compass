Sundlaug Hofsóss

A celebrated infinity pool perched above Skagafjörður fjord.

The Hofsós pool is one of Iceland's most beautiful, designed by Basalt Architects and completed in 2010. The edge of the 25-metre pool visually merges with Skagafjörður so the water appears to spill into the sea. The view takes in Drangey island and the Þórðarhöfði headland.
