About

Café Kaja is a certified organic café in Akranes with an emphasis on organic ingredients and wholesome food. The menu includes fish soup, falafel salad, avocado toast, cakes and coffee, with good options for vegetarian and vegan guests. It is a homely, relaxed spot for a light meal or an unhurried cup of coffee.

Good to know

The café is in Akranes, a short drive from Reykjavík and conveniently on the way for travellers exploring West Iceland. Its focus on organic, wholesome ingredients makes it a good choice for guests seeking lighter fare. It works well for lunch or an afternoon break while touring the area.
