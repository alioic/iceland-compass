Noa Seafood

A seafood restaurant on Hafnarstræti focused on local fish.

Noa Seafood sits on Hafnarstræti a short way from downtown and serves local fish, lamb and beef prepared in various ways. Dishes come with fresh vegetables that round out the flavors.

About

Noa Seafood is on Hafnarstræti a short distance from central Akureyri, serving local fish, lamb and beef prepared in various ways. Dishes come with fresh vegetables that round out the flavours. Its focus on seafood and good ingredients makes it a solid choice for anyone wanting to enjoy northern Icelandic fish in a comfortable setting.

Good to know

Located on Hafnarstræti, a short walk from Akureyri's centre and within reach of accommodation and shops. Well suited to dinner. As the emphasis is on fresh fish, the selection can be seasonal, and booking can pay off at busy times.
