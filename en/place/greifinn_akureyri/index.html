Greifinn

A popular family restaurant with a broad menu of pizzas, burgers and more.

Greifinn is all about variety, offering everything from pizzas and burgers to more traditional Icelandic dishes. The aim is a wide menu where everyone can find something to their liking.

About

Greifinn is a popular family restaurant in Akureyri with an emphasis on variety. The menu runs from pizzas and burgers to more traditional Icelandic dishes, so most people should find something to their taste. A relaxed, child-friendly spot that works well when a group wants different dishes at the same table.

Good to know

Located in Akureyri and popular with families. Its broad menu makes it an easy choice when tastes differ within a group. It can fill up in the evenings and at weekends, so it is worth considering a booking at busy times.
