Hraunborgir

A pool with three hot tubs in the Hraunborgir summer-house area in Grímsnes.

At Hraunborgir in Grímsnes there is a swimming pool with three hot tubs and a steam bath in the area's service centre. There is also mini golf, a nine-hole golf course and children's playground equipment.

A swim at Hraunborgir

Hraunborgir in Grímsnes is a popular summer-house and camping area about an hour's drive from Reykjavík. The area's service centre has a swimming pool with three hot tubs and a steam bath, making Hraunborgir a good stop for families touring South Iceland. The site also offers mini golf, a nine-hole golf course, children's playground equipment and a viewpoint. Hraunborgir is an ideal choice for anyone wanting to combine a swim, outdoor activities and relaxation in the beautiful Grímsnes area, close to the Golden Circle.

A family-friendly bathing spot in Grímsnes

Hraunborgir suits families especially well, as the pool, hot tubs and sauna are part of a larger recreation area with golf, mini golf and playground equipment. The site is in Grímsnes, near many of South Iceland's natural attractions and the Golden Circle. The pool is a welcome amenity for summer-house guests and travellers passing through. Note that the service centre is privately operated, so it is best to check opening hours before setting out to visit.
