Viking World

A museum in Njarðvík home to the Viking ship Íslendingur and exhibitions on settlement and seafaring.

Viking World stands by the sea in Njarðvík, Reykjanesbær, and houses five exhibitions on Vikings, the settlement of Iceland and Norse seafaring. Its centrepiece is the Viking ship Íslendingur, an exact replica that sailed to America in the year 2000. In summer the museum adds a settlement zoo, a playground and an outdoor classroom.

A Viking ship by the sea in Reykjanesbær

Viking World in Njarðvík is a museum devoted to the Viking age and the settlement of Iceland. Its centrepiece is the Viking ship Íslendingur, an exact replica of a Norse longship that sailed all the way to America in the year 2000 to mark the millennium of Leif Eiríksson's voyages. Visitors can view the ship up close and learn about its building and journey. The museum stands by the sea with fine views, close to Keflavík Airport, making it a great stop at the start or end of a trip.

Five exhibitions and a family programme

Alongside the Viking ship, Viking World houses five exhibitions that explore Vikings, Norse seafaring and the settlement of Iceland in an accessible and educational way. In summer the site comes further to life when a settlement zoo, playground and outdoor classroom are added, delighting the youngest guests. This makes Viking World a varied destination for families who want to combine history and fun in a single visit on the Reykjanes peninsula.

Location and visiting

Viking World is in Njarðvík, Reykjanesbær, only a few minutes from Keflavík Airport and about a forty-minute drive from Reykjavík. On site there is a shop and a café, and the sea view adds to the experience. The museum works well as a first or last stop on a trip around the country, as it is easy to reach between the airport and other destinations on the Reykjanes peninsula.
