Whales of Iceland

Europe's largest whale exhibition, with life-size whale models at the Old Harbour.

Whales of Iceland is an exhibition at Reykjavík's Old Harbour where visitors walk among life-size whale models, including the blue whale and the sperm whale. The darkened hall uses ambient ocean sounds and multimedia to tell the story of the whales' lives. It suits all ages and is a short walk from the city centre.

Life-size whales under one roof

Whales of Iceland at Reykjavík's Old Harbour is Europe's largest whale exhibition. Visitors walk among life-size models, from the immense blue whale to the sperm whale and rarer species. The hall is kept dark and filled with the sounds of the ocean, creating the sense of moving along the seabed. It is a rare chance to grasp the true scale of animals that are usually only glimpsed far out at sea.

Learning and interaction for all ages

The exhibition includes a multilingual audio guide that covers each whale species in detail, along with touchscreens and stations where you can listen to whale song and learn about echolocation and migration. This makes Whales of Iceland both educational and entertaining for every age group. Because it is indoors, it is a reliable option regardless of the weather and fits easily into a city itinerary at any time of year.

Location and visiting

Whales of Iceland is located at Reykjavík's Old Harbour, about a fifteen-minute walk from the city centre along the waterfront, with parking on site for those who drive. Give yourself plenty of time to enjoy the exhibition and read about each species. The surrounding harbour district, with its cafés, restaurants and maritime atmosphere, makes the visit a pleasant part of a day out for families and whale enthusiasts alike.
