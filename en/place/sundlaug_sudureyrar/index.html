Suðureyri Swimming Pool

A cosy outdoor pool in a tiny fishing village on Súgandafjörður.

A small, welcoming outdoor pool in the heart of Suðureyri village on Súgandafjörður, run by the Ísafjarðarbær municipality. A local gathering spot and a great stop for travellers exploring the northern Westfjords.
