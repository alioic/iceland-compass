Pakkhús

A seafood restaurant in an old warehouse by the harbour in Höfn.

Pakkhús is a restaurant in a former warehouse overlooking the harbour in Höfn, often called Iceland's langoustine capital. It emphasises langoustine and fresh fish alongside other local dishes.

About

Pakkhús is a seafood restaurant in a former warehouse by the harbour in Höfn. The focus is on langoustine and fresh fish alongside other local dishes. The historic building gives the place real character, and the prized Hornafjörður langoustine shines in a warm, cosy setting right by the water.

Good to know

The restaurant is down by the old harbour in Höfn, often called Iceland's langoustine capital. Höfn sits on the southeast corner along the Ring Road, near Vatnajökull and Jökulsárlón. Booking a table is recommended in summer.
