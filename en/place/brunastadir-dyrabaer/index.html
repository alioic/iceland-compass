Brunastadir Petting Zoo

A family-run petting zoo in Fljot with many friendly farm animals.

At Brunastadir in the Fljot district, farmers run a welcoming petting zoo where visitors get close to Icelandic farm animals. Among them are goats, pigs, rabbits, chickens, turkeys, lambs, calves and horses. There is also a farm store selling homemade products and ice cream.

A petting zoo for the whole family

The Brunastadir petting zoo in the Fljot district is a real paradise for children who want to get close to animals. You can pet and feed goats, pigs, rabbits, lambs and other friendly farm animals in a beautiful rural setting. The family that runs the zoo welcomes guests warmly and creates a cosy atmosphere. It is a welcome activity on a trip through the north and is especially well suited to the youngest travellers.

Country charm in Fljot

Brunastadir sits in the scenic Fljot district, and a visit offers a genuine glimpse of Icelandic farm life. The on-site farm store sells homemade goat cheese and other produce straight from the farm. Visitors can also enjoy soft-serve ice cream, coffee and pastries and relax at their own pace. It is a pleasant, child-friendly stop that combines animals, food and rural calm.

Open through the summer

The Brunastadir petting zoo is open daily through the summer months and works well as a short stop on the route around the Trollaskagi peninsula. The animals are used to visitors, so it is easy for children to approach them safely. The surroundings are peaceful and the views are lovely, making the visit even more enjoyable. It is a good idea to check current opening hours on the farm's social media.
