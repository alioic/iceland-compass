Almar Bakari

A well-established bakery and café in Hveragerði with fresh pastries.

Almar Bakari is a bakery and café that makes bread and pastries using traditional Icelandic methods. Popular picks include rye bread, cinnamon rolls and kleinur, alongside coffee, light lunches and sandwiches.

About

Almar Bakari is a well-established bakery and café in Hveragerði that bakes bread and pastries using traditional Icelandic methods. It is a popular place to grab fresh rye bread, cinnamon rolls and kleinur alongside a good coffee. It also offers light lunch dishes and sandwiches. An ideal spot to start the day or pick up something for the road.

Good to know

Almar Bakari is in Hveragerði in South Iceland, close to Reykjavík and a convenient first stop as you head into the region. The bakery is ideal for breakfast or a quick coffee break. Prices are moderate. Worth keeping in mind as a relaxed option for fresh pastries before travelling on.
