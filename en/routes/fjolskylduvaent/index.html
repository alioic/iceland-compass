Route

Family-friendly

Places the kids will love

Iceland is wonderful for families, and here are places that charm children and adults alike: the erupting Strokkur geyser, Goðafoss waterfall, the Friðheimar tomato greenhouse with its horse show, the Secret Lagoon natural pool, swimming pools (such as Laugardalslaug with its water slides), whale watching in Húsavík, the Vestmannaeyjar volcanic islands with their puffins, and the Forest Lagoon baths. Easy access and something for everyone.

Places on this route

  • Friðheimar Dining inside a tomato greenhouse on the Golden Circle.
  • Geysir og Strokkur The original erupting hot spring — Strokkur erupts every few minutes.
  • Goðafoss The waterfall of the gods, where the pagan gods were cast aside around the year 1000.
  • Gamla laugin (Flúðir) The oldest swimming pool in the country, a natural geothermal pool in Flúðir.
  • Laugardalslaug Iceland's largest swimming complex in the Laugardalur valley, packed with hot pots and slides for the whole family.
  • Húsavík Europe's whale-watching capital with sea baths on the cliff edge.
  • Vestmannaeyjar A volcanic archipelago with a huge puffin colony and the volcano Eldfell.
  • Skógarböðin (Forest Lagoon) Geothermal baths tucked into a birch forest above Eyjafjörður.
