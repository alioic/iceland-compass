Borg Swimming Pool

The Borg sports centre in Grímsnes with a pool, slide and steam bath.

Borg's pool is part of the Grímsnes- og Grafningshreppur sports centre, with an outdoor pool, hot tubs, a children's pool, a steam bath and a water slide. A popular stop near Kerið crater and the Golden Circle.
