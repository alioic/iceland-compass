Beljandi Brugghús

Micro-brewery and bar in a restored house in tiny Breiðdalsvík.

Beljandi is a small craft brewery started by two locals in a restored old house in Breiðdalsvík. The upstairs bar pours the brewery's own beers, from pale ale to porter, alongside light platters made with local ingredients. The bar is mainly open in the evenings through summer.

A brewery in a tiny seaside village

Beljandi Brugghús is a hallmark of Breiðdalsvík, a small but lively brewery in the heart of the village. Two locals set it up in a restored old house and brew craft beer using pure Icelandic water. The upstairs bar has a warm, rustic feel, with views over the brewing kettles and the sea and usually several beers on tap. For travellers in the East Fjords it is a fun stop to taste local produce and support a small countryside producer.

The beer and the food

Beljandi typically has several of its own beers on tap, from a light pale ale to darker porter and brown ale. Alongside the beer you can order light dishes and platters made with local ingredients, well suited to sharing. The focus is on quality and small-batch production rather than volume, and the atmosphere is relaxed. The bar is mainly open in the evenings during summer, so it is worth checking the opening hours before you visit.

Visiting Breiðdalsvík

Breiðdalsvík is a small village in the southern East Fjords, and Beljandi Brugghús gives it a special place on Iceland's beer map. It suits travellers on the ring road who want to pause, taste craft beer and meet the locals. Accommodation and services are nearby, and the nature of the Breiðdalur valley is close at hand. Beljandi is a good example of local produce where production, hospitality and a sense of place come together under one roof.
