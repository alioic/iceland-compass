Álftaneslaug

Home to Iceland's only wave pool, out on the Álftanes peninsula in Garðabær.

Álftaneslaug is best known for Iceland's only wave pool, where bathers of all ages ride gentle artificial waves. It also has a 25-metre outdoor pool, an indoor pool, two hot pots, a children's pool, a sauna and two water slides, the longer stretching about 85 metres.
