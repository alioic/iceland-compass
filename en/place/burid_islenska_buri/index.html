Búrið – The Icelandic Pantry

Grandi cheese shop stocking specialties from small Icelandic producers.

Búrið on Grandagarður is a cheese shop and pantry specialising in artisan cheeses, jams, chutneys and treats from small Icelandic farms and producers. Founded in 2008, it also runs informal cheese workshops over the summer months.

Cheese from Icelandic farms

Búrið at Grandagarður 35 is a small cheese shop that has earned a special place among cheese lovers. The shop specialises in artisan cheeses made from cow's, goat's and ewe's milk from small Icelandic farms, alongside organic skyr and other products straight from producers across the country. For travellers who want to taste and buy genuine Icelandic food from small makers, it is one of the most interesting stops in Reykjavík and a fine way to support local production.

More than cheese

Beyond cheese, Búrið sells jams, chutneys, olives, cured meats and all sorts of treats that pair well with cheese. The shop was founded in 2008 and focuses on goods from small producers that are often hard to find elsewhere. It is an ideal place to put together a gift basket or picnic of Icelandic local goods. The location in Grandi, a former fish-packing district that has become a food quarter, makes the visit all the more enjoyable.

Cheese workshops

Over the summer months Búrið runs informal cheese workshops where guests learn about cheese and taste the selection in a relaxed setting. The workshops suit both curious beginners and seasoned cheese enthusiasts and are a fun way to get to know Icelandic cheesemaking. For food lovers who want to deepen their knowledge and experience Icelandic food culture, it is a rare opportunity. Búrið thus combines shopping, learning and a passion for good food in one spot in the heart of Grandi.
