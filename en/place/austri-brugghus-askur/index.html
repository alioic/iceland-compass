Austri Brugghús / Askur Taproom

Craft brewery in Egilsstaðir with its own taproom bar.

Austri is a craft brewery in Egilsstaðir that names its beers after East Iceland landmarks. At the Askur taproom you can taste the beer straight from the brewer, including an IPA and a double IPA infused with locally grown wasabi. The taproom is small and homely, right in the centre of town.

A craft brewery in the Hérað district

Austri Brugghús is a craft brewery in Egilsstaðir, the hub of East Iceland, and stands out by naming its beers after local landmarks. Its India pale ale is named after the Steinketill peak, and a double IPA called Skessa is infused with wasabi grown in the region. For travellers keen to buy local produce, it is a good example of food craft that grows straight out of its surroundings and uses regional ingredients in an original way.

The Askur taproom

Austri's beer is served at the Askur taproom, a small, homely bar in central Egilsstaðir. Here you can sit down, taste the beer straight from the maker and see how a small brewery operates in rural Iceland. The atmosphere is relaxed and suits winding down after a day exploring the East Fjords. Because opening hours can change with the season, it is sensible to check them before planning a visit.

Egilsstaðir as a base

Egilsstaðir is the main service centre in East Iceland and a good base for exploring the fjords, the forest at Hallormsstaður and Lagarfljót. Austri Brugghús and the Askur taproom add to the local scene and give visitors a chance to taste craft beer from the region. The town has a range of accommodation, shops and restaurants within easy reach. For anyone collecting local produce on their travels, the brewery is a welcome addition to the East Iceland food and drink scene.
