Kaffi Krús

A well-established café in a charming older timber house in Selfoss.

Kaffi Krús on Austurvegur in Selfoss is a popular café in an older timber house with a garden. Known for home-baked cakes, soups and pizzas and a cosy atmosphere — a good stop on the way around South Iceland.

About

Kaffi Krús on Austurvegur in Selfoss is a long-established, popular café set in a lovely older timber house with a garden. It is known for home-baked cakes, soups and pizzas and a cosy, laid-back atmosphere. The place is a favourite stop for both locals and travellers passing through South Iceland, whether for coffee or a light meal.

Good to know

Kaffi Krús is centrally located in Selfoss, the largest town in South Iceland, making it a handy stop as you travel the region. The garden is pleasant on fair-weather days. The menu suits families well and prices are moderate. Worth keeping in mind as a relaxed option away from the busiest tourist sites.
