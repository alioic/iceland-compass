Sólgarðar í Fljótum

A country pool at Sólgarðar in Fljót, heated with water from a nearby borehole.

The pool at Sólgarðar in Fljót in Skagafjörður is a 16.6-metre pool heated with water from a nearby borehole. A new steam room has been added, and the pool is run on behalf of the municipality.

A swim in Fljót

The pool at Sólgarðar lies in Fljót in the northernmost part of Skagafjörður, in a beautiful and quiet area not far from Siglufjörður. The pool is 16.6 metres and heated with water from a nearby borehole, and a steam room has recently been added. Sólgarðar is a former school site in the countryside, and the pool serves both locals and travellers passing through the Tröllaskagi peninsula and northern Skagafjörður. For anyone driving between Siglufjörður and Sauðárkrókur, Sólgarðar is a cosy and little-known bathing spot.

A hidden country pool on Tröllaskagi

Sólgarðar in Fljót is an example of a small country pool offering a genuine Icelandic swimming experience away from the tourist trail. The pool is heated with locally sourced borehole water, and the new steam room makes a visit even nicer, including special women's hours in winter. The setting is dramatic, ringed by the mountains of Tröllaskagi and close to Miklavatn lake. Sólgarðar suits anyone wanting to stop for a swim while touring the Fljót district and enjoy the calm of a northern farming community.
