Bryggjan

Fisherman's harbour café in Grindavík known for its lobster soup.

Bryggjan is a café with a fisherman's atmosphere by the harbour in Grindavík. It is best known for its lobster soup, which has long been a favourite with visitors.

About

Bryggjan is a café with a genuine fisherman's feel down by the harbour in Grindavík. It is best known for its acclaimed langoustine soup, which has been hugely popular with guests over the years. The atmosphere is homely and warm, with a historic seaside character. A cosy place to warm up by the harbour over a bowl of soup and a coffee.

Good to know

Bryggjan is by the harbour in Grindavík on the Reykjanes peninsula. Note that conditions in Grindavík have been changeable due to volcanic activity in the area, so it is sensible to check opening and access in advance. Prices are moderate. The langoustine soup is the signature dish.
