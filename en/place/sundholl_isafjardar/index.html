Sundhöll Ísafjarðar

A cozy indoor pool in the heart of Ísafjörður, open since 1945.

Sundhöll Ísafjarðar is the town's oldest bathing facility, opened in 1945 and set close to the centre. It has a 16-metre indoor pool along with a geothermal hot tub, a cold plunge and a sauna. It's a classic slice of Icelandic pool culture in a warm, welcoming setting.
