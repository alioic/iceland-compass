Múlagljúfur

A hidden canyon with waterfalls and views toward Öræfajökull.

Múlagljúfur is a lush, deep canyon just off the Ring Road between Vík and Höfn that has become one of Iceland's most popular hidden gems in recent years. A trail climbs to viewpoints along the rim overlooking Hangandifoss, a waterfall dropping more than 100 metres, with Öræfajökull glacier as a backdrop. The access track is a short gravel road and the route is passable in summer.
