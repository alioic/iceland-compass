Bíldudalur Swimming Pool

Small village pool in the fishing hamlet of Bíldudalur on Arnarfjörður.

Bíldudalur Swimming Pool is a cosy outdoor pool with a hot tub in the centre of this small village on Arnarfjörður. It serves locals and travellers exploring the southern Westfjords.
