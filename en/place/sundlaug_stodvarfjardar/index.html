Stöðvarfjörður Swimming Pool

A small outdoor pool below the school in Stöðvarfjörður, open in summer.

Stöðvarfjörður's pool is a small outdoor pool with a hot tub on Skólabraut. Open only in summer, it is a quiet bathing spot in the fjord that is home to Petra's Stone Collection.
