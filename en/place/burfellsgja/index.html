Búrfellsgjá and Búrfell

A family-friendly walk along a dramatic lava channel from the Heiðmörk parking area up to the Búrfell crater.

Búrfellsgjá is a 3.5 km lava channel running from the Búrfell crater, which erupted more than 7,000 years ago. A well-cairned, mostly easy trail follows the channel from Heiðmörk between high lava walls and small caves up to the crater itself. It is a gentle, enjoyable hike close to the city that suits the whole family.
