Papey Island Boat Tours from Djúpivogur

Papey Island Boat Tours offer a memorable boat trip from Djúpivogur out to the island of Papey, known for its large puffin colony and abundant birdlife. On the trip you sail across the sound, walk around the island with a guide and see the old lighthouse and the island's history. Papey is one of the safest and most accessible places to see puffins up close, making the trip a popular experience for families. The tours run only through the summer months when birdlife is at its peak. It is a wonderful way to enjoy the nature and wildlife of the East Fjords.

Taking the trip

Papey Island tours depart from the harbour in Djúpivogur during the summer, and the trip takes a few hours, including the crossing and a walk around the island. It is wise to dress for the weather and bring warm clothing, as the sea breeze can be cool. As this is a boat trip, weather can affect departures, so it is sensible to book in advance and confirm on the day. Djúpivogur is a pretty, quiet village on the southern East Fjords and an ideal starting point for the trip. Papey is a real gem for birdwatchers and families alike.

Puffins and birdlife on Papey

Papey is one of the few islands off East Iceland where you can go ashore and experience a puffin colony up close. In summer the island teems with life as puffins, eider ducks and other species nest and tend their young. For children it is an unforgettable experience to see puffins in flight and by their burrows, while guides share knowledge about life on the island. Beyond the birdlife, Papey has a rich history, including the old lighthouse and traces of former habitation. A trip to Papey combines nature, history and the outdoors in a unique way.
