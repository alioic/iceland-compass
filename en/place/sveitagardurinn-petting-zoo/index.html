Sveitagarðurinn

A petting zoo and play park in Grímsnes where children can get close to farm animals.

Sveitagarðurinn is a petting zoo and play park in Grímsnes, close to Selfoss and the Golden Circle. It is home to horses, calves, pigs, sheep, goats, chickens, ducks, rabbits and kittens that children can get close to and get to know. The site also has a play area, making it an enjoyable stop for families in summer.

Farm animals up close

Sveitagarðurinn in Grímsnes is a delightful petting zoo for families in South Iceland. It is home to a variety of farm animals, from horses, calves and pigs to sheep, goats, chickens and rabbits, that children can get close to and get to know. The focus is on safe, close contact where the youngest visitors learn to handle animals. Alongside the animals there is a play area that makes the visit varied. The zoo is a great addition to a Golden Circle trip and suits younger children especially well.

Play and animals together

What makes Sveitagarðurinn special is how well it combines animals and play in one place. Children can both pet the farm animals and let off steam in the play park, making the visit a perfect day out for families. The setting is rural and calm, far from the bustle, and gives a real sense of Icelandic country life. For many children it is a rare chance to touch live animals directly and experience the countryside in a hands-on way.

Visiting Grímsnes

Sveitagarðurinn is in Grímsnes, about fifteen kilometres from Selfoss and close to the Golden Circle, making it easy to visit while exploring the region. The zoo is open through the summer and works well as an addition to other nearby attractions. It is worth checking opening hours before you set off. For families with young children, Sveitagarðurinn is ideal for combining animals, play and the outdoors in a calm, child-friendly setting in South Iceland.
