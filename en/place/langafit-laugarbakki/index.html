Langafit Handcraft Market

A handcraft shop and coffee house in the village of Laugarbakki by Miðfjörður.

In the village of Laugarbakki by Miðfjörður, the Langafit shop sells handcraft made by local villagers and nearby farms. There is also a coffee house, open through the summer months beside the campsite. A greenhouse nearby sells strawberries and vegetables.

Handcraft at Laugarbakki

Langafit is a handcraft shop in the small village of Laugarbakki by Miðfjörður in Northwest Iceland. It offers handcraft made by villagers and people from nearby farms, from knitwear to woodwork. The shop is run in connection with the local campsite and community centre and is open through the summer months. For travellers on the Ring Road it is a convenient and personal stop.

Country market and coffee house

At Langafit there is also a coffee house where guests can sit down with a cup of coffee and enjoy the homely atmosphere. The handcraft market reflects the creativity of local people and offers a chance to buy items found nowhere else. Nearby, a greenhouse sells strawberries, vegetables and other fresh produce straight to visitors. Together these places make a pleasant stop for a short break on the journey.

A stop in Miðfjörður

Laugarbakki lies just off the Ring Road and is a handy stop for those travelling through Húnaþing vestra. The Langafit shop makes it easy to support locals directly and take home a souvenir with a genuine connection to the region. Nearby you can also visit Hvammstangi and the Icelandic Seal Centre. Opening hours follow the summer season, so it is best to plan a visit outside deep winter.
