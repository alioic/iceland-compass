The Christmas Garden

Year-round Christmas cheer in a gingerbread house just south of Akureyri.

The Christmas Garden is a delightful destination where Christmas reigns all year round. The red house resembles a gingerbread house, and inside you will find Christmas decorations from around the world alongside Icelandic Christmas items and handicrafts. A pretty garden surrounds the house, with benches and a tower that holds a giant Advent calendar.

Christmas all year at the Christmas Garden

The Christmas Garden in Eyjafjardarsveit is a magical destination where the Christmas spirit reigns every single day of the year. The red house resembles a gingerbread house, and inside you can browse a wide range of decorations from many countries alongside Icelandic handicrafts. Children and adults alike are charmed by the colourful surroundings and warm atmosphere. The garden is only about a ten-minute drive from downtown Akureyri, making it easy to drop by.

A pretty garden to enjoy

The Christmas Garden is surrounded by a tidy garden with benches and tables, perfect for sitting down and enjoying the moment. Among its features is a tower housing a giant Advent calendar and other charming details to discover. It is a pleasant stop for families travelling through Eyjafjordur, and bringing a picnic is a lovely idea. The setting is calm and beautiful in any weather.

Shopping and handicrafts

At the Christmas Garden you can also shop for decorations, gifts and Icelandic handicrafts, turning the visit into an enjoyable shopping trip. The selection is varied and ideal for finding special souvenirs or Christmas presents. The garden is open all year, making it a convenient stop regardless of the season. It is a unique and child-friendly activity near Akureyri.
