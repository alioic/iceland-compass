Birkimelur

A lovely little pool and natural hot pot on Barðaströnd overlooking Breiðafjörður.

At Birkimelur on Barðaströnd there is a small concrete pool alongside a natural hot pot closer to the shore. The view sweeps across Breiðafjörður and, on clear days, all the way to the Snæfellsjökull glacier. The local youth club runs the pool and there is a donation box by the changing rooms.
