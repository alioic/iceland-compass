Aðalbakarí

A bakery and café on Aðalgata in Siglufjörður, open year-round.

Aðalbakarí is located on Aðalgata in central Siglufjörður and is open all year round. It offers freshly baked bread, pastries and coffee, ideal for breakfast or a break while strolling around town.

About

Aðalbakarí is on Aðalgata in central Siglufjörður and is open all year round. It offers freshly baked bread, pastries and coffee, ideal for breakfast or a bite while walking around town. A cosy neighbourhood bakery where you can grab something fresh to go or settle in with a cup of coffee.

Good to know

Located on Aðalgata in central Siglufjörður, within walking distance of the harbour and the main landmarks. Open year-round, which makes it a reliable option at any time. It is worth stopping by early in the day when the fresh selection is at its best.
