The Brothers Brewery

A Westman Islands brewery brewing beer from local ingredients, including dulse picked on the islands.

The Brothers Brewery began in 2012 as a kitchen experiment by two friends and their brothers and today is a brewery with its own tavern in Vestmannaeyjar. It brews craft beer from carefully chosen ingredients, including its flagship Eldfell Volcano Red Ale, which uses dulse foraged on the islands. The tavern is a lively gathering place with live music and events.

Beer from the Westman Islands

The Brothers Brewery is a true island brewery, the only one in Vestmannaeyjar. It began as a kitchen experiment in 2012 and now occupies a spacious building with its own tavern. The beer is brewed from local ingredients and some brews even use dulse foraged on the islands' shores. For visitors taking the Herjólfur ferry across, it is one of the most enjoyable pieces of local produce available.

Eldfell and more

The brewery's flagship is Eldfell Volcano Red Ale, named after the volcano that erupted in 1973, made with island dulse. The range spans many brews that reflect the creativity of the brewers. The tavern often hosts live music and events, making it a hub of island social life. Here you can taste, chat and take home beer found nowhere else.

A trip to the islands

Vestmannaeyjar is an adventurous destination, and The Brothers Brewery is a natural part of the visit. The tavern is a lively meeting place for both locals and travellers in the heart of town. Check the opening hours before you sail over on the Herjólfur ferry and enjoy supporting a small family-and-friends brewery that grew from a kitchen into one of the islands' landmarks.
