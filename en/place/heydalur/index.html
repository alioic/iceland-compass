Heydalur

A small pool and hot tubs at the Heydalur country hotel in Mjóifjörður.

At Heydalur in Mjóifjörður by Ísafjarðardjúp there is a small pool and hot tub inside a greenhouse, plus three outdoor hot tubs built from rock and shell sand. A natural hot spring at Galtahrygg lies within walking distance.

A bathing haven in Mjóifjörður

Heydalur is a country hotel and travel service at the head of Mjóifjörður on the southern side of Ísafjarðardjúp in the Westfjords. It has a small pool and hot tub inside a greenhouse, making a soak pleasant in any weather, and outside there are three hot tubs built from rock and shell sand. A natural hot spring at Galtahrygg lies within walking distance. The surroundings are pristine and quiet, ideal for relaxing after kayaking, hiking or fishing nearby. Heydalur is a distinctive addition to Westfjords bathing culture.

Unwind after an adventure

Heydalur sits away from the main road, about 12 km off Route 61, making it a peaceful destination among mountains and fjord. The hot tubs and greenhouse pool are perfect for resting tired limbs after a kayak trip on Mjóifjörður, a horse ride or a hike. Note that the pool and tubs are primarily part of the travel service, so it is best to contact them in advance about access and opening hours. For anyone touring Ísafjarðardjúp, Heydalur is an essential rest stop.
