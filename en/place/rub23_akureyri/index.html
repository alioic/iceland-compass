Rub23

A well-known restaurant in the heart of Akureyri focused on fresh fish and Icelandic-Japanese cuisine.

Rub23 is one of North Iceland's best-known restaurants, built around fresh ingredients sourced from local fishermen and farmers. The menu spans sushi and seafood dishes alongside Icelandic culinary traditions.

About

Rub23 is one of North Iceland's best-known restaurants, in the heart of Akureyri. The focus is on fresh ingredients from local fishermen and farmers, with a menu that spans sushi and fish dishes blended with Icelandic culinary tradition. A polished, upmarket spot, ideal for anyone wanting to treat themselves to Japanese-Icelandic cooking.

Good to know

Located in central Akureyri, within walking distance of accommodation, shops and other sights. Well suited to dinner and special occasions. As it is a popular place, booking a table is a good idea, especially in summer and at weekends.
