Eimverk / Flóki Distillery

A family distillery growing its own barley to make Flóki whisky and other Icelandic spirits.

Eimverk is a family-run distillery producing Flóki whisky, gin and brennivín from 100% Icelandic barley. The barley is grown at Bjálmholt farm near Hella in South Iceland, where tastings and tours are also offered. It was Iceland's first whisky distillery and the spirits are made from grain to glass.

Icelandic whisky from home-grown barley

Eimverk is Iceland's first whisky distillery and is unusual for using only Icelandic barley. The barley is grown at Bjálmholt farm in South Iceland, where the family has built facilities to welcome guests. Flóki whisky emerged after years of experimentation and is made from grain to glass. For lovers of local produce it is a rare chance to get acquainted with Iceland's distilling tradition.

Flóki, gin and brennivín

Alongside Flóki whisky, Eimverk makes gin and brennivín from the same Icelandic barley, each with character drawn from Icelandic nature. The spirits have earned attention far beyond Iceland yet remain genuine local produce, grown and distilled in the south. Guests can taste the range, learn about the production and take home a bottle that reflects both the land and the craft behind it.

Visiting Bjálmholt

Bjálmholt lies just outside Hella, making the distillery an easy addition to a South Iceland road trip. It offers tastings and tours explaining the barley cultivation and the journey from grain to glass. Booking ahead and checking Eimverk's website is recommended. The visit is a welcome addition for anyone who wants to support small Icelandic producers.
