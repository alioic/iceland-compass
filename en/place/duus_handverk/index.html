Duus Handverk

Craft shop where twenty Reykjanes artisans sell under one roof.

Duus Handverk on Hafnargata in Reykjanesbær is a cosy gift boutique where around twenty artisans from the Reykjanes Peninsula sell their work together: wool sweaters, textiles, glass art, ceramics, jewellery and paintings. A small café rounds out the visit.

Crafts from Reykjanes

Duus Handverk at Hafnargata 62 in Reykjanesbær is a cosy and colourful gift boutique where around twenty artisans from the Reykjanes Peninsula have joined together under one roof. Here you find a varied range of quality craft, from Icelandic wool sweaters and textiles to glass art, ceramics, jewellery, paintings and other artworks. For travellers passing through Reykjanes, whether heading to or from Keflavík Airport, it is an ideal place to buy genuine local goods straight from the people who make them.

Many makers, one place

What makes Duus Handverk special is that twenty artisans sell together in one place, so the selection is broad and varied yet all locally made. The goods change as the makers create new work, so every visit is a little different. It gives guests a chance to discover the diversity of craft on the Reykjanes Peninsula and to find something unique not sold elsewhere. The shop is open daily and visits outside opening hours can be arranged on request.

Coffee and waffles

The shop also has a small café where guests can sit down for waffles with cream, chocolate and homemade jam, along with coffee, tea and hot cocoa. This makes Duus Handverk a pleasant place to pause, browse the crafts and enjoy a moment. For travellers who want to experience Reykjanes culture while supporting locals, it is one of the most enjoyable stops in Reykjanesbær and an easy detour near the airport.
