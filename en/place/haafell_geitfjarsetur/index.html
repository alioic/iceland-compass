Háafell Goat Farm

A goat farm in Borgarfjörður where visitors get to know the Icelandic goat.

Háafell Goat Farm in Borgarfjörður is a working farm dedicated to preserving the Icelandic goat breed that opens its doors to visitors in summer. Friendly, people-loving goats greet guests, who learn about the Icelandic goat and the story of the farm. The on-site shop sells goat products, soaps, creams and handicrafts.

The Icelandic goat in Borgarfjörður

Háafell Goat Farm is a working farm in Borgarfjörður in West Iceland that has for years worked to preserve the Icelandic goat breed, a rare animal with a long history in the country. Since 2012 the farm has welcomed visitors, who get to meet the goats up close and learn about their story and the importance of the conservation work. The goats are friendly and curious and greet guests warmly, making the visit a heartwarming and memorable experience for children and adults.

A countryside experience for families

A visit to Háafell is a unique chance to experience Icelandic country life and get close to the animals in a calm and personal way. There is also a small shop selling goat-related products, including soaps, creams, leather and handicrafts made on the farm. This makes the visit an enjoyable stop for families travelling through Borgarfjörður who want to support the preservation of the Icelandic goat breed.

Opening hours and visiting

Háafell Goat Farm is open to visitors during the summer months, generally from early June to the end of August, and also by arrangement outside that period. The farm lies in Borgarfjörður, near Reykholt and Húsafell, so it fits well into a tour of the historic and natural highlights of West Iceland. It is best to get in touch before visiting to confirm opening hours, as this is a working farm where daily life sets the pace.
