Garðskagi

Two lighthouses at the peninsula's northernmost tip, a paradise for birdwatchers.

At Garðskagi stand two lighthouses and a small folk museum. The area is among the country's best birdwatching spots and a wonderful place to watch the sunset and even whales off the shore.

Garðskagi – Lighthouses and a Birdwatcher's Paradise on Reykjanes

Garðskagi stands on the northernmost tip of the Reykjanes Peninsula beside the village of Garður, known for its two lighthouses – one old, one new – that define the landscape. The area is among Iceland's finest birdwatching spots and celebrated for spectacular sunsets over the ocean. Garðskagi is also home to a small folk museum preserving the history of fishing and daily life in the Suðurnes region. Just minutes from Keflavík Airport and Reykjanesbær, Garðskagi makes a peaceful, uncrowded stop on any tour of the Reykjanes Peninsula.

What to See and Do at Garðskagi

At Garðskagi, visitors can walk out to both lighthouses, explore the folk museum and watch a rich variety of birds – both breeding species and migrants that pause here on their journeys. The shoreline offers a gentle walk with ocean views, where whales and seals are sometimes spotted. Garðskagi's sunsets are especially popular with photographers. Sea angling is available nearby, along with plenty of quiet and calm. The site is ideal for birdwatching, photography and a relaxed encounter with the nature of Reykjanes.

How to Get to Garðskagi on the Reykjanes Peninsula

Garðskagi lies on the northern tip of the Reykjanes Peninsula, next to the village of Garður, just a 10-minute drive from Keflavík Airport and Reykjanesbær and about 45 minutes from Reykjavík. Follow Garðskagavegur road all the way to the point, where a car park sits beside the lighthouses. The road is paved and accessible year-round. Garðskagi works well as a first or last stop on an Iceland trip thanks to its closeness to the airport, and connects easily with other Reykjanes sights such as Brimketill, Gunnuhver and the Blue Lagoon.

Best Time to Visit Garðskagi and Practical Tips

Garðskagi is open all year and free to visit. Spring and autumn are best for birdwatching as migrants pass through, while summer evenings bring long, beautiful sunsets under the midnight sun. In winter you may catch the northern lights above the lighthouses. Dress in warm, windproof and waterproof layers, as the site is fully exposed to sea winds. Bring binoculars for birds and whale spotting. Garðskagi is a perfect place to begin or end a Reykjanes trip in peace and quiet, close to Keflavík Airport.
