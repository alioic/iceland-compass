Sandholt

A long-established family bakery on Laugavegur, running since 1920.

Sandholt is a fourth-generation artisan bakery on Laugavegur, known for sourdough bread, croissants, pastries and a fine breakfast and lunch. A popular stop in the city centre.

About

Sandholt is a long-established artisan bakery on Laugavegur, family-run in its fourth generation and going since 1920. It is known for sourdough bread, croissants, fine pastries and a careful breakfast and lunch menu. The coffee is good too, and everything is baked with real craft. A warm, popular stop for a taste of Icelandic baking tradition.

Good to know

The bakery is on Laugavegur, the main shopping street downtown, so it is easy to drop in while walking around. It is a great choice for breakfast or a light lunch and is often lively in the mornings. Come early for the freshest pastries and a better shot at a seat during busy hours.
