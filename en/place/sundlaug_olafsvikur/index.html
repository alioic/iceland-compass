Sundlaug Ólafsvíkur

Snæfellsbær's pool in Ólafsvík beneath the Snæfellsjökull glacier.

Town pool on Ennisbraut with an outdoor pool, hot tubs, steam bath and a water slide. A good stop while circling Snæfellsjökull National Park.
