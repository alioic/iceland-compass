Akureyri Botanical Garden

A beautiful botanical garden in the heart of Akureyri with diverse plants.

The Akureyri Botanical Garden is one of the world's northernmost botanical gardens, showcasing a wide range of Icelandic and foreign plants. The garden is sheltered and well tended, with paths, flowerbeds and benches where you can sit and relax. Entry is free, and it is a popular resting spot for families in summer.

A botanical garden in the heart of Akureyri

The Akureyri Botanical Garden is a true gem and one of the northernmost botanical gardens in the world. Many Icelandic and foreign plants grow here in a sheltered, well-tended setting that delights the eye. The garden suits families who want to relax, wander the paths and enjoy the greenery. Admission is free, and its central location in town makes it an easy stop.

Free outdoor time for families

The Botanical Garden is perfect for a gentle walk, letting children explore colourful flowerbeds and diverse plants. In summer the garden is in full bloom, and benches offer a good rest on the journey. There is also a cafe where you can enjoy refreshments in beautiful surroundings. It is a relaxing, green stop in the middle of a city visit.

Plants and learning

At the Akureyri Botanical Garden you can learn about Icelandic flora and see plants from around the world thriving at northern latitudes. The garden is well labelled and gives a good sense of the diversity of the plant world. It is open through the summer and is popular with both locals and travellers. A visit combines learning, beauty and pleasant time outdoors.
