Halldórskaffi

A cozy café and restaurant in Vík's historic Brydebúð building.

Halldórskaffi occupies the historic Brydebúð building in Vík and serves Icelandic dishes made with local ingredients. The menu features lamb, Arctic char, fish and wood-fired oven pizzas.

About

Halldórskaffi is a cosy café and restaurant in the historic Brydebúð house in Vík. The focus is on Icelandic dishes made with local ingredients, including lamb, Arctic char and fish, along with stone-oven pizzas. The mood is warm and homely, and the place is popular with both locals and travellers along the south coast.

Good to know

Halldórskaffi is in the village of Vík í Mýrdal, a convenient stop along the south coast and close to Reynisfjara. The building itself, Brydebúð, carries historical value and adds character to the visit. It can get lively during the summer peak, so arriving early helps. Prices are moderate.
