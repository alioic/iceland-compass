Ísafjörður

The largest town in the Westfjords, encircled by steep mountains.

Ísafjörður is the capital of the Westfjords, home to some of the oldest houses in the country in Neðstikaupstaður, a lively cultural scene and a thriving music community. The town is the departure point for trips to Hornstrandir and around Jökulfirðir.

Ísafjörður – the capital of the Westfjords beneath steep mountains

Ísafjörður is the largest town in the Westfjords and the unofficial capital of this remote region. It sits on a narrow spit of land reaching into the fjord of the same name, surrounded by high, steep mountains that give it a distinctive and dramatic setting. Ísafjörður has a rich history as a trading and fishing centre and preserves some of the oldest houses in Iceland in the Neðstikaupstaður quarter. Today the town buzzes with a lively cultural scene, music festivals and a strong community. Ísafjörður is a natural hub for travellers exploring the Westfjords and a launching point for adventures to Hornstrandir and the Jökulfirðir fjords.

Things to see and do in Ísafjörður

In Ísafjörður it is a pleasure to wander the old town and explore the historic houses of Neðstikaupstaður, where the Westfjords Heritage Museum preserves the region's history. The town is known for its vibrant music and cultural life and its many festivals throughout the year. Above the town, Naustahvilft, the so-called Troll Seat, beckons hikers who want to climb up and enjoy spectacular views over the fjord. Kayaking on the calm fjord and boat trips to Hornstrandir are among the most popular activities. Cafés, restaurants and a cosy atmosphere make a stay here memorable.

Getting to Ísafjörður – location in the Westfjords

Ísafjörður lies at the heart of the northern Westfjords and is reached by both road and domestic flights from Reykjavík. The flight takes only about 40 minutes and is the quickest option, while driving through the Westfjords is a spectacular experience of fjords, mountain passes and tunnels, though it takes several hours. In winter, mountain roads can close due to snow, so flying is often the safest choice in that season. From Ísafjörður the main gems of the Westfjords are within easy reach, and the town makes an ideal base for exploring the whole region and heading out to Hornstrandir.

Best time to visit Ísafjörður and practical tips

Ísafjörður welcomes visitors all year, but summer, from June to August, is the liveliest season, with the longest daylight, active boat tours and numerous festivals. This is also when roads are passable and access to Hornstrandir is best. In winter the town is quiet and beautiful under snow, drawing ski enthusiasts, though mountain-road conditions require careful attention. Book accommodation and boat trips ahead during the peak season. Wear warm, windproof clothing year-round, and allow time to enjoy both the town and the stunning nature of the surrounding Westfjords.
