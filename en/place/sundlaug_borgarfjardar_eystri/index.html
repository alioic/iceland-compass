Borgarfjörður eystri Swimming Pool

A small geothermal pool in Bakkagerði with mountain and fjord views.

The pool at Borgarfjörður eystri sits by Fjarðarborg and is run by Múlaþing municipality. A perfect place to meet locals and warm up after hiking the Víknaslóðir trails in this remote, colourful fjord.
