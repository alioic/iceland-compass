Breiðdalsvík Swimming Pool

An oval outdoor pool from 2002 in Breiðdalsvík, open in summer.

Breiðdalsvík's pool is a small oval outdoor pool, 8x14 metres, with a hot tub. Built in 2002 and open from June to September, it is a cosy bathing spot in a quiet village.
