Holtsel (Holtsels-Hnoss)

A dairy farm in Eyjafjörður making homemade ice cream from its own milk.

At Holtsel in Eyjafjarðarsveit, about 20 km south of Akureyri, the family has made ice cream from their cows' milk since 2006. The Holtsels-Hnoss ice cream comes in dozens of flavours, including liquorice, blueberry and rhubarb. The farm has a shop that sells only farm-direct produce.

Homemade ice cream in Eyjafjörður

Holtsel is a dairy farm in Eyjafjarðarsveit, about 20 kilometres south of Akureyri, where the family has made ice cream from their own milk since 2006. The ice cream, called Holtsels-Hnoss, is made from high-quality ingredients and the milk of the farm's cows. The flavours number in the dozens, ranging from liquorice and blueberry to rhubarb from the garden. It is a popular destination for locals and travellers in Northeast Iceland.

A farm-direct shop

At Holtsel there is a shop that sells only produce straight from the farm, the first of its kind in the country. There you can buy the ice cream and other local goods right beside the production itself. Buying farm-direct supports local people and lets travellers taste fresh products that originate on site. The visit is an enjoyable glimpse into daily life on an Icelandic dairy farm.

A family-friendly stop

Holtsel suits families travelling around Eyjafjörður who want to stop in a calm rural setting. The ice cream is the draw, but there is also a chance to learn about dairy farming and see where the produce comes from. The farm is a short distance from Akureyri and so an easy detour for those staying in town. The range of flavours ensures everyone finds something to their liking.
