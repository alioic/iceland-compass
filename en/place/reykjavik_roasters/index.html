Reykjavík Roasters

A popular specialty roastery with a cosy atmosphere and a record player.

Reykjavík Roasters roasts its own beans and is a favourite among coffee lovers. A relaxed atmosphere with vinyl records, carefully brewed coffee and home-baked treats in the heart of the city.

About

Reykjavík Roasters roasts its own coffee and is a favourite among coffee lovers. The atmosphere is laid-back, with vinyl records spinning, carefully crafted brews and home-baked treats. Quality goes into every cup, whether espresso or hand-poured filter. A cosy place that is something of a paradise for coffee enthusiasts.

Good to know

The café is in central Reykjavík and often buzzing, so seats can fill up at peak times. Ideal for a slow coffee break, a morning cup, or picking up quality beans to take home. Relaxed and homely, with a warm record-shop mood.
