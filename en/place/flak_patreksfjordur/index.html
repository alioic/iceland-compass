About

Flak is a culture-minded pub and fish-soup spot by the harbour in Patreksfjörður. It serves light meals and home-brewed beer, and often has concerts or other events on the programme. The atmosphere is relaxed and lively, and the place is an enjoyable blend of restaurant, pub and cultural venue in a small fishing town.

Good to know

Flak sits by the harbour in Patreksfjörður, the largest town in the southern Westfjords. The fish soup and home-brewed beer are house signatures. It is worth checking the venue's programme, as events and concerts shape the mood. It is an ideal spot for an evening while travelling around the Westfjords.
