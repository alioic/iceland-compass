Franska kaffihúsið

A tiny countryside café overlooking Rauðasandur beach.

Franska kaffihúsið (the French Café) sits by the famous red-sand beach of Rauðasandur in a remote rural district south of Patreksfjörður. It serves waffles, soup, cakes and hot and cold drinks in a peaceful setting with views over the beach.

About

Franska kaffihúsið sits by the famous Rauðisandur beach in a remote area south of Patreksfjörður. It serves waffles, soup, cakes and hot and cold drinks in a peaceful setting with views over the shore. This little country café is a welcome shelter and resting point for those who make the journey to this remarkable place.

Good to know

Rauðisandur is known for its long, golden-to-red sand beach and remote surroundings. The road there is gravel and fairly winding, so it is worth allowing extra time. The café is mainly open during the summer season and makes a good stop after a walk along the beach.
