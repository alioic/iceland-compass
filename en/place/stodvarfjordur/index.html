Stöðvarfjörður

Petra's world-famous private stone collection in a small East Fjords village.

In Stöðvarfjörður you'll find Petra's Stone Collection, one of the largest private mineral collections in the world, gathered by Petra Sveinsdóttir over her lifetime. The garden and house are full of colourful crystals, jasper and zeolites from the East Fjords mountains.

Stöðvarfjörður and Petra's Stone Collection in the East Fjords

Stöðvarfjörður is a small, tranquil fishing village in the East Fjords of Iceland, best known for Petra's Stone Collection (Steinasafn Petru), one of the largest private mineral collections in the world. Over her lifetime, Petra Sveinsdóttir gathered colourful crystals, jasper, zeolites and other stones from the surrounding mountains, and today her house and garden draw visitors from around the globe. Framed by steep peaks, the fjord offers authentic East Iceland calm, where nature, local history and geology meet in a memorable stop along the region's coastal route.

Things to see and do in Stöðvarfjörður

The main attraction in Stöðvarfjörður is Petra's Stone Collection, where visitors wander through a house and garden brimming with vivid stones and crystals gathered from the East Fjord mountains. The village's old church now houses a cultural centre with exhibitions and creative projects, and along the shore you can watch the Saxa sea geyser surge up through a rock fissure. Beach walks, gentle hillside strolls and photography round out a visit. This little fjord is ideal for travellers who want a slower pace and a hands-on connection to East Iceland's geology.

How to get to Stöðvarfjörður

Stöðvarfjörður lies on the southern East Fjords along Route 1, the Ring Road, between Breiðdalsvík and Fáskrúðsfjörður. From the regional hub of Egilsstaðir it is a scenic drive south into the fjords, and many travellers fold a stop here into a wider East Iceland journey to or from Höfn. The route follows beautiful coastal roads with sweeping views of fjords and mountains. Ring Road travellers can easily pause in Stöðvarfjörður as part of the string of East Fjord villages between Djúpivogur to the south and Reyðarfjörður to the north.

Best time to visit Stöðvarfjörður and practical tips

The summer months of May to September are the best time to visit Stöðvarfjörður, when Petra's Stone Collection is open, the roads are clear and daylight is long. Summer brings the mildest weather and the most accessible walking around the fjord. In winter, snow and ice can make driving through the East Fjords challenging, so check road conditions and the forecast before setting out. Allow plenty of time for the collection, bring warm layers and sturdy shoes, and take the chance to soak up the relaxed atmosphere of this quiet East Iceland village.
