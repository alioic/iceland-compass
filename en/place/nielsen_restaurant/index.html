About

Nielsen Restaurant occupies the oldest house in Egilsstaðir and focuses on local, seasonal ingredients from East Iceland. The menu follows the seasons and whatever is freshest at the time. The historic building and warm mood make it one of the region's leading places to eat, ideal for an unhurried evening.

Good to know

The restaurant is in central Egilsstaðir, the largest town in East Iceland and a good base for exploring the fjords, Hallormsstaður forest and Lagarfljót. Reserving a table is a good idea, especially in the summer months.
