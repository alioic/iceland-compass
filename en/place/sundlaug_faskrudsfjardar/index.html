Fáskrúðsfjörður Swimming Pool

A small indoor pool in the French-heritage town of Fáskrúðsfjörður.

Fáskrúðsfjörður's pool is a 12.5-metre indoor pool with an outdoor hot tub. Open through the winter, it makes a cosy stop in this historic French fishing town.
