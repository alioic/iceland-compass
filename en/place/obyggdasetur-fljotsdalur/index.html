The Wilderness Center

A culture and experience centre on the edge of the highlands.

The Wilderness Center lies in Norðurdalur in Fljótsdalur, on the edge of the largest uninhabited area in Northern Europe. It houses a living exhibition on the history of highland travel and the struggle for survival at the edge of the wilderness, along with accommodation, dining and outdoor areas for guests. Horse riding and other experiences are offered in dramatic surroundings.

The Wilderness Center

The Wilderness Center sits at the head of Fljótsdalur, at the boundary between settlement and the vast uninhabited highlands of East Iceland. It houses a living exhibition telling the story of highland travel, mountaineering and the struggle for survival at the edge of the wilderness. The exhibition is presented in a visual and hands-on way that appeals to children and adults alike. The setting is dramatic, with glaciers, rivers and mountains in every direction, and guests can add horse riding and outdoor activities to their visit. It is a unique destination for anyone seeking the calm and scale of the Icelandic wilderness.

Visiting the center

The Wilderness Center is in Norðurdalur in Fljótsdalur, about an hour's drive from Egilsstaðir, including a gravel road for the final stretch. It is open through the summer and offers an exhibition, dining and accommodation in a historic setting. As the place is remote, it is worth allowing plenty of time and preparing for the drive. You can combine the visit with other sights in Fljótsdalur, such as Skriðuklaustur and Snæfellsstofa. It is advisable to confirm opening and book experiences in advance, especially horse rides and meals.

An experience for the whole family

The Wilderness Center combines history, nature and the outdoors in one place, making it ideal for families seeking something special in East Iceland. Children can touch and try many things in the exhibition, which brings history to life, and the outdoor area offers space to move around. Guided horse rides suit beginners and experienced riders alike. The setting, far from the bustle of modern life, offers a peace and quiet that is hard to find elsewhere. A visit to the Wilderness Center makes a memorable addition to a journey through the eastern valleys.
