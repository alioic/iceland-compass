Langabúð

A café in the oldest building in Djúpivogur that doubles as a cultural centre.

Langabúð is a café in one of Djúpivogur's oldest buildings, which also houses museums and exhibitions. It serves homemade soups, bread with salmon and smoked lamb, coffee and cakes, including vegan and gluten-free options.

About

Langabúð is a café in one of the oldest houses in Djúpivogur that also hosts museums and exhibitions. It offers homemade soups, bread with salmon and smoked lamb, coffee and cakes, including vegan and gluten-free options. It's a cosy cultural house where history and hospitality meet in a warm setting.

Good to know

The house stands by the harbour in Djúpivogur, a quiet Cittaslow town in East Iceland. Besides the café, you can explore the museums and exhibitions on site. It's a lovely stop for soup and a slice of cake while touring the fjords.
