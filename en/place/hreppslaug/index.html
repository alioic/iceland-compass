Hreppslaug

A historic outdoor pool in Andakíll from 1928, heated by water from the hillside above.

Hreppslaug is a historic outdoor pool in Andakíll in Borgarfjörður, built by locals in 1928. It has two hot tubs, a sauna, a cold tub and an outdoor shower, and is run by the local youth club Íslendingur.

One of Borgarfjörður's oldest pools

Hreppslaug in Andakíll was built in 1928 and is among the oldest outdoor pools in West Iceland. It is heated by water from streams and springs on the hillside above and has kept its historic charm. Alongside the pool are two hot tubs, a sauna, a cold tub and an outdoor shower, where guests can relax in the beautiful Borgarfjörður countryside. The local youth club Íslendingur runs the pool, which opens through summer. Hreppslaug is ideal for anyone wanting an old-fashioned, homely pool away from the crowds.

A calm country bathing spot

Hreppslaug is a hidden gem in Andakíll, a short distance from Skorradalur and Hvanneyri in Borgarfjörður. The outdoor pool, hot tubs and sauna make it a fine stop on a West Iceland road trip. It opens mainly in summer, daily except Mondays in high season, and at weekends into the autumn, so check the schedule before visiting. The setting is simple and welcoming, suiting families and travellers who want a swim off the beaten track.
