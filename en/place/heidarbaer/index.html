Heiðarbær

A small, cosy country pool in Þingvallasveit with a single hot tub.

The pool at Heiðarbær in Þingvallasveit is a small, cosy outdoor pool with one hot tub and a sunbathing area. There is also a campsite and food for sale on site, and the pool is open in summer.

A swim by Þingvallavatn

The pool at Heiðarbær is a small, cosy outdoor pool in Þingvallasveit, a short distance from lake Þingvallavatn. It has one hot tub and a good sunbathing area, and there is also a campsite and food for sale on site. The pool is open in summer and suits travellers visiting Þingvellir and the surrounding area. Heiðarbær is a welcome and little-known bathing spot for anyone wanting a swim in a calm country setting while touring the Golden Circle or hiking around the Þingvellir area.

A quiet bathing spot in historic surroundings

Heiðarbær in Þingvallasveit is an example of a small country pool offering peace and quiet away from the crowds. The pool, hot tub and campsite make it a comfortable stop for families and travellers in South Iceland. The surroundings are historic, near the Þingvellir national park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Note that opening hours are limited to summer, so check the schedule before visiting. Heiðarbær is ideal for anyone wanting to experience simple, authentic Icelandic swimming culture.
