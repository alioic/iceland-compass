Vatnaveröld

A lively family waterworld in downtown Keflavík with an indoor pool and slides.

Vatnaveröld is Reykjanesbær's swimming centre, popular with locals and travellers alike. It has a 50-metre indoor pool, a 25-metre outdoor pool, a children's pool, four hot pots, a steam room and water slides, all heated by geothermal water from the Reykjanes peninsula.
