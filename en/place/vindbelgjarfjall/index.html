Vindbelgjarfjall

A short but steep climb to the best viewpoint over Mývatn.

Vindbelgjarfjall rises west of Mývatn and from its summit (529 m) is probably the best view over the lake, the pseudo-craters and the surrounding mountains. The walk is short but fairly steep for the last stretch - ideal for gaining an overview of the Mývatn area.

Vindbelgjarfjall — the best viewpoint over Lake Mývatn

Vindbelgjarfjall rises on the western side of Lake Mývatn in North Iceland, and from its summit lies arguably the finest view over the entire Mývatn area. The mountain is 529 metres high and the climb is short but fairly steep near the top, ideal for anyone wanting a quick overview of the lake, its pseudo-craters and the surrounding ring of mountains. Vindbelgjarfjall is one of the hidden gems of the Mývatn region and perfect for understanding the landscape of this remarkable volcanic area. For hikers and photographers, this peak is an essential addition to a visit to Lake Mývatn.

The Vindbelgjarfjall hike and views over the pseudo-craters

The hike up Vindbelgjarfjall is about three kilometres there and back and takes only one and a half to two hours, so it suits most visitors even though the final stretch is steep. From the summit Lake Mývatn spreads out in all its glory, with countless pseudo-craters, islets and colourful mountains ringing the water. Rich birdlife thrives at the foot of the mountain, as Mývatn is famous for its exceptional avifauna. The view over the pseudo-craters and the mountain ring makes this short walk one of the most rewarding in the whole Mývatn area, especially in the evening light.

Getting to Vindbelgjarfjall — location by Lake Mývatn

Vindbelgjarfjall stands on the western side of Lake Mývatn, close to the Ring Road and to the area's other natural attractions such as Dimmuborgir, Hverfjall and the Skútustaðir pseudo-craters. You drive to the trailhead west of the lake, from where a short walk leads to the foot of the mountain. Its location makes Vindbelgjarfjall an easily reached stop on a Mývatn trip, whether you arrive from Akureyri, Goðafoss or Húsavík. The mountain is part of the dense network of points of interest around Mývatn, and the hike pairs well with a visit to the Mývatn Nature Baths or other local gems.

Best time to hike Vindbelgjarfjall and practical tips

Summer and early autumn are the best times to climb Vindbelgjarfjall, when the trail is dry and the views over Mývatn are clearest. Evening light and a low sun create especially beautiful conditions for photographing the pseudo-craters. Bring water, windproof clothing and good hiking shoes, as the final stretch is steep and loose underfoot. In summer the midges around Mývatn can be persistent, so a head net or insect repellent is useful. Watch the weather, take care on the steep section, and enjoy the extraordinary view over one of Iceland's most distinctive natural areas.
