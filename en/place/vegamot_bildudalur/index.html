Vegamót

A café and shop in the heart of Bíldudalur.

Vegamót is a café, shop and eatery in Bíldudalur on Arnarfjörður. It serves coffee, light meals and cakes in a homely setting in this small village.

About

Vegamót is a café, shop and eatery in the heart of Bíldudalur by Arnarfjörður. It offers coffee, light meals and cakes in a homely setting in a small village. The place is a gathering spot for locals and a handy stop for travellers passing through the southern Westfjords.

Good to know

Bíldudalur is a small village by Arnarfjörður, known among other things for its Sea Monster Museum. Vegamót combines a café and a shop, making it a convenient stop for both refreshment and essentials on the road. It is a good break while travelling around one of the most beautiful fjords in the Westfjords.
