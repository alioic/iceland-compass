About

Pallett is a specialty coffee house and small artisan bakery in the harbour town of Hafnarfjörður. It is known for carefully made specialty coffee alongside freshly baked scones and cakes. The atmosphere is cosy and personal, and the focus on quality shows in every cup. A lovely spot for coffee lovers after something handcrafted.

Good to know

The café is by the harbour in Hafnarfjörður, in calm and pretty surroundings. Ideal for a coffee break with a freshly baked treat while wandering the town. It is small and personal, so seats can fill at busy times — but that is part of the charm.
