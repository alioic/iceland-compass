Lágafellslaug

A popular family pool in Mosfellsbær with three slides and plenty of hot pots.

Lágafellslaug in Mosfellsbær is a spacious complex with an outdoor pool, indoor pool, children's pool and play pool. It offers three water slides, two hot pots, a massage pot, a cold tub and a steam room.
