Sjávarpakkhúsið

A seafood restaurant with views over Stykkishólmur harbour.

Sjávarpakkhúsið is a restaurant with lovely views over the harbour in Stykkishólmur. It emphasises locally sourced, seasonal food in cooperation with local fishermen and farmers, alongside a good selection of beer and wine.

About

Sjávarpakkhúsið is a seafood restaurant with lovely views over the harbour in Stykkishólmur. It emphasises local and seasonal ingredients in cooperation with the community, alongside a good selection of beer and wine. The atmosphere is relaxed yet warm, and the place is popular at both lunch and dinner with guests seeking fresh seafood from Breiðafjörður.

Good to know

The restaurant sits by the harbour in Stykkishólmur, where many tables face the boats and islands. As the menu is seasonal, it changes with what the day's catch brings. Keep in mind that during the peak of summer it can be wise to reserve a table.
