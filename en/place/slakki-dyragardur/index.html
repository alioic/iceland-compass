Slakki Petting Zoo

A cosy petting zoo in Laugarás where children can meet and touch calves, rabbits, guinea pigs and more.

Slakki is a much-loved petting zoo in Laugarás in the South Iceland highlands, just off the Golden Circle. Visitors can pet and feed calves, rabbits, guinea pigs, kittens, ducks, turtles and other animals. The site also has a playground, mini golf and a café, making it an easy stop for families with young children.

A family day at Slakki

Slakki in Laugarás is one of the most enjoyable family attractions in South Iceland. The zoo houses a variety of farm animals that children can pet and feed, from calves and rabbits to guinea pigs and kittens. Much of it is indoors and warm, so a visit stays comfortable regardless of the weather. The site also has a playground, mini golf and a café, so it is easy to spend a good while here. Its location just off the Golden Circle makes Slakki a convenient stop while touring the region.

The animals at Slakki

Slakki offers a varied cast of animals that delights children and adults alike. Visitors meet calves, rabbits, guinea pigs, kittens, ducks and turtles, and other animals often appear depending on the season. The focus is on close, safe contact where children learn to handle animals with care. Staff guide guests on feeding and animal welfare, making the visit both fun and educational. For many children it is a first chance to touch live farm animals up close.

Visiting Slakki

Slakki sits in Laugarás in the South Iceland highlands, roughly a half-hour drive from Selfoss via roads 30 and 31. The zoo is open daily through the summer and pairs well with a Golden Circle tour. It is worth checking opening hours before you go, especially outside peak season. On site there is space to sit down, grab refreshments and let the children play. Slakki is an ideal destination for families wanting to combine the outdoors, animals and entertainment in one place.
