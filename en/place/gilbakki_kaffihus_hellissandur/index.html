Gilbakki Kaffihús

A charming café in one of the prettiest houses in Hellissandur.

Gilbakki Kaffihús is a small café in one of the loveliest houses in Hellissandur on the Snæfellsnes peninsula. It serves fish soup with bread, coffee and homemade cakes.

About

Gilbakki Kaffihús is a small, charming café set in one of the prettiest houses in Hellissandur on the Snæfellsnes peninsula. It serves fish soup with bread, homemade cakes and coffee in a warm, cosy setting. The place has a genuine country charm and makes a welcome stop for anyone travelling around the northern side of Snæfellsnes.

Good to know

Hellissandur is a small fishing village at the tip of Snæfellsnes, near the Snæfellsjökull glacier and national park. The café works well as a resting point alongside walks and sightseeing in the area. The beautiful old house is part of the experience, and the place feels homely and personal.
