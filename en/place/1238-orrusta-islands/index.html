1238: The Battle of Iceland

Interactive Sturlung-era exhibition with virtual and augmented reality.

1238: The Battle of Iceland is a modern experience in the heart of Saudarkrokur that brings the violent conflicts of the Sturlung era to life. Using virtual reality, augmented reality and hands-on storytelling, visitors relive the Battle of Orlygsstadir, the largest battle in Icelandic history. The exhibition engages all ages and makes history vivid in an innovative way.

A virtual reality experience in Saudarkrokur

1238: The Battle of Iceland is unlike a traditional museum, using modern technology to bring the Sturlung era to life. Visitors put on a headset and experience the Battle of Orlygsstadir as if standing in the middle of the fighting. Located in the heart of Saudarkrokur, it suits both children and adults who want to learn about the country's history in an engaging way. It is an ideal indoor activity in any weather.

The Sturlung era comes alive

During the Sturlung era, powerful clans fought for control of Iceland, and 1238: The Battle of Iceland tells that story powerfully. Through virtual reality, augmented reality and interactive storytelling, visitors gain a deeper understanding of this turbulent time. The exhibition is accessible and interesting for the whole family and makes complex history easy to grasp. It is a great addition to a trip through Skagafjordur.

Services and facilities

The 1238 exhibition in Saudarkrokur also has a bistro where you can enjoy dishes made with local ingredients in a cosy setting. There is a gift shop as well, offering handmade products and Icelandic design. This lets visitors combine an educational experience, good coffee and some shopping in one place. Its central location in downtown Saudarkrokur makes the visit convenient for travellers in Northwest Iceland.
