Hólmavík Swimming Pool

A 25 m pool with hot tubs and steam bath in the Strandir region.

A 25-metre pool with two hot tubs, a children's wading pool and a steam bath at the Hólmavík sports centre. The main pool of the Strandir coast and a great stop en route to the remote northern Westfjords.
