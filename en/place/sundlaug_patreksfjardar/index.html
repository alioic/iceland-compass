Patreksfjörður Swimming Pool

Outdoor pool with a splendid view over Patreksfjörður fjord.

The pool at the Brattahlíð sports centre sits high in the town with a superb view over the fjord. The perfect end to a day visiting the Látrabjarg bird cliffs or Rauðisandur beach.
