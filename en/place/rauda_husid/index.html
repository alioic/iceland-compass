Rauða húsið

A well-established seafood restaurant in a historic red house in Eyrarbakki.

Rauða húsið (The Red House) is a restaurant in a near-century-old building in the historic fishing village of Eyrarbakki. It focuses on seafood, especially langoustine and fish, while also offering meat dishes.

About

Rauða húsið is a long-established seafood restaurant in a nearly century-old, historic red house in the old fishing village of Eyrarbakki. The focus is on seafood, above all langoustine and fresh fish, though meat dishes are also on offer. The mood is warm and inviting in this picturesque old building, a real destination for food lovers.

Good to know

Rauða húsið is in Eyrarbakki in South Iceland, a short drive from Selfoss and Stokkseyri. It is popular and reservations are recommended, especially in the evenings and at weekends. Prices are on the higher side, in keeping with the langoustine and specialty dishes. Ideal as a destination for a fine meal away from the crowds.
