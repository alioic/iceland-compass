Kaffi Rauðka

A café in a bright red house by the harbour in Siglufjörður.

Kaffi Rauðka occupies a bright red house by the marina in Siglufjörður and is open all year round. It serves home-cooked lunches on weekdays and a varied menu from cakes and sandwiches to hot dishes, and hosts concerts regularly.

About

Kaffi Rauðka occupies a bright-red house by the small-boat harbour in Siglufjörður and is open year-round. It serves home cooking at weekday lunchtimes and a varied menu ranging from cakes and sandwiches to hot dishes. Concerts are held regularly, so a warm and lively atmosphere often fills this harbour-side spot.

Good to know

Located on the Siglufjörður harbour, within walking distance of the Herring Era Museum and neighbouring Hannes Boy. Open all year, which makes it a good option outside the peak season. Keep an eye on the concert schedule if you are interested in live music.
