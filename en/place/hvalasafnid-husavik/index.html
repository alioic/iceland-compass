Husavik Whale Museum

One of the world's few whale museums, in the whale-watching town of Husavik.

The Husavik Whale Museum is dedicated to whales and marine life, housed in a former slaughterhouse by the harbour. It displays full whale skeletons suspended from the ceiling alongside informative exhibits on whale ecology and conservation. It is a perfect stop before or after a whale-watching tour.

The Whale Museum in the heart of Husavik

The Husavik Whale Museum is one of only a handful of museums in the world devoted entirely to whales. Housed in a former slaughterhouse by the harbour, full whale skeletons hang from the ceiling, showing the scale of these magnificent sea creatures. The museum teaches visitors about whale biology, behaviour and conservation in an accessible way suited to all ages. It is a perfect complement to the whale watching for which Husavik is famous.

Before and after whale watching

Husavik is often called Iceland's whale-watching capital, and the Whale Museum adds real depth to the trip. Visiting the museum before a boat tour gives guests a better understanding of what they will see at sea. After the tour, it is fun to view the skeletons and connect them to the living animals just spotted. This makes the whole experience more complete and educational for the family.

Family-friendly indoor learning

The Husavik Whale Museum is indoors, making it an ideal destination when the weather is not suited to outdoor activities. The exhibitions are visual and engaging and hold the attention of children and adults alike. The museum sits centrally by the harbour, with cafes and other services close at hand. The visit is a comfortable, informative and enjoyable addition to a trip through Northeast Iceland.
