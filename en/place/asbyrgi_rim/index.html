Ásbyrgi

A rim loop around the horseshoe canyon of Ásbyrgi with views down into its forest.

Ásbyrgi is a dramatic horseshoe-shaped canyon in Vatnajökull National Park, carved by catastrophic floods of the Jökulsá á Fjöllum river and tied to the legend of Sleipnir's hoofprint. The trail follows the canyon rim, offering views down into the lush woodland and across to the rock island of Eyjan. The path is clear but fairly long.
