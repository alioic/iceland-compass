Café Riis

A long-established restaurant in the oldest house in Hólmavík.

Café Riis occupies the oldest building in Hólmavík and has served classic Icelandic dishes since 1996. It emphasises high-quality ingredients from land and sea, and its pizzas are especially popular with guests and locals alike.

About

Café Riis is a long-established restaurant in the oldest house in Hólmavík and has served classic Icelandic dishes since 1996. It emphasises quality ingredients from land and sea, and its pizzas are especially popular with guests and locals alike. The historic house and relaxed atmosphere make it a welcoming destination.

Good to know

Café Riis is centrally located in Hólmavík by Steingrímsfjörður in the Strandir region. Its varied menu of both Icelandic dishes and pizzas makes it a good choice for different tastes. It is an ideal stop while touring Strandir, where the Museum of Icelandic Sorcery and Witchcraft is nearby.
