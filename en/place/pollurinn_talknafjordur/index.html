Pollurinn

Beloved hillside hot pots just outside the village of Tálknafjörður.

Pollurinn is a cluster of concrete pools of varying sizes and temperatures, originally built by local farmers in the mid-20th century. The water runs a toasty 40–45°C and the pools look out over the fjord. There are showers and enclosed changing rooms, and entry is free with a donation box.
