Við Voginn

A cozy spot in the middle of the village in Djúpivogur serving home-cooked food.

Við Voginn is a small, cozy spot in the heart of Djúpivogur. It offers home-style lunches along with fish and chips, hamburgers and sandwiches, with good views over the harbour and fjord.

About

Við Voginn is a small, cosy spot in the heart of Djúpivogur. It serves homely lunch fare along with fish and chips, burgers and sandwiches. Unpretentious and friendly, it suits families and anyone after simple, good food at a fair price while travelling through the East Fjords.

Good to know

The place is in the middle of the village of Djúpivogur, with a good view over the harbour and fjord. Djúpivogur is a calm Cittaslow town in East Iceland, known for the Eggs of Merry Bay artwork. It's a handy stop along the Ring Road.
