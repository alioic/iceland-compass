Geirabakarí Kaffihús

A bakery by Borgarfjörður, known from the Walter Mitty film.

Geirabakarí in Borgarnes sits by the sea with views over Borgarfjörður and Mount Hafnarfjall. The bakery featured in The Secret Life of Walter Mitty and serves fresh bread, cinnamon buns and coffee.

About

Geirabakarí Kaffihús is a bakery and café in Borgarnes, sitting by the sea with views over Borgarfjörður and Hafnarfjall. It serves fresh bread, cinnamon buns and other pastries alongside good coffee. The bakery appeared in the film The Secret Life of Walter Mitty and is a popular stop for travellers passing through West Iceland.

Good to know

Its spot by Borgarfjörður makes the bakery an easy stop just off the main road, whether for morning coffee or an afternoon treat. The view over the fjord and mountains is part of the experience. It suits a quick but satisfying break on the way between Reykjavík and Snæfellsnes or the Westfjords.
