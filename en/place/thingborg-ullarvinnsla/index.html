Þingborg Wool Centre

A wool workshop and shop in an old community hall where a group of women work Icelandic wool from scratch.

Þingborg occupies an old community hall and schoolhouse from 1927, about 8 km east of Selfoss. A group of women gathers here to knit, spin, card, felt and weave Icelandic wool in natural colours. The shop sells lopapeysa sweaters, knitwear, hand-dyed yarn and lopi, all handmade on site.

Wool worked from scratch

Þingborg is a wool workshop and shop in an old community hall east of Selfoss, where Icelandic wool is worked from scratch. A group of women cards, spins, knits, felts and weaves specially selected lambswool in natural colours. The result is unique craftwork where each garment has a story. For guests who want to buy genuine Icelandic local produce straight from the makers, it is an essential stop.

Lopapeysa sweaters and yarn

The Þingborg shop offers lopapeysa sweaters, hats, mittens and other knitwear, along with lopi and hand-dyed yarn for those who want to knit their own. Everything is handmade from Icelandic wool on site and shows careful craftsmanship. It is an ideal stop for knitters and anyone who values Iceland's wool tradition, just a short drive from Selfoss on the South Iceland route.

History and craft

The building that houses Þingborg was raised in 1927 as a community hall and schoolhouse and has taken on a new role as a wool centre. The shop is open weekdays and Saturdays and invites guests to discover a living craft tradition. Here you directly support a small group of makers and take home a product that is truly homegrown from the Icelandic countryside.
