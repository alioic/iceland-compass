Petra's Stone Collection

One of the world's largest private stone collections.

Petra's Stone Collection in Stöðvarfjörður holds the life's work of Petra Sveinsdóttir, who spent decades gathering stones and crystals from the nature of the East Fjords. The collection is displayed both indoors and in a beautiful garden around Petra's former home, where colourful stones lie in rows everywhere. It is a unique experience for anyone captivated by minerals and the craftsmanship of nature.

Petra's Stone Collection

Petra's Stone Collection is one of the best-known museums in East Iceland, holding a spectacular collection of stones and crystals that Petra Sveinsdóttir gathered over a long lifetime. The stones are displayed both inside her house and out in the garden, where the range of colours and forms surprises visitors at every turn. The collection is a living testament to one woman's passion for the nature of her home region. It is especially well suited to families, as the colourful stones captivate children and adults alike. A visit to Petra's garden is a calm and beautiful experience in the heart of Stöðvarfjörður.

Opening hours and visiting

Petra's Stone Collection is open daily through the summer season and welcomes visitors of all ages. Stöðvarfjörður is a small, peaceful village on the southern East Fjords, along the coastal road. A visit usually takes half an hour to an hour, and you can wander the indoor display and the garden at your own pace. There is a small shop on site where you can buy stones and souvenirs. Outside the peak season it can be wise to confirm opening in advance. The museum is a pleasant and interesting stop while touring the East Fjords.

For rock lovers and families

Petra's Stone Collection is a true paradise for anyone who enjoys stones, geology and beautiful nature. In the garden, stones large and small lie in rows, many with glittering crystals and unusual colours found in the mountains of East Iceland. Children love hunting for the prettiest stones while adults admire the sheer variety. The setting is welcoming and the atmosphere relaxed, making the visit a good rest along the journey. Petra's Stone Collection is a remarkable example of how one person's passion can become a priceless treasure for the community.
