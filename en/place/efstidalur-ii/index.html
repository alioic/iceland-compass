Efstidalur II

A family farm on the Golden Circle with a cowshed café, homemade ice cream and animals all around.

Efstidalur II is a working dairy farm in Bláskógabyggð, between Laugarvatn and Geysir, that warmly welcomes visitors. In the cowshed café you can enjoy homemade ice cream made from the farm's own milk while watching the cows through large windows. Around the farm are horses, calves and other animals, making it a fun stop for families on the Golden Circle.

The cowshed café and homemade ice cream

Efstidalur II is a special stop on the Golden Circle where farm life and dining come together. In the cowshed café you can enjoy homemade ice cream made from the farm's own cows' milk while watching the animals through large windows. This gives children and adults a fun glimpse of where food comes from. The setting is warm and welcoming, making it an ideal place to pause, rest and enjoy while travelling between Laugarvatn and Geysir.

Farm animals up close

At Efstidalur the animals are close by, making the visit lively and family-friendly. The cows in the barn, horses in the fields and other farm animals give the place character, and children love watching daily life on a working farm. This is a real farm, not a staged experience, which gives the visit an authentic feel. Families can take their time exploring, getting to know the animals and experiencing the Icelandic countryside in a calm, hands-on way.

A stop on the Golden Circle

Efstidalur II is well placed in Bláskógabyggð, between Laugarvatn and Geysir, and fits naturally into a Golden Circle tour. The site combines dining, ice cream and animals, making it a convenient stop for families. It pairs well with other attractions in the area, and the cowshed café is worth lingering in. For many visitors this blend of countryside, animals and homemade ice cream becomes the most memorable part of their day in South Iceland.
