Kaupfélag Steingrímsfjarðar – Pakkhúsið

Long-established locally owned co-op store in Hólmavík.

Kaupfélag Steingrímsfjarðar was founded in 1898 and runs the Pakkhúsið shop in Hólmavík, which is entirely locally owned. It offers personal service and a varied range of goods for residents and travellers in the Strandir region. The store is a classic Icelandic village shop and an important stop on the way through the area.

A village shop with history

Kaupfélag Steingrímsfjarðar was founded in 1898 and is one of the oldest operating shops in the Strandir region. The co-op runs the Pakkhúsið store in Hólmavík, which is entirely locally owned and offers personal service and a varied range of goods. The shop is a classic Icelandic village store where residents and travellers pick up what they need. For those travelling through Strandir it is an important stop and a fine example of community enterprise with a long history.

Services in Hólmavík

In Hólmavík, Pakkhúsið is the heart of retail and service in the village. It sells groceries and a variety of goods, and the co-op also handles distribution and transport in the area. The store is open on weekdays and is welcome for travellers who need to restock supplies on their way through Strandir, where distances between service points can be considerable. Personal service and local knowledge make a visit more than an ordinary shopping trip.

Strandir as a destination

Hólmavík is the largest settlement in Strandir and a good base for exploring this remote and captivating part of the Westfjords. The Pakkhúsið co-op store ensures that residents and guests have access to essentials, and the place reflects the history of cooperative retail in Iceland. For travellers who want to experience a genuine Icelandic village shop and support a rural community, it is an ideal stop on the way through Strandir and the northern Westfjords.
