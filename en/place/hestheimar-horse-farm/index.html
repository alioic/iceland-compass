Hestheimar

A family-run horse farm near Hella offering riding tours for beginners and experienced riders alike.

Hestheimar is a family-run horse farm and guesthouse in Ásahreppur, close to Hella and just off the Ring Road. It offers riding tours on Icelandic horses for both beginners and more experienced riders, with views towards Hekla and Eyjafjallajökull. The farm warmly welcomes families and is a great place to meet the Icelandic horse and experience rural life in South Iceland.

Meet the Icelandic horse

Hestheimar in Ásahreppur is an ideal place for families to meet the Icelandic horse in South Iceland. It offers riding tours suited to both beginners and experienced riders, travelling through beautiful countryside with views towards Hekla and Eyjafjallajökull. Experienced staff guide guests and choose calm horses for those taking their first steps. This makes the visit safe and enjoyable for all ages and offers a unique way to experience the Icelandic horse in its natural surroundings.

Country atmosphere near Hella

Hestheimar is a family-run farm where guests find a warm rural atmosphere close to Hella and the Ring Road. Alongside riding tours, the site offers accommodation, hot tubs and a cosy setting for relaxing after time outdoors. The surroundings are calm and expansive, with mountains and glaciers in the backdrop. For families it is a good place to combine the outdoors, animals and rest in one spot. Its proximity to the nature and historic sites of South Iceland makes it a handy base for further exploring.

Visiting and location

Hestheimar is in Ásahreppur, roughly thirteen kilometres from Hella and just off the Ring Road, making it easy to visit while touring South Iceland. Riding tours are offered year-round, and it is worth booking ahead to secure a place. The farm works well as a stop between the Golden Circle and the south coast. For those wanting to experience the Icelandic horse and rural life in beautiful surroundings, Hestheimar is a destination that lives up to expectations for families and experienced riders alike.
