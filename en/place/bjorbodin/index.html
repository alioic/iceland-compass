Bjórböðin Beer Spa

Iceland's original beer spa paired with geothermal hot tubs on Eyjafjörður.

Bjórböðin at Árskógssandur is Iceland's first beer spa, where guests soak in young beer, hops and live yeast in private hardwood tubs. The bathing session is followed by outdoor geothermal hot tubs overlooking Eyjafjörður, plus a relaxation room and restaurant.
