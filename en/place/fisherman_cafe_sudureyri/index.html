Fisherman Café

A cosy local favourite in the heart of the fishing village of Suðureyri.

Fisherman Café is a local favourite at the centre of Suðureyri, with a relaxed atmosphere and warm, characterful surroundings. It serves cakes and espresso alongside locally landed seafood such as fish cakes and lobster soup.

About

Fisherman Café is a local favourite in the middle of the fishing village of Suðureyri, with a relaxed and welcoming atmosphere. It serves cakes and espresso alongside local seafood dishes such as fish balls and langoustine soup. It is a cosy spot that reflects the fishing-village mood and works well for both a coffee and a light meal.

Good to know

Suðureyri is a small fishing village by Súgandafjörður, connected to Ísafjörður through a tunnel. The village is known for its strong fishing heritage and closeness to fresh seafood. The café is an ideal stop for those wanting to experience a quiet Westfjords village and taste local seafood.
