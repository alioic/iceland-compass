Kolaportið Flea Market

Iceland's largest weekend flea market in the old customs house.

Kolaportið is Iceland's largest flea market, open on weekends in the old customs house by the harbour in Reykjavík. Dozens of vendors sell crafts, wool, vintage clothing and Icelandic food such as dried fish, kleinur doughnuts and fermented shark.

Weekend market by the harbour

Kolaportið is Iceland's largest flea market, open on weekends from 11 to 17 in the old customs house on Tryggvagata by the Reykjavík waterfront. Dozens of vendors gather with a varied mix of crafts, wool, vintage clothing, books and all sorts of found treasures. The market has been part of the city's culture for decades and is an ideal place to wander at leisure, hunt for bargains and soak up the atmosphere straight from the locals who run the stalls.

Icelandic food

Kolaportið has a dedicated food corner where visitors can taste and buy Icelandic food traditions. You will find dried fish, skyr, flatbread, kleinur doughnuts and even fermented shark for the brave, plus liquorice and freshly baked treats. It is a rare chance to meet Icelandic food straight from the vendors and take the flavours home. Many stalls prefer cash, but there is an ATM at the entrance. For many visitors the food corner is the highlight of the trip.

Crafts and finds

Beyond the food, Kolaportið is full of crafts and Icelandic wool, including lopapeysa sweaters and knitwear from locals, along with vintage clothing, records, books and curios of every kind. The selection changes from week to week as vendors come and go, so every visit is different. For travellers who want to shop local at fair prices and experience everyday Icelandic culture, this market is one of the most enjoyable stops in central Reykjavík.
