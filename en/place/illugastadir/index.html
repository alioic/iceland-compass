Illugastaðir

An outdoor pool in the Illugastaðir holiday village in Fnjóskadalur, open to all in summer.

At Illugastaðir in Fnjóskadalur, about 27 km east of Akureyri, is a 16.5-metre outdoor pool with two hot tubs and a sunbathing area. The pool sits in a union holiday village but is open to everyone during summer.

A swim in the Fnjóskadalur holiday village

Illugastaðir in Fnjóskadalur is a family-friendly holiday village about 27 km east of Akureyri, with a 16.5-metre outdoor pool that is open to everyone in summer. The pool has two hot tubs and a good sunbathing area, perfect for enjoying the quiet of the valley. The area is ideal for hiking, fishing and outdoor recreation and suits families touring the Þingeyjarsýslur districts and Eyjafjörður. The outdoor pool is a welcome addition for anyone staying nearby or passing through North East Iceland in summer.

A stop in a beautiful valley

Fnjóskadalur is one of Iceland's most verdant valleys, and the outdoor pool at Illugastaðir is a welcome stop when travelling the area. It is open from early June to the end of August and suits both swimmers and those who just want to soak in a hot tub after a hike or drive. Although the village mainly serves union holiday guests, the pool itself is open to the public. Remember to follow Icelandic pool rules and shower thoroughly before entering the water.
