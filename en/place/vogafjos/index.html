Vogafjós

A restaurant in a cowshed by Mývatn with views over the cows.

Vogafjós is a unique country restaurant where you dine beside a glass wall separating the dining room from the cowshed. The focus is on homemade local food - smoked trout, homemade mozzarella and geothermal rye bread baked in the ground by Mývatn.

About

Vogafjós is a one-of-a-kind country restaurant by Lake Mývatn where guests dine beside a glass wall separating the dining room from a working cowshed. The focus is on homemade local fare — smoked Mývatn trout, house-made cheese and geothermal rye bread baked in the ground. Warm and cosy, it is a genuine taste of Icelandic farm cooking where agriculture and cuisine meet.

Good to know

Set right by Lake Mývatn, it makes a natural stop while touring the area alongside Dimmuborgir, Hverfjall and the Mývatn Nature Baths. Family-friendly, with cows to watch through the glass. Check opening hours in the winter months and expect it to be busy at peak times.
