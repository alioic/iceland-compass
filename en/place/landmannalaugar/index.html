Landmannalaugar

Landmannalaugar – rainbow mountains and a natural hot spring in the highlands

Landmannalaugar is the crown jewel of the Fjallabak region in Iceland's southern highlands and one of the country's most sought-after destinations for hikers and nature lovers. The area is famous for its colourful rhyolite mountains in shades of red, yellow and green, sprawling lava fields and a warm natural pool where visitors can bathe surrounded by spectacular scenery. This is the starting point of the Laugavegur, one of the world's most famous hiking trails, leading to Þórsmörk. Landmannalaugar offers a pristine highland experience where geothermal heat, vivid colour and wilderness merge into an unforgettable whole.

Things to see and do in Landmannalaugar

At Landmannalaugar you can soak in the warm natural pool after a long day on the trails and hike a variety of day routes through the colourful mountains. A popular walk climbs Brennisteinsalda, a vividly coloured mountain with steaming geothermal vents, while the Ljótipollur crater and the Laugahraun obsidian lava field offer striking landscapes. For longer treks, the Laugavegur is the launch point for a multi-day, hut-to-hut adventure. Marked trails of varying difficulty suit both beginners and experienced hikers. The area is a true paradise for photographers, walkers and anyone who loves highland nature.

Location and how to reach Landmannalaugar

Landmannalaugar lies deep in the highlands within the Fjallabak region and is only accessible in summer when the mountain roads open. The route follows highland tracks such as the Fjallabaksleið nyrðri (F208), which require sturdy four-wheel-drive vehicles because unbridged rivers must be crossed. The journey from Reykjavík takes several hours. Travellers without a suitable 4x4 can take a scheduled highland bus in summer, a convenient and safe option. Always check road conditions and accessibility with the Icelandic Road Administration before setting off into the highlands.

Best time to visit Landmannalaugar and practical tips

Landmannalaugar is only accessible from June to September, when the highland roads are open and the snow has melted. Summer is the ideal time for hiking and bathing in the natural pool, but highland weather can change rapidly and turn cold without warning. Dress in warm, waterproof layers, bring swimwear for the pool and good hiking boots. There is a mountain hut and a campsite on site but no shop, so carry your own food and all necessary gear. Book huts well in advance and check the forecast and road conditions before you head out.
