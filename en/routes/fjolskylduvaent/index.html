Family-friendly

Places the kids will love

Iceland is wonderful for families, and here are places that charm children and adults alike: the erupting Strokkur geyser, Goðafoss waterfall, the Friðheimar tomato greenhouse with its horse show, the Secret Lagoon natural pool, swimming pools (such as Laugardalslaug with its water slides), whale watching in Húsavík, the Vestmannaeyjar volcanic islands with their puffins, and the Forest Lagoon baths. Easy access and something for everyone.

Places on this route

Capital Region7

  • Laugardalslaug Iceland's largest swimming complex in the Laugardalur valley, packed with hot pots and slides for the whole family.
  • Reykjavík Family Park and Zoo Farm animals, native Icelandic wildlife and rides in Laugardalur in the heart of Reykjavík.
  • Whales of Iceland Europe's largest whale exhibition, with life-size whale models at the Old Harbour.
  • Perlan – Wonders of Iceland Interactive nature exhibition, a man-made ice cave and an observation deck on Öskjuhlíð hill.
  • Árbær Open Air Museum An open-air museum of old houses showing how life was lived in Reykjavík in times past.
  • FlyOver Iceland A flight-simulation ride in the Grandi district that lets you soar over Iceland's landscapes.
  • Aurora Reykjavík A northern lights centre in the Grandi district where you can learn about and experience the aurora all year round.

Reykjanes Peninsula3

  • Viking World A museum in Njarðvík home to the Viking ship Íslendingur and exhibitions on settlement and seafaring.
  • Duus Museum The art and cultural centre of Reykjanesbær by the harbour in Keflavík.
  • Icelandic Museum of Rock 'n' Roll An interactive museum on the history of Icelandic popular music in Reykjanesbær.

West Iceland3

  • The Settlement Center Exhibitions on the settlement of Iceland and Egil's Saga in the heart of Borgarnes.
  • Háafell Goat Farm A goat farm in Borgarfjörður where visitors get to know the Icelandic goat.
  • Erpsstaðir Creamery A family-run dairy farm in the Dalir region with homemade ice cream, skyr and a petting zoo.

Westfjords6

  • The Icelandic Sea Monster Museum A museum devoted to the sea monsters of Arnarfjörður.
  • The Arctic Fox Centre A nature centre about the Icelandic arctic fox in Súðavík.
  • Museum of Icelandic Sorcery & Witchcraft An exhibition on sorcery and the witch-hunts of Strandir.
  • Ósvör Maritime Museum A reconstructed fishing station from the age of rowing boats.
  • Samúel Jónsson's Art Farm The folk art of Samúel Jónsson in a remote valley.
  • Hrafnseyri – Jón Sigurðsson Museum The birthplace and museum of Jón Sigurðsson by Arnarfjörður.

Northwest Iceland4

  • The Icelandic Seal Center Interactive seal exhibition in the red harbour house at Hvammstangi.
  • Glaumbaer Turf Farm Museum A historic turf farm in Skagafjordur where old rural life comes alive.
  • 1238: The Battle of Iceland Interactive Sturlung-era exhibition with virtual and augmented reality.
  • Brunastadir Petting Zoo A family-run petting zoo in Fljot with many friendly farm animals.

Northeast Iceland9

  • Goðafoss The waterfall of the gods, where the pagan gods were cast aside around the year 1000.
  • Húsavík Europe's whale-watching capital with sea baths on the cliff edge.
  • Skógarböðin (Forest Lagoon) Geothermal baths tucked into a birch forest above Eyjafjörður.
  • Husavik Whale Museum One of the world's few whale museums, in the whale-watching town of Husavik.
  • The Christmas Garden Year-round Christmas cheer in a gingerbread house just south of Akureyri.
  • Akureyri Botanical Garden A beautiful botanical garden in the heart of Akureyri with diverse plants.
  • The Motorcycle Museum of Iceland A museum devoted to the history of motorcycles in Iceland, at Krokeyri in Akureyri.
  • Daladyrd Petting Zoo A family-run petting zoo in Fnjoskadalur valley with diverse animals.
  • Kjarnaskogur Forest Iceland's most-visited forest, with playgrounds and trails by Akureyri.

East Iceland5

  • The Wilderness Center A culture and experience centre on the edge of the highlands.
  • Petra's Stone Collection One of the world's largest private stone collections.
  • Skriðuklaustur A culture centre and historic site in the author's house.
  • East Iceland Heritage Museum The cultural history of East Iceland and a reindeer exhibition.
  • Papey Island Boat Tours A boat trip to the puffin island of Papey from Djúpivogur.

South Iceland14

  • Friðheimar Dining inside a tomato greenhouse on the Golden Circle.
  • Geysir og Strokkur The original erupting hot spring — Strokkur erupts every few minutes.
  • Gamla laugin (Flúðir) The oldest swimming pool in the country, a natural geothermal pool in Flúðir.
  • Vestmannaeyjar A volcanic archipelago with a huge puffin colony and the volcano Eldfell.
  • Slakki Petting Zoo A cosy petting zoo in Laugarás where children can meet and touch calves, rabbits, guinea pigs and more.
  • LAVA Centre An interactive exhibition about Iceland's volcanoes, earthquakes and geology in the heart of Hvolsvöllur.
  • Efstidalur II A family farm on the Golden Circle with a cowshed café, homemade ice cream and animals all around.
  • Icelandic Lava Show A live show where real molten lava is poured in front of the audience in Vík.
  • Eldheimar A volcano museum in the Westman Islands built around a house buried in ash by the 1973 eruption.
  • SEA LIFE Trust Beluga Whale Sanctuary A visitor centre in the Westman Islands with an aquarium, rescued puffins and beluga whales in Klettsvík bay.
  • Skógar Museum A folk museum in Skógar with thousands of artefacts, turf houses and old buildings showing Iceland's rural past.
  • Hestheimar A family-run horse farm near Hella offering riding tours for beginners and experienced riders alike.
  • Saga Centre A saga exhibition in Hvolsvöllur dedicated to Njáls saga, with a Viking hall and a giant tapestry.
  • Sveitagarðurinn A petting zoo and play park in Grímsnes where children can get close to farm animals.
