Ullarselið – Wool Centre

A locals' wool centre in Hvanneyri with hand-made wool goods.

Ullarselið in Hvanneyri in Borgarfjörður is a craft shop devoted to Icelandic wool and other natural materials. Locals banded together to sell hand-made goods: knitwear, felted and woven pieces, scarves and accessories made from quality wool.

Wool from the region

Ullarselið sits in the heart of Hvanneyri, a small rural village in Borgarfjörður known for its agricultural heritage and the Agricultural Museum of Iceland. The locals of Hvanneyri banded together to set up a shop where they sell hand-made goods from Icelandic wool and other natural materials. Here you find knitwear, felted and woven pieces, scarves and more, all made by hand by local people. For travellers in West Iceland, it is an ideal place to buy genuine local goods straight from the makers.

Craft and tradition

Ullarselið emphasises traditional methods of wool work, where the wool is combed, spun, knitted, felted and woven. The selection reflects the craftsmanship and care of the locals, and each item carries a story and a personal touch. The centre also holds workshops where guests can learn traditional knitting and wool techniques and even make their own piece. This turns the visit into a living experience of Icelandic craft tradition rather than an ordinary shopping trip and a memorable stop on the way west.

Hours and location

Ullarselið is open in summer from 15 May to 15 September and in winter on Thursdays, Fridays and Saturdays. Hvanneyri is about a seventeen-minute drive from Borgarnes, so it is easy to reach on a tour of West Iceland. The setting is peaceful and historic and the shop small and personal. For those who want to get off the beaten path and buy wool goods straight from the locals, it is one of the most interesting stops in Borgarfjörður.
