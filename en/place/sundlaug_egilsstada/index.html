Sundlaug Egilsstaða

A well-equipped town pool in the heart of Egilsstaðir.

The Egilsstaðir swimming pool is a 25-metre outdoor pool with hot tubs at three temperatures, a cold plunge, a water slide, a sauna and a children's pool. It is a well-maintained and accessible bathing spot for both locals and travellers in East Iceland.
