Tjöruhúsið

A celebrated fish buffet in one of the oldest houses in Ísafjörður.

Tjöruhúsið in Neðstikaupstaður is a legend among fish lovers. In an old timber house from the 18th century, day-fresh fish is served straight from the boats, pan-fried and presented as a buffet where guests can go back again and again.

About

Tjöruhúsið in Neðstikaupstaður in Ísafjörður is a legend among fish lovers. In an old 18th-century timber house, day-fresh fish comes straight off the boats, pan-fried and served as a buffet where guests may go back again and again. The atmosphere in the historic building is unique, and the place has long been celebrated for its freshness and quality.

Good to know

The house is part of Neðstikaupstaður, the oldest quarter of Ísafjörður, which also holds the Westfjords Heritage Museum. Because of its popularity, booking a table is strongly recommended, especially in summer. The fish buffet is the heart of the experience, so it is best to arrive hungry.
