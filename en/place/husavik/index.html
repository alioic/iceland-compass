Húsavík

Europe's whale-watching capital with sea baths on the cliff edge.

Húsavík is a colourful fishing town and one of the best places in the country for whale watching - humpbacks, minke whales and even blue whales are seen regularly. The GeoSea sea baths offer warm seawater with views over Skjálfandi bay.

Húsavík – Europe's Whale-Watching Capital

Húsavík is a colourful, charming fishing town on Skjálfandi bay in North Iceland, often celebrated as Europe's whale-watching capital. The town is renowned for the rich waters just offshore, where humpback and minke whales, and sometimes even blue whales, can be seen in summer. A highlight of the Diamond Circle (Demantshringurinn), Húsavík combines a lively harbour atmosphere with brightly painted houses and sweeping views across the bay toward distant mountains. The town has carved out a special reputation as a destination for nature lovers and anyone hoping to experience the thrill of seeing great whales in their natural habitat off the northern coast.

Things to See and Do in Húsavík

In Húsavík, whale watching is the main draw, with boats setting out across Skjálfandi bay in search of great whales and other marine life. The town's whale museum introduces visitors to ocean wildlife and the history of whale watching in the region. The GeoSea geothermal sea baths offer a unique experience, letting you soak in warm seawater while gazing over the bay. Húsavík's beautiful wooden church is one of its most beloved landmarks and gives the town centre its character. Together with the harbour, restaurants and colourful houses, they make Húsavík a delightful place to linger.

Getting to Húsavík on the Diamond Circle

Húsavík lies within easy reach of Akureyri and Lake Mývatn and is one of the key stops on the Diamond Circle in North Iceland. A well-maintained road leads north from Mývatn to Húsavík, and the town also connects to other gems of the loop such as Ásbyrgi and Dettifoss. Húsavík is frequently chosen as a base or overnight stop while touring the north, thanks to its good range of services, accommodation and dining. Its location on Skjálfandi bay makes it a natural stop for travellers wanting to combine whale watching with a full circuit of the region.

Best Time to Visit Húsavík and Practical Tips

Summer is the best time to visit Húsavík, especially for whale watching, when chances of spotting great whales are highest and daylight lasts late into the evening. Dress in warm, windproof layers for boat tours, as it can feel cold out on the water even on fine summer days; most operators provide protective overalls. The GeoSea sea baths are enjoyable in any season and a relaxing way to end the day. It is wise to book whale-watching tours in advance during peak season and to allow plenty of time to enjoy the town and its harbour.
