Samúel Jónsson's Art Farm

The folk art of Samúel Jónsson in a remote valley.

In Selárdalur at the outer edge of Arnarfjörður stands the art farm of Samúel Jónsson, a self-taught folk artist often called the artist with a child's heart. Here he built a church, an art house and many concrete sculptures, including a lion fountain and statues, with no formal art training. The works stand in the open air and are open to visitors through the summer.

Samúel Jónsson's Art Farm

At the far end of Selárdalur by Arnarfjörður lies one of Iceland's most remarkable works of folk art, the creation of Samúel Jónsson. Samúel, often called the artist with a child's heart, built a church, an art house and many concrete sculptures late in life, despite never having received any art training. Among his best-known works is a colourful lion fountain inspired by distant lands. The works stand in the open air in a remote and beautiful valley, surrounded by mountains and sea. Volunteers have worked to restore the pieces, and the site is open to visitors free of charge.

Visiting Selárdalur

Samúel Jónsson's Art Farm is open to visitors through the summer, and admission is free. To reach Selárdalur you drive an unpaved road from Bíldudalur, about half an hour, so it is wise to take care with the drive and the weather. The setting is unique, far from other tourist sites, and visitors experience both the calm and the beauty of the Westfjords. On site you can see the sculptures, the church and the art house that Samúel built himself. A visit to Selárdalur is an adventure for families who want to get off the beaten track and discover an unusual piece of art history.

Folk art in a unique setting

Samúel Jónsson's creation is a remarkable example of the power of folk art and one man's passion for creation. Without any formal training he shaped a whole world out of concrete, where lions, statues and a church testify to boundless imagination. For children the site is like an adventure, with unusual figures that spark curiosity, while adults admire the artist's sincerity and resourcefulness. The remote valley setting makes the visit even more memorable. Samúel Jónsson's Art Farm in Selárdalur is a hidden treasure that rewards those who make the journey.
