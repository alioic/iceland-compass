Eimverk Distillery (Flóki)

Iceland's first whisky distillery, made entirely from Icelandic barley.

This family-run distillery in Garðabær makes Flóki single malt whisky, Vor gin and Víti brennivín, all from 100% Icelandic barley. Guided tours with tastings run by appointment, and an on-site shop sells the full range of spirits and gifts.

Icelandic barley whisky

Eimverk in Garðabær is Iceland's first and only whisky distillery, making all of its spirits from 100% Icelandic barley. Flóki single malt matures in the Icelandic climate and takes its distinctive character from the raw ingredient and the weather. Travellers who want to bring home genuine local production will find whisky, gin and brennivín here, straight from the maker. The distillery is a small family business and the on-site shop is open on weekdays for anyone who wants to buy direct.

Tours and tastings

Eimverk offers guided tours of the distillery by appointment, letting guests follow the production process from barley to bottle. The tour lasts a little over an hour and ends with a tasting of Flóki whisky, Vor gin and Víti brennivín. A member of the team answers questions and guests get access to the shop. It is an ideal visit for anyone interested in Icelandic craft production and a good way to support a small local business by buying direct.

What's on offer

The Eimverk shop sells the full range of the distillery's spirits plus gifts, located at Lyngás 13 in Garðabær, just a short drive from central Reykjavík. Here you can buy Flóki single malt in various expressions, Vor gin flavoured with Icelandic botanicals and Víti brennivín. The products make excellent gifts or souvenirs and are made in small batches. For travellers who want to shop local straight from the producer, this is one of the most interesting stops in the capital region.
