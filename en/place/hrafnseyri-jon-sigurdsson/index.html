Hrafnseyri – Jón Sigurðsson Museum

Hrafnseyri by Arnarfjörður

Hrafnseyri is a historic site and museum by Arnarfjörður, the birthplace of Jón Sigurðsson, who led Iceland's campaign for independence in the 19th century. The site has a museum tracing his life, ideals and work, along with a reconstructed turf farm showing how people lived around the time Jón was born. The surroundings are peaceful and beautiful, enclosed by mountains and fjord, and there is a small café for visitors. Hrafnseyri is a welcome stop for anyone wanting to learn about Icelandic history and understand the roots of the independence movement in the lovely Westfjords landscape.

Opening hours and visiting

Hrafnseyri is open to visitors daily through the summer, and the visit suits both families and history enthusiasts. The site is on the northern side of Arnarfjörður, within driving distance of Þingeyri and Ísafjörður, often reached via the Dýrafjörður tunnel. At the museum you can learn about Jón Sigurðsson and his era, explore the turf farm and enjoy coffee and cake. The surroundings also offer scenic walks and views over the fjord. Note that opening is seasonal, so it is best to plan your visit during the summer months.

The story of independence

Jón Sigurðsson is among the most influential figures in Icelandic history, and Hrafnseyri offers a rare chance to learn about his origins and ideals. The museum connects Jón's personal story with the larger history of a nation striving for independence. The reconstructed turf farm helps visitors understand everyday life in earlier times and put the history in context. For families the visit is educational without being heavy, and the setting offers the outdoors and quiet. Hrafnseyri brings together history, culture and nature at one of the most beautiful spots in the Westfjords.
