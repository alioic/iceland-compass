About

Sker is a cosy restaurant in the heart of Ólafsvík on the Snæfellsnes peninsula, with views over the harbour. The menu is varied, with an emphasis on good ingredients including fresh local seafood. The atmosphere is relaxed, and the place suits both lunch and dinner for travellers exploring the northern side of the peninsula.

Good to know

The restaurant is centrally located in Ólafsvík, overlooking the harbour, and makes a convenient stop on a loop around Snæfellsnes. Ólafsvík is a good base for whale watching and hiking near the Snæfellsjökull glacier. Its varied menu makes it a solid choice for different tastes.
