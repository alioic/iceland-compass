Stofan Kaffihús

Cosy café in a historic building with a living-room feel.

Stofan Kaffihús on Vesturgata in the old town has the feel of a warm living room, with sofas and armchairs. It is known for its hot chocolate and freshly baked cakes.

About

Stofan Kaffihús sits on Vesturgata in the old town and feels like a warm living room, complete with sofas and armchairs. It is known for hot chocolate and home-baked cakes, and the mood is relaxed and cosy. The old building gives it a distinctive charm, making it a lovely place to sit down and unwind.

Good to know

The café is in the old town, within walking distance of the old harbour and downtown. It is ideal for warming up over a hot chocolate on a cold day or lingering for a while. The sofas invite you to stay, so arrive in good time at busy hours to grab a comfortable seat.
