Hólmavík is the main village in the Strandir region and home to the Museum of Sorcery and Witchcraft, which tells the story of 17th-century witch persecutions in the Westfjords. Nearby are the swimming pool at Drangsnes and hot tubs at the water's edge.

Hólmavík – Gateway to the Strandir Coast and Iceland's Witchcraft Legends

Hólmavík is the largest village on the remote Strandir coast in Iceland's Westfjords and the natural base for travellers exploring this little-visited region. It is best known for the Museum of Icelandic Sorcery and Witchcraft, which tells the dark story of the 17th-century witch hunts that swept the Westfjords. Here a living fishing village, dramatic coastal scenery and deep roots in Icelandic folklore come together. For many visitors, Hólmavík is the doorway to the quiet, sparsely travelled north of the Westfjords, where wide-open space and history meet.

Things to See and Do in Hólmavík

The Museum of Icelandic Sorcery and Witchcraft is an essential stop, bringing the region's history of magic, sorcery and witch trials vividly to life. Just a short drive away lies Drangsnes, famous for its free hot pots at the water's edge where you can soak while gazing out over Húnaflói bay. A swimming pool, coastal walking trails and beachcombing round out a stay. Travellers can also simply savour the calm of the Strandir coast, watch abundant birdlife and explore small fishing hamlets that preserve old Icelandic maritime culture.

How to Get to Hólmavík

Hólmavík sits on the northern edge of the Westfjords, linked to the ring road by Route 68 branching off the main Westfjords road. From Reykjavík you drive north through Borgarnes and Brú in Hrútafjörður, then east along the Strandir coastline to the village. The road is paved and open year-round, and the several-hour journey rewards you with sweeping views across Húnaflói bay. Hólmavík makes an ideal waypoint for anyone wanting to connect North Iceland and the Westfjords on a single road trip.

Best Time to Visit Hólmavík and Practical Tips

The best time to visit Hólmavík is from May to September, when roads are clear, museums are open and the weather is at its mildest. Summer brings long daylight hours, a lively village atmosphere and good conditions for hiking the Strandir coast. Keep in mind that services are limited outside peak season, so it pays to book accommodation and plan meals in advance. Pack warm, waterproof clothing, as weather on the Strandir coast can be unpredictable, and fill up on fuel before venturing further afield.
