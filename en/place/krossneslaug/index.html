Krossneslaug

One of Iceland's most remote pools, set on the beach in Strandir.

Krossneslaug was built by locals in 1954 and is heated by natural geothermal water, a rarity in the Strandir region. It sits on a gravel bank facing the open ocean, with the main pool around 38–40°C plus a smaller, hotter tub. The gravel road in is long and rough, but the reward is unforgettable.
