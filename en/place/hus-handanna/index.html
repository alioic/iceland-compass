Hús Handanna

Craft and design shop in the heart of Egilsstaðir.

Hús Handanna is a small craft and design shop founded in 2010 at East Iceland's busiest crossroads. It carries a broad selection of Icelandic product design, art craft, clothing and chosen souvenirs. The shop emphasises eco-friendly goods and an artistic lifestyle.

Design and craft in Egilsstaðir

Hús Handanna sits in the heart of Egilsstaðir, at one of East Iceland's busiest crossroads, and since 2010 has been a showcase for Icelandic design and craft. The shop carries product design, art craft, clothing and selected souvenirs, often from East Iceland and Icelandic designers. The focus is on quality and an artistic lifestyle rather than mass production. For travellers keen to buy local produce and unique gifts, it is an ideal place to support the creative sector in rural Iceland.

What is on offer

Hús Handanna holds a varied selection of design and craft goods, from ceramics and textiles to clothing and jewellery. The shop also offers an eco-friendly line for the home and personal care. Many of the products are made by Icelandic designers and artists, making them unique souvenirs with a story. Its central location in Egilsstaðir makes it easy to drop in while travelling around East Iceland and find well-made local produce.

Central in East Iceland

Egilsstaðir is the service hub of East Iceland, and Hús Handanna is within walking distance of accommodation, dining and other shops. The store is ideal for those who want to buy quality Icelandic design and craft close to its source rather than mass-produced souvenirs. You will find gifts for yourself and others while supporting creative people in the region. It is best to check opening hours on the website, especially outside the high season.
