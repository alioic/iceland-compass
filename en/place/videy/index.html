Viðey

A tranquil island in the sound with history, artworks and Yoko Ono's Imagine Peace Tower.

Viðey is a small island just off Reykjavík, only a few minutes by boat. It is home to the country's oldest stone house, Viðeyjarstofa, walking trails and Yoko Ono's Imagine Peace Tower, which lights up the winter sky.

Videy – a Peaceful Historic Island on Reykjavik's Doorstep

Videy is a small, tranquil island in the sound just off Reykjavik, only a few minutes' sail from the city yet a world of its own. On the island stands Videyjarstofa, one of Iceland's oldest stone houses, alongside Videy church and traces of centuries of settlement. Today Videy is a popular destination for the outdoors, history and art, where visitors stroll grassy paths, take in views of Reykjavik and Mount Esja, and enjoy a stillness that is rare so close to the capital.

Top Things to See and Do on Videy

On Videy, history, nature and contemporary art blend in a unique way. Yoko Ono's Imagine Peace Tower lights up the winter sky with a beam of light in memory of John Lennon, drawing visitors from far and wide. Richard Serra's outdoor artwork Milestones (Afangar) forms a series of stone pillars around the island. Walking paths criss-cross the whole island, birdlife is abundant, and Videyjarstofa houses a cafe and history. The island is ideal for a relaxed day trip, photography and quiet time away from the bustle of the city.

Getting to Videy – Location and the Ferry

Videy lies in Kollafjordur bay just off the Reykjavik shore and is reached only by boat. The ferry usually sails from Skarfabakki pier at Sundahofn harbour, and in summer also from the old harbour downtown, on a short and easy crossing. From central Reykjavik the ferry terminal is easy to reach by bus, car or taxi. Check the ferry timetable in advance, as it shapes your visit, and allow plenty of time to walk the island before sailing back to the mainland.

Best Time to Visit Videy and Practical Tips

The main season on Videy runs from May through September, when ferries are most frequent, vegetation is in bloom and daylight is long. Summer is perfect for walking, birdwatching and picnics. The Imagine Peace Tower, however, is lit through the winter months and gives a special reason to visit on dark evenings. Dress for the weather and bring wind protection, as the island can be exposed. Pack a snack if you want to linger, and plan your trip around the ferry schedule.
