Fisherman

Fishmonger and bistro in the fishing village of Suðureyri.

Fisherman is a family-run fishmonger, bistro and café in Suðureyri, a small fishing village on Súgandafjörður. The fish arrives fresh straight from the harbour boats, and the kitchen uses as much local produce from within the village as possible. The company also offers guided seafood walks through the village.

Fresh fish in Súgandafjörður

Fisherman is a family business in Suðureyri, a small fishing village on Súgandafjörður often called the original fishing village. It runs a fishmonger, bistro and café, and the fish arrives fresh straight from the harbour boats. The kitchen focuses on ingredients from within the village and nearby, including herbs from its own garden. For travellers keen to buy local produce and taste Westfjords seafood straight from the source, Suðureyri is a unique destination.

Seafood walks and slow food

Fisherman is not only a fishmonger and eatery but also a travel service built on the slow food philosophy. Guests can walk through the village with a guide, taste seafood and watch people at work in the fishing industry. In this way the food connects directly to the community and its history. This approach makes a visit more than a meal and highlights how local produce and local culture come together. It is best to book walks ahead and check opening hours.

Visiting Suðureyri

Suðureyri is a small but lively fishing village in the northern Westfjords, connected to the outside world through the Westfjords tunnel. Fisherman handles much of the village's food and hospitality, with a fishmonger, bistro, café and accommodation. For those collecting local produce and wanting to experience genuine Westfjords village life, it is an ideal stop. Nearby there is nature, hiking and quiet, and Ísafjörður is close. The freshness of the ingredients is what guests remember most.
