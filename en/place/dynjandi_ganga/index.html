Dynjandi

A short walk up past a cascade of falls to the majestic Dynjandi, the Westfjords' tallest waterfall.

Dynjandi, also called Fjallfoss, is the tallest and most spectacular waterfall in the Westfjords, roughly 100 metres high and fanning out like a bridal veil. From the parking area on Route 60 a 15-minute path climbs to the base of the falls, passing six smaller cascades along the way. The trail is rocky but manageable for most.
