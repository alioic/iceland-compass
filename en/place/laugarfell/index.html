Laugarfell

Two stone-built natural hot pools on the edge of the highlands beneath Mt. Snæfell.

Laugarfell sits on the rim of Iceland's eastern highlands near Vatnajökull National Park, about an hour from the Ring Road. Two natural hot springs built from stone in the traditional way give the place its name, the larger one around 37–39°C, with sweeping views toward Mt. Snæfell.
