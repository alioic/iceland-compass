Bruggsmiðjan Kaldi

Iceland's first craft brewery, with a shop in Árskógssandur.

Bruggsmiðjan Kaldi was Iceland's first craft brewery, founded in 2006 in Árskógssandur by Eyjafjörður. The beer is unfiltered and brewed with pure Icelandic water, and is sold straight from the brewery. On site there is also the Bjórböðin beer spa, where guests can bathe in beer.

Iceland's first craft brewery

Bruggsmiðjan Kaldi in Árskógssandur by Eyjafjörður was the country's first craft brewery when it was founded in 2006. The brewery specialises in unfiltered beer brewed with pure Icelandic water and has earned a firm place in Icelandic beer culture. Guests can buy beer directly from the brewery and learn the story behind Kaldi. It is a rewarding stop for beer lovers in Northeast Iceland.

Beer straight from the brewery

At Bruggsmiðjan Kaldi you can buy the beer directly from the producer, often at better value than in ordinary shops. The brewery also offers tours where you can learn about the brewing process and the company's history. Kaldi has been a pioneer in Icelandic craft beer and enjoys popularity across the country. A visit to Árskógssandur is a chance to support local entrepreneurship.

Beer spa and experience by Eyjafjörður

Connected to the brewery is the Bjórböðin beer spa, where guests can bathe in young beer and relax with a view over Eyjafjörður. This has become one of the site's signature attractions and draws visitors year round. Árskógssandur is a small fishing village near Dalvík and Akureyri, well placed as a stop on a tour of Northeast Iceland. Kaldi thus combines craft, history and experience in one place.
