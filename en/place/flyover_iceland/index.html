FlyOver Iceland

A flight-simulation ride in the Grandi district that lets you soar over Iceland's landscapes.

FlyOver Iceland in Reykjavík's Grandi district is an experience where guests sit in moving seats before a giant spherical screen and soar over glaciers, fjords and famous Icelandic landmarks. Wind, mist and scents accompany the film to create the sensation of flight. It suits all ages who meet the height requirement.

Soar over Iceland without leaving the ground

FlyOver Iceland in Reykjavík's Grandi district is a novel experience in which guests glide over the country's landscapes in moving seats before a giant spherical screen. The film carries you over glaciers, fjords, waterfalls and famous landmarks while wind, mist and scents deepen the sense of flight. The ride is short but powerful and offers a memorable overview of Iceland's varied nature in just a few minutes, whatever the weather outside.

Indoor fun for the whole family

FlyOver Iceland suits families well, as it is an experience everyone can enjoy together, provided the youngest guests meet the height requirement for safety. A pre-show sets the scene before the main ride begins. Because everything takes place indoors, it is a safe and entertaining choice on rainy days and in winter, and it fits neatly into a visit to the Grandi district, where museums, cafés and restaurants are also within walking distance.

Location and practical information

FlyOver Iceland is located in Reykjavík's Grandi district by the harbour, within walking distance of the city centre and also reachable by car or bus. The ride itself is brief, but it is worth arriving in good time for the pre-show and preparation. Nearby are many other attractions, including a whale exhibition and a northern lights centre, so it is easy to build a full day of family activities in this lively harbour area.
