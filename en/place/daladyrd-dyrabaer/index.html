Daladyrd Petting Zoo

A family-run petting zoo in Fnjoskadalur valley with diverse animals.

Daladyrd is a friendly petting zoo at Brunagerdi farm in the Fnjoskadalur valley, where visitors get close to Icelandic farm animals. You will find horses, sheep, goats, rabbits, cats, dogs, pigs, cows and chickens of various breeds. There is also a small cafe emphasising products from the farm.

A petting zoo in Fnjoskadalur

Daladyrd at Brunagerdi farm in the Fnjoskadalur valley is a delightful petting zoo that pleases children and adults alike. You can get close to horses, sheep, goats, rabbits, pigs and other friendly animals in a beautiful rural setting. The family that runs the zoo focuses on a good experience and a warm welcome. It is a welcome stop for families travelling through Northeast Iceland, a short distance from Akureyri.

Close to Icelandic farm animals

At Daladyrd, children have the chance to pet and feed animals in safe conditions, an unforgettable experience for many. The animals are used to visitors and the variety is great, from chickens and rabbits to horses and cows. The visit offers a vivid glimpse of Icelandic farm life. The peaceful, beautiful surroundings of Fnjoskadalur make the moment even better.

Cafe and country charm

Daladyrd has a small cafe emphasising products from the farm, a good place to sit after saying hello to the animals. This makes the visit a complete experience combining animals, food and country atmosphere. The zoo is open through the summer and is especially well suited to the youngest travellers. It is a good idea to check opening hours before setting out.
