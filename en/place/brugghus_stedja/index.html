Brugghús Steðja

A farm brewery in Borgarfjörður that loves to experiment.

Brugghús Steðja is a farm brewery in Borgarfjörður, founded in 2012 and known for experimenting with unusual flavours such as seaweed and chocolate. Group visits and tastings are offered by arrangement in a relaxed, characterful tasting room.

A farm brewery in Borgarfjörður

Brugghús Steðja was founded in 2012 and is a farm brewery set in the beautiful surroundings of Borgarfjörður in West Iceland. The brewery has made a name for itself by daring to experiment, using unusual ingredients such as Icelandic seaweed and chocolate in its beers. Production spans seasonal brews such as Christmas, summer and Easter beers. For travellers in West Iceland, it is an enjoyable place to get to know Icelandic craft brewing right out in the countryside, straight from the source.

Visits and tastings

Brugghús Steðja welcomes groups for visits and tastings by arrangement, where guests settle into a spacious tasting room with a broad selection of beer. The owner sometimes offers smoked beer straight from the barrel as a special bonus for visitors. The atmosphere is homely and personal, far from large factories. It is a good way to support a small rural business and taste beers that are hard to find anywhere except direct from the maker in the heart of the countryside.

On a West Iceland tour

Steðja lies a short distance from Borgarnes in Borgarfjörður and fits well into a tour of West Iceland, whether heading to Snæfellsnes, Húsafell or the Westfjords. It is best to get in touch in advance to make sure visitors can be received. For beer lovers who want to experience the Icelandic countryside and craft beer at the same time, Brugghús Steðja is one of the more memorable stops in the region and a fine detour off the ring road.
