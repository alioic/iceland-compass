Húnabúð Kaffihús

A cozy café in Blönduós with homemade treats and a gift shop.

Húnabúð is a welcoming café in Blönduós that also houses a gift and flower shop. It emphasizes fresh ingredients, and visitors are often treated to a cup of coffee and homemade cakes.

About

Húnabúð is a cosy café in Blönduós that also houses a gift and flower shop. The emphasis is on fresh ingredients, and guests often enjoy a cup of coffee with home-baked cakes. It's a warm, personal place that's nice to pause and linger in, with an appealing blend of café and shop.

Good to know

The café is in Blönduós, a town at the mouth of the Blanda river in Northwest Iceland, right on the Ring Road roughly midway between Reykjavík and Akureyri. It's a handy stop on the long drive north. You can browse gifts while you're there.
