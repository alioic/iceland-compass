Gísli, Eiríkur, Helgi - Kaffihús Bakkabræðra

A cozy café in Dalvík named after the Bakkabræður brothers of Icelandic folklore.

This café in Dalvík is named after the three Bakkabræður brothers of old Icelandic folk tales. It is known for its warm atmosphere and a popular fish soup served with homemade bread, along with coffee, cakes and local beer.

About

The café Gísli, Eiríkur, Helgi in Dalvík is named after the three Bakkabræður brothers of old Icelandic folk tales. It is known for its warm atmosphere and fish soup served with homemade bread, along with coffee, cakes and local beer. A cosy, characterful spot with a charming folklore connection.

Good to know

Located in Dalvík on Eyjafjörður, within walking distance of the harbour where whale-watching trips and the Hrísey ferry depart. An ideal stop for soup and a bite while touring the Tröllaskagi peninsula. Check opening hours outside summer.
