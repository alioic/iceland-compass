Duus Museum

The art and cultural centre of Reykjanesbær by the harbour in Keflavík.

Duus Museum is the art and cultural centre of Reykjanesbær, set in a former merchant's warehouse from 1877 by the harbour in Keflavík. Under one roof it holds the local heritage museum, the art gallery and the Reykjanes Geopark visitor centre. A large collection of model ships is a highlight for children. Rotating exhibitions change throughout the year.

A cultural centre by the harbour in Keflavík

Duus Museum is at the heart of cultural life in Reykjanesbær, housed in a handsome warehouse built in 1877 by the harbour in Keflavík. The building has protected status and largely retains its original appearance. Inside, a heritage museum, an art gallery and the Reykjanes Geopark visitor centre are gathered under one roof, giving visitors the chance to explore the area's history, art and geology in a single beautiful, historic setting by the sea.

Model ships and varied exhibitions

One of the most eye-catching features of Duus Museum is a large and impressive collection of wooden model ships that appeals to children and adults alike. There are also displays tracing the area's history, art exhibitions that rotate throughout the year and material connected to the Reykjanes Geopark. This variety makes the visit interesting for families, as different age groups can each find something to enjoy on a single trip through the museum.

Opening hours and visiting

Duus Museum is open every day except Mondays, generally from midday into the afternoon. Access is easy, and the museum stands by the harbour in Keflavík, a short drive from Keflavík Airport and other destinations on the Reykjanes peninsula. The harbourside setting invites pleasant walks, and the visit fits nicely into a tour of Reykjanesbær for anyone interested in the history, art and culture of the area.
