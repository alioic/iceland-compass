The Icelandic Sea Monster Museum

A museum devoted to the sea monsters of Arnarfjörður.

The Sea Monster Museum in Bíldudalur tells the tales of the many sea monsters said to haunt Arnarfjörður and the wider Westfjords. The exhibition mixes folklore, eyewitness accounts and multimedia in a way that keeps both children and adults engaged. There is a cosy coffee shop on site.

The Sea Monster Museum in Bíldudalur

The Icelandic Sea Monster Museum in Bíldudalur is a fun family attraction dedicated to the sea monsters of Arnarfjörður. The exhibition tells stories of strange creatures said to lurk in the fjord, using multimedia, sound and visuals to bring the legends to life. Because it is indoors, it makes an ideal stop on a rainy Westfjords day when the weather turns. Both children and adults enjoy the tales and the atmosphere, and after the exhibition you can enjoy coffee and cake in the on-site coffee shop before continuing your journey.

Opening hours and visiting

The Sea Monster Museum is open daily through the summer season and welcomes visitors of all ages. A visit usually takes under an hour, making it an easy stop while exploring Arnarfjörður and the surrounding area. There is a coffee shop with refreshments and a small museum shop on site. Outside the main season it is wise to contact the museum in advance to confirm opening. Bíldudalur is a small, picturesque fishing village where it is pleasant to pause, stroll around the harbour and enjoy the calm of the fjord.

A stop for families

The Sea Monster Museum is a perfect destination for families travelling through the Westfjords. The sea-monster stories spark children's imaginations, and the multimedia presentation makes the material accessible to all ages. Being indoors, it is especially welcome when it rains or the wind picks up. Bíldudalur is well connected along the southern Westfjords, so you can easily combine the visit with other nearby sights such as Samúel Jónsson's Art Farm in Selárdalur. The museum offers a cosy and educational break for the whole family.
