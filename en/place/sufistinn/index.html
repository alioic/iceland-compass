Súfistinn Kaffihús

Long-established café in the heart of Hafnarfjörður.

Súfistinn is a popular café on Strandgata in central Hafnarfjörður. It serves coffee, sandwiches, pies and cakes in a relaxed setting near Hellisgerði park.

About

Súfistinn is a popular, long-standing café on Strandgata in the centre of Hafnarfjörður. It serves coffee, sandwiches, pies and cakes in a cosy, relaxed setting. The place is a warm stop for both locals and visitors, ideal for sitting down with a good coffee and a light bite.

Good to know

The café is in the heart of Hafnarfjörður, a short walk from the Hellisgerði park and the harbour. A nice coffee or lunch break if you are exploring the town. Calm and homely, it suits both a quick pause and a longer stay over a book or a chat.
