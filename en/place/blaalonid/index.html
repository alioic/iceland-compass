The Blue Lagoon – World-Famous Geothermal Spa on the Reykjanes Peninsula

The Blue Lagoon is one of Iceland's most popular attractions and one of the world's most famous geothermal spas, set amid rugged lava on the Reykjanes Peninsula. Its milky-blue, mineral- and silica-rich water is a warm 38–40°C and forms a magical contrast with the black lava all around. The water is considered good for the skin and draws visitors from across the globe. The Blue Lagoon combines relaxation, wellness and a unique natural setting, and has become one of the leading icons of Icelandic tourism.

Things to Do at the Blue Lagoon

Guests at the Blue Lagoon soak in the warm, silica-rich geothermal seawater, apply the famous silica mask and enjoy steam baths and in-water massage in a lava setting. Beside the lagoon are the Lava restaurant and other dining options, while the Retreat spa offers deeper wellness and a private lagoon for those seeking a luxury experience. Skincare treatments and spa services turn the visit into holistic relaxation. The in-water bar, steam caves and views over the lava make bathing here an unforgettable experience for everyone.

Getting to the Blue Lagoon – Location and Access

The Blue Lagoon sits on the Reykjanes Peninsula, conveniently located between Keflavik International Airport and Reykjavik. It is only about a 20-minute drive from the airport and roughly 45–50 minutes from the capital, making it a perfect stop on arrival or departure. Bus transfers run regularly from Reykjavik and the airport. It is essential to book admission in advance, as demand is high and entry is timed. Many visitors use the lagoon as the first or last part of their Iceland trip, beginning or ending the journey in complete relaxation.

Best Time to Visit the Blue Lagoon and Practical Tips

The Blue Lagoon is open all year, and every season has its charm – the steam rising over the blue water is as enchanting in summer light as under a winter sky, even with northern lights overhead. To avoid the biggest crowds, aim for early morning or late afternoon. Always book ahead. Remember that the mineral-rich water can dry out your hair, so apply plenty of conditioner and tie it up. Take it easy, drink water, and give yourself time to enjoy both the bathing and the surroundings to the full.
