Stokkseyri Swimming Pool

Cosy village pool in the heart of Stokkseyri with a slide and hot tubs.

Stokkseyri's pool is a welcoming 18-metre outdoor pool with a slide, a wading pool and two hot tubs. A quiet, family-friendly bathing spot in the seaside village.
