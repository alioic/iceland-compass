Lýsuhólslaug

A mineral-rich natural pool with distinctive green water on the south side of Snæfellsnes.

Lýsuhólslaug is a geothermal mineral pool fed by naturally carbonated spring water with no added chemicals. Its striking green tint comes from chlorella algae thriving in the iron- and mineral-rich water, which holds around 37-39°C. The pool is open over the summer months.
