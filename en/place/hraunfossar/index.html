Hraunfossar and Barnafoss – Unique Waterfalls in Borgarfjörður

Hraunfossar is among Iceland's most unusual waterfalls, where countless clear springs emerge from beneath the Hallmundarhraun lava field and stream out along roughly a kilometre of riverbank into the Hvítá river. The water seeps through the lava and appears as innumerable blue, crystalline cascades – a sight with few equals. Close by is Barnafoss, a narrow, powerful gorge with a dramatic folk legend. Set in Borgarfjörður in West Iceland, near Húsafell and Reykholt, these falls are a popular and easily accessible destination throughout the year.

What to See and Do at Hraunfossar and Barnafoss

At Hraunfossar, viewing platforms and paths run along the bank with clear views over the springs flowing from beneath the lava. A short walk leads up to Barnafoss, where the river narrows into a powerful gorge and a bridge links the banks. The colours are especially beautiful – blue water, green vegetation and reddish autumn foliage. Nearby lies Deildartunguhver, Europe's most powerful hot spring by flow, and the Krauma geothermal baths. The area suits a short scenic walk, photography and families travelling through West Iceland.

How to Get to Hraunfossar in Borgarfjörður

Hraunfossar and Barnafoss are located in Borgarfjörður in West Iceland, about an hour and fifteen minutes' drive from Reykjavík. Head through Borgarnes and continue up the valley on Routes 50 and 518 toward Húsafell and Reykholt, where a well-signed car park sits beside the falls. The road is paved and accessible year-round. The site connects easily with other Borgarfjörður highlights such as Deildartunguhver, Reykholt and Húsafell, making it a fine day trip from the capital or a stop on a wider West Iceland tour.

Best Time to Visit Hraunfossar and Practical Tips

Hraunfossar is open and accessible all year, free of charge. Summer and autumn are especially lovely, when vegetation and fall colours frame the blue springs, while winter wraps the falls in a magical setting of snow and ice. Take care at Barnafoss – stay on the paths and behind the railings, as the current is powerful. Wear sturdy shoes and warm clothing. Combine your visit with Deildartunguhver and the Krauma baths in Borgarfjörður, and allow time to enjoy both Hraunfossar and Barnafoss at a relaxed pace.
